# Campgrounds Near Yellowstone Visitor Centers

## 1. Albright Visitor Center (Mammoth Hot Springs)

### Mammoth Campground
- **Distance from Visitor Center**: Adjacent (within walking distance)
- **Amenities**: 
  - Flush toilets
  - Drinking water
  - Fire pits/grills
  - Picnic tables
  - No showers
  - No hookups
  - No laundry facilities
- **Reservation Information**: First-come, first-served year-round. No reservations accepted.
- **Notes**: Open year-round, making it unique among Yellowstone campgrounds. Located in a less thermal area with more forest cover.

## 2. Canyon Visitor Education Center

### Canyon Campground
- **Distance from Visitor Center**: Approximately 0.5 miles (very close)
- **Amenities**:
  - Flush toilets
  - Drinking water
  - Fire pits/grills
  - Picnic tables
  - No showers
  - No hookups
  - No laundry facilities
- **Reservation Information**: Reservations required and can be made up to 6 months in advance through recreation.gov. Typically open late May to mid-September.
- **Notes**: One of the most popular campgrounds due to its central location and proximity to the Grand Canyon of the Yellowstone.

## 3. Fishing Bridge Visitor Center

### Bridge Bay Campground
- **Distance from Visitor Center**: Approximately 2 miles
- **Amenities**:
  - Flush toilets
  - Drinking water
  - Fire pits/grills
  - Picnic tables
  - Boat ramp access
  - No showers
  - No hookups
  - No laundry facilities
- **Reservation Information**: Reservations required and can be made up to 6 months in advance through recreation.gov. Typically open late May to late September.
- **Notes**: Located near Yellowstone Lake with good access for boating and fishing.

### Grant Village Campground
- **Distance from Visitor Center**: Approximately 18 miles south
- **Amenities**:
  - Flush toilets
  - Drinking water
  - Fire pits/grills
  - Picnic tables
  - Showers available at nearby Grant Village
  - Laundry facilities at nearby Grant Village
  - No hookups
- **Reservation Information**: Reservations required and can be made up to 6 months in advance through recreation.gov. Typically open mid-June to mid-September.
- **Notes**: Close to West Thumb Geyser Basin and provides access to the southern part of Yellowstone Lake.

## 4. Madison Information Station

### Madison Campground
- **Distance from Visitor Center**: Approximately 1 mile
- **Amenities**:
  - Flush toilets
  - Drinking water
  - Fire pits/grills
  - Picnic tables
  - No showers
  - No hookups
  - No laundry facilities
- **Reservation Information**: Reservations required for most sites and can be made up to 6 months in advance through recreation.gov. Some first-come, first-served sites may be available. Typically open late May to mid-October.
- **Notes**: Located at the confluence of the Gibbon and Firehole Rivers, excellent for wildlife viewing and fishing.

## 5. Norris Geyser Basin Museum

### Norris Campground
- **Distance from Visitor Center**: Approximately 0.5 miles (adjacent to Norris Geyser Basin)
- **Amenities**:
  - Vault toilets
  - Drinking water
  - Fire pits/grills
  - Picnic tables
  - No showers
  - No hookups
  - No laundry facilities
- **Reservation Information**: First-come, first-served only. No reservations accepted. Typically open late May to late September.
- **Notes**: Smaller campground with a more rustic feel, located in a heavily forested area.

## 6. Old Faithful Visitor Education Center

### Old Faithful Campground
- **Distance from Visitor Center**: Approximately 0.5 miles
- **Amenities**:
  - Flush toilets
  - Drinking water
  - Fire pits/grills
  - Picnic tables
  - Showers available at nearby Old Faithful Inn/Lodge
  - Laundry facilities at nearby Old Faithful Lodge
  - No hookups
- **Reservation Information**: Reservations required and can be made up to 6 months in advance through yellowstonenationalparklodges.com. Typically open mid-May to early October.
- **Notes**: The largest campground in Yellowstone with over 300 sites. Very convenient for viewing Old Faithful and other Upper Geyser Basin features.

### Grant Village Campground (Alternative option)
- **Distance from Visitor Center**: Approximately 20 miles south
- **Amenities**: As listed above under Fishing Bridge section
- **Reservation Information**: As listed above under Fishing Bridge section
- **Notes**: While not as close as Old Faithful Campground, it's an alternative if Old Faithful is full.

## General Reservation Information:
- Most Yellowstone campgrounds require reservations during peak season (late May through September)
- Reservations can be made through recreation.gov or yellowstonenationalparklodges.com depending on the campground
- First-come, first-served campgrounds fill very early in the day (often by 7-8 AM)
- All campgrounds have strict food storage regulations due to bear activity
- Maximum stay limits apply (typically 14 days total in the park, with possible restrictions on consecutive days at one campground)

## Important Notes:
- Campground openings and closings are weather-dependent
- Bear-proof food storage containers are required at all campgrounds
- Generator hours are typically limited to specific times of day
- All sites accommodate tents and RVs (though some have length restrictions)
- Water may be turned off in early/late season at some campgrounds
