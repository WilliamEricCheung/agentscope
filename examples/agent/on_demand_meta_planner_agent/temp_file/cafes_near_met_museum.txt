HIGHLY-RATED CAFES NEAR THE METROPOLITAN MUSEUM OF ART
=====================================================

NOTE: Due to technical limitations with accessing live review platforms (Google Maps, Yelp, TripAdvisor), 
this information is based on established, well-known cafes in the area that historically maintain 
high ratings (4.0+). For current ratings, exact walking distances, and up-to-date information, 
please verify directly on review platforms.

The Metropolitan Museum of Art is located at 1000 5th Ave, New York, NY 10028, at the corner of 
82nd Street and Fifth Avenue on the Upper East Side.

KNOWN HIGHLY-RATED CAFES WITHIN ~0.5 MILES:

1. **Cafe Sabarsky** (at Neue Galerie)
   - Location: 1048 5th Ave (at 86th Street) - approximately 0.3 miles from The Met
   - Typical Rating: 4.5+ stars
   - Description: Elegant Viennese-style cafe with authentic Austrian pastries, coffee, and light meals. Upscale atmosphere with beautiful interior design.

2. **Society Cafe Espresso** (at The Mark Hotel)
   - Location: 25 E 77th St (between Madison & 5th Ave) - approximately 0.4 miles from The Met
   - Typical Rating: 4.0+ stars  
   - Description: Sophisticated cafe offering breakfast, lunch, and afternoon tea. Known for excellent coffee and pastries in an elegant setting.

3. **Birch Coffee** (Upper East Side location)
   - Location: 1259 Lexington Ave (between 84th & 85th St) - approximately 0.4 miles from The Met
   - Typical Rating: 4.0+ stars
   - Description: Popular local coffee chain known for quality coffee, cozy atmosphere, and community focus. Offers pastries and light snacks.

4. **Joe Coffee Company**
   - Location: 1250 Lexington Ave (at 84th Street) - approximately 0.4 miles from The Met  
   - Typical Rating: 4.0+ stars
   - Description: Well-regarded specialty coffee shop with expertly crafted espresso drinks and quality pastries.

5. **Patisserie Chanson**
   - Location: 1274 Lexington Ave (between 85th & 86th St) - approximately 0.4 miles from The Met
   - Typical Rating: 4.0+ stars
   - Description: Japanese-French patisserie offering beautiful pastries, cakes, and coffee in an elegant setting.

6. **E.A.T. (East Side Artisanal Table)**
   - Location: 1226 Lexington Ave (between 83rd & 84th St) - approximately 0.3 miles from The Met
   - Typical Rating: 4.0+ stars
   - Description: Gourmet market and cafe offering prepared foods, sandwiches, salads, and excellent coffee.

WALKING DISTANCES:
- All locations listed are within approximately 0.3-0.4 miles of The Met
- Walking time: roughly 6-10 minutes from The Met

RECOMMENDATION:
For the most current ratings, reviews, hours, and exact walking directions, please check:
- Google Maps
- Yelp  
- TripAdvisor
- Individual cafe websites

The Upper East Side around The Met has many excellent cafe options, and new establishments frequently open while others may change their offerings or ratings.
