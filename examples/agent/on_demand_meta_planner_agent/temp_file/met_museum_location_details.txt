THE METROPOLITAN MUSEUM OF ART - LOCATION DETAILS

EXACT ADDRESS:
1000 Fifth Avenue
New York, NY 10028
United States

LOCATION DESCRIPTION:
- Situated on the eastern edge of Central Park
- Located along Manhattan's famous "Museum Mile" on Fifth Avenue
- Between 80th and 84th Streets
- Faces Central Park to the west and Fifth Avenue to the east

NEAREST SUBWAY STATIONS:
- 86th Street Station (4, 5, 6 trains) - Lexington Avenue Line
  * Approximately 2-3 blocks south of the museum
  * Walk west on 86th Street to Fifth Avenue, then north to the museum
  
- 81st Street–Museum of Natural History Station (B, C trains) - Eighth Avenue Line
  * Located on the west side of Central Park
  * Requires walking through Central Park or around via Columbus Avenue/Central Park West
  
- 77th Street Station (6 train) - Lexington Avenue Line
  * Approximately 3 blocks south of the museum
  * Walk west on 77th Street to Fifth Avenue, then north to the museum

NOTABLE LANDMARKS NEARBY:
- Central Park (immediately west of the museum)
- Guggenheim Museum (approximately 6 blocks north at 1071 Fifth Avenue)
- Cooper Hewitt, Smithsonian Design Museum (across the street at 2 East 91st Street)
- The Frick Collection (several blocks south)
- Museum of the City of New York (across Central Park at 1220 Fifth Avenue)
- Mount Sinai Hospital (nearby on Madison Avenue)
- Various upscale residential buildings along Fifth Avenue and Central Park

ADDITIONAL INFORMATION:
- The main entrance is on Fifth Avenue
- The museum also has a rear entrance facing Central Park
- Parking is limited in the area; public transportation is recommended
- The museum is easily accessible by bus routes along Fifth Avenue and Madison Avenue
