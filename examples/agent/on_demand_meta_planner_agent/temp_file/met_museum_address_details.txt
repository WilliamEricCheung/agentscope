The Metropolitan Museum of Art - Location Details

FULL STREET ADDRESS:
1000 Fifth Avenue
New York, NY 10028

NEIGHBORHOOD:
Upper East Side, Manhattan

CROSS STREETS:
Corner of Fifth Avenue and 82nd Street

LOCATION INFORMATION FOR VISITORS:
- Located on Museum Mile along the eastern edge of Central Park
- Easily accessible by public transportation (subway lines 4, 5, 6 to 86th Street station)
- Multiple entrances: Main entrance on Fifth Avenue, and additional entrances at 81st Street (for the Met Cloisters) and 80th Street
- The museum occupies the entire block between 79th and 83rd Streets along Fifth Avenue
- Part of Manhattan's prestigious Museum Mile cultural district
- Adjacent to Central Park, making it easily walkable from many Upper East Side hotels and attractions

VISITOR NOTES:
- The main entrance faces Fifth Avenue with the iconic steps and columns
- The museum is one of the largest art museums in the world
- Located in one of New York City's most affluent and culturally rich neighborhoods
- Easy access from major transportation hubs and tourist areas
