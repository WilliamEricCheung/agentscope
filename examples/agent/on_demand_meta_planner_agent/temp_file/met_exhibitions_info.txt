Current Special Exhibitions at The Metropolitan Museum of Art

Due to technical limitations with web browsing tools, I was unable to directly access The Metropolitan Museum of Art's website to retrieve current exhibition information. However, here are the recommended methods to find current special exhibitions:

1. **Official Website**: Visit https://www.metmuseum.org/exhibitions
   - This page typically displays current, upcoming, and past exhibitions
   - Special exhibitions are usually prominently featured with dates, descriptions, and ticket information

2. **Direct Navigation**:
   - Go to metmuseum.org
   - Look for "Exhibitions" in the main navigation menu
   - Filter by "Current" or "On View" exhibitions

3. **Alternative Sources**:
   - Check the Met's social media accounts (Instagram, Twitter, Facebook)
   - Visit museum aggregator sites like Time Out New York or NYC tourism websites
   - Call the Met directly at (212) 535-7710 for current exhibition information

4. **What to Expect**:
   - The Met typically has multiple special exhibitions running simultaneously
   - Exhibitions often feature works from specific artists, time periods, cultures, or themes
   - Many exhibitions require timed tickets in addition to general admission
   - Exhibition dates are usually listed clearly with start and end dates

For the most accurate and up-to-date information about current special exhibitions, please visit the official Met website directly.
