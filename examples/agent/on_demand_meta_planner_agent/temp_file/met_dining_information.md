# Metropolitan Museum of Art Dining Options

**Note: This information is based on general knowledge and may not reflect current operations, hours, or policies. Please verify all details directly on the official Met Museum website (metmuseum.org) before your visit.**

## Typical Dining Options at The Met

### 1. The Dining Room
- **Type**: Upscale fine dining restaurant
- **Location**: Main building
- **Typical Offerings**: Full-service dining with seasonal menus
- **Hours**: Usually open for lunch and sometimes dinner (varies by season)

### 2. Petrie Court Café and Wine Bar
- **Type**: Casual café with wine bar
- **Location**: Near the European Sculpture and Decorative Arts galleries
- **Typical Offerings**: Light fare, sandwiches, pastries, coffee, and wine selection
- **Hours**: Generally open during museum hours

### 3. American Wing Café
- **Type**: Casual café
- **Location**: Within the American Wing
- **Typical Offerings**: Sandwiches, salads, soups, and beverages
- **Hours**: Open during museum hours

### 4. Garden Cafeteria
- **Type**: Self-service cafeteria
- **Location**: Lower level or garden area
- **Typical Offerings**: Variety of hot and cold meals, including vegetarian options
- **Hours**: Extended hours during peak times

### 5. Coffee Carts and Kiosks
- **Type**: Quick service beverage and snack stations
- **Location**: Various locations throughout the museum
- **Typical Offerings**: Coffee, tea, pastries, and light snacks
- **Hours**: Throughout museum operating hours

## General Policies (Subject to Change)
- Outside food and drink are typically not permitted in gallery spaces
- Food and drink are usually allowed only in designated dining areas
- Some dining venues may require reservations, especially for The Dining Room
- Hours and availability may vary by season, day of the week, and special events
- Pricing ranges from cafeteria-style affordable options to fine dining prices
