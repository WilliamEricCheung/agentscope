# Top Cafes Near The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Based on proximity (within 500 meters) and customer ratings, here are the best cafe options near the Metropolitan Museum of Art.

## 1. 787 Coffee ⭐ 4.9/5 (3,472 reviews)
**Address:** 228 E 80th St, New York, NY 10075  
**Phone:** (646) 850-0234  
**Website:** http://www.787coffee.com/  
**Currently Open:** No

**Highlights:**
- Exceptionally high rating (4.9/5) with thousands of positive reviews
- Known for unique rum-infused coffee beans and specialty drinks
- Cozy, neighborhood feel with friendly staff who remember regulars
- Features a swing by the window for a unique seating experience
- Fast WiFi and pet-friendly (dogs welcome)
- Freshly baked pastries throughout the day

**Customer Feedback:** 
Reviewers consistently praise the exceptional service, unique coffee flavors (especially the rum-infused varieties), and welcoming atmosphere. Many mention the staff by name (particularly Alex, Fox, and Isa) for their outstanding hospitality.

## 2. Antonucci Cafe ⭐ 4.5/5 (428 reviews)
**Address:** 170 E 81st St, New York, NY 10028  
**Phone:** (212) 570-5100  
**Website:** https://antonuccicafe81.com/  
**Price Level:** $$$ (Higher-end)  
**Currently Open:** No

**Highlights:**
- Authentic Italian cafe and restaurant with charming ambiance
- Walls adorned with artwork creating a warm, intimate setting
- Excellent Burrata salad with marinated tomatoes
- Perfectly cooked fresh pasta (including gluten-free options)
- Homemade pastries (note: some dessert items only available in the evening)

**Customer Feedback:**
Patrons appreciate the authentic Italian cuisine and cozy atmosphere. Some note that the space is small and can be noisy, but the quality of food and service makes up for it. A few reviews mention inconsistent seasoning in some dishes.

## 3. Tarallucci e Vino at Cooper Hewitt ⭐ 4.5/5 (30 reviews)
**Address:** 9 E 90th St, New York, NY 10128 (Inside Cooper Hewitt Design Museum)  
**Phone:** (212) 849-8483  
**Website:** https://www.taralluccievino.net/cooper-hewitt-location  
**Price Level:** $$ (Moderate)  
**Currently Open:** No

**Highlights:**
- Located inside the beautiful Cooper Hewitt Design Museum
- Outdoor seating available in the museum courtyard (weather permitting)
- Excellent coffee and house-made pastries and cookies
- Notable staff member Fabian receives consistent praise for exceptional service
- Good selection of wines

**Customer Feedback:**
Visitors love the charming location and excellent service, particularly from staff member Fabian. Some note limited vegetarian options and that seating can be occupied by non-paying laptop users for extended periods.

## 4. Starbucks ⭐ 4.1/5 (927 reviews)
**Address:** 1142 Madison Ave, New York, NY 10028  
**Phone:** (212) 288-1506  
**Website:** https://www.starbucks.com/store-locator/store/1015661  
**Price Level:** $$ (Moderate)  
**Currently Open:** Yes

**Highlights:**
- Currently the only option open among the top-rated cafes
- Convenient location just steps from the Met
- Clean facilities with restrooms available
- Mobile ordering available through the Starbucks app
- Comfortable seating area

**Customer Feedback:**
Generally positive reviews for convenience and comfort, especially as a resting spot after visiting Central Park or the Met. One recent negative review mentioned extremely long wait times (40+ minutes) and poor management response.

## Summary Recommendation:
For the best overall experience, **787 Coffee** stands out with its exceptional rating, unique offerings, and outstanding service. If you're looking for something currently open, the nearby **Starbucks** provides a reliable option. For an authentic Italian experience, **Antonucci Cafe** offers excellent food in a charming setting, while **Tarallucci e Vino** provides a unique museum cafe experience.
