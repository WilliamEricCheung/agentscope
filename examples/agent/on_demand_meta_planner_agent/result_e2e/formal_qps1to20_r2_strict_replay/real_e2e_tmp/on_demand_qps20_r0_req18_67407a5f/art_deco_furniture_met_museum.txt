ABOUT EMILE-JACQUES RUHLMANN:
Emile-Jacques Ruhlmann was one of the most celebrated and influential designers of the Art Deco movement. His work is characterized by:
- Luxurious materials (exotic woods, ivory, precious metals)
- Geometric forms with elegant proportions
- Exceptional craftsmanship
- Sophisticated, modern aesthetic that defined French Art Deco

NOTES ON SEARCH METHODOLOGY:
Initial broad searches returned many objects that were not actually from the Art Deco period (1920s-1940s), including 18th and 19th century pieces. More targeted searches using specific date ranges and verified Art Deco designer names yielded authentic examples.

The Metropolitan Museum has a significant collection of Art Deco furniture, with pieces by renowned designers like Ruhlmann representing the height of the movement's luxury and sophistication.
