# Top-Rated Cafes Near The Metropolitan Museum of Art

This report details the highest-rated cafes within walking distance (1.5 km or less) from the Metropolitan Museum of Art in New York City, including their ratings, distances, and key details.

## 1. Cositas Café
- **Rating**: 4.8/5 (184 reviews)
- **Distance**: 1.4 km (19-minute walk)
- **Address**: 353 E 78th St, New York, NY 10075
- **Phone**: (347) 673-1733
- **Website**: https://www.cositascafenyc.com/
- **Price Level**: $
- **Currently Open**: No
- **Highlights**: 
  - Authentic Latin American café with Colombian influence
  - Known for excellent baked goods, flan, smoothies, and empanadas
  - Small, cozy atmosphere with friendly service
  - Highly praised for authentic Latin pastries like almojábanas and buñuelos

## 2. La Bomboniera
- **Rating**: 4.7/5 (560 reviews)
- **Distance**: 1.2 km (17-minute walk)
- **Address**: 1378 Lexington Ave, New York, NY 10128
- **Phone**: (646) 370-6302
- **Website**: http://labombonieranyc.com/
- **Currently Open**: No
- **Highlights**:
  - Eco-friendly café that avoids single-use plastics
  - Serves excellent hot chocolate with cream and Italian biscotti
  - Beautiful ambiance with bar service available
  - Known for lemon peel cake and chocolate cake
  - Some reviewers mention occasional service issues despite great food

## 3. Cabuc Coffee
- **Rating**: 4.6/5 (153 reviews)
- **Distance**: 0.5 km (8-minute walk)
- **Address**: 1211 Lexington Ave, New York, NY 10028
- **Phone**: (646) 891-0011
- **Currently Open**: Yes
- **Highlights**:
  - Bright, welcoming space perfect for working (offers Wi-Fi)
  - Known for Turkish coffee and unique baklava latte
  - Friendly staff and pleasant atmosphere with Turkish music
  - Serves savory honey croissant that's highly recommended
  - Ceramic cups provided for in-house consumption

## 4. Uptown Cafe and Pastries
- **Rating**: 4.5/5 (85 reviews)
- **Distance**: 1.5 km (21-minute walk)
- **Address**: 100 E 96th St, New York, NY 10128
- **Phone**: (646) 410-0132
- **Currently Open**: Yes
- **Highlights**:
  - Modern yet comfortable design with spacious seating
  - Seasonal cold foam drinks highly praised (chocolate strawberry, maple sea salt)
  - Uses ceramic mugs instead of paper cups
  - Hidden menu hot chocolate and honey pecan danish are customer favorites
  - Good spot for both quick coffee and longer stays

## 5. Madame Bonté Café
- **Rating**: 4.5/5 (380 reviews)
- **Distance**: 1.1 km (16-minute walk)
- **Address**: 318 E 84th St, New York, NY 10028
- **Phone**: (917) 409-5555
- **Website**: http://www.madamebonte.com/
- **Price Level**: $$
- **Currently Open**: No
- **Highlights**:
  - Unique policy: no electronic screens allowed in the downstairs area
  - Charming hidden gem with downstairs seating and outdoor patio
  - Excellent honey lavender latte and other specialty drinks
  - Serves Croque Monsieur and other French-inspired food items
  - Great for conversation and face-to-face interaction

## Summary
The closest highly-rated cafe to the Met is Cabuc Coffee at just 0.5 km away (8-minute walk), which is currently open and offers a great workspace environment. For those willing to walk a bit further, Cositas Café offers the highest rating (4.8/5) with authentic Latin American treats. La Bomboniera stands out for its eco-friendly practices and excellent hot chocolate, while Madame Bonté provides a unique screen-free social experience.
