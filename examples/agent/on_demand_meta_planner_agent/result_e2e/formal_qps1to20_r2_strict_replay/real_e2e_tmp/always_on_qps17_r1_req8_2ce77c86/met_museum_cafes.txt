# Top-Rated Cafes Near The Metropolitan Museum of Art

This report details the highest-rated cafes within walking distance of the Metropolitan Museum of Art in New York City, including their ratings, distances, and key features.

## 1. 787 Coffee ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ (4.9/5)
- **Address**: 228 E 80th St, New York, NY 10075
- **Distance from Met**: 0.9 km (13-minute walk)
- **Phone**: (646) 850-0234
- **Website**: http://www.787coffee.com/
- **Currently Open**: Yes
- **Key Features**:
  - Exceptionally high rating with over 3,400 reviews
  - Known for unique rum-infused coffee beans
  - Friendly staff who remember regular customers
  - Pet-friendly establishment
  - Fast WiFi available
  - Swing seating by the window
  - Freshly baked pastries daily

- **Distance from Met**: 0.5 km (7-minute walk)
- **Phone**: (646) 891-0011

- **Distance from Met**: 0.8 km (12-minute walk)
