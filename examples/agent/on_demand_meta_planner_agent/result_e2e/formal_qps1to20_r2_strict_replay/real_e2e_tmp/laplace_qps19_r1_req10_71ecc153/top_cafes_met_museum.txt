# Top 3 Cafes Near The Metropolitan Museum of Art, NYC

## 1. 787 Coffee
**Rating:** 4.9/5 (3,472 reviews)
**Address:** 228 E 80th St, New York, NY 10075
**Phone:** (646) 850-0234
**Website:** http://www.787coffee.com/

**Highlights:**
- Exceptionally high rating with thousands of positive reviews
- Known for unique rum-infused and whiskey-flavored coffee beans
- Cozy, neighborhood feel with friendly baristas who remember regular customers
- Features a swing by the window for a unique seating experience
- Fast WiFi and pet-friendly (customers report bringing their dogs)
- Freshly baked pastries throughout the day

**Customer Feedback:**
Customers consistently praise the exceptional coffee quality, particularly the rum-infused cappuccino and specialty drinks. The staff (especially Alex, Fox, and Isa) are noted for their warmth and knowledge. Many reviewers mention the welcoming atmosphere that makes them feel like part of a community.

## 2. Cositas Café
**Rating:** 4.8/5 (184 reviews)
**Address:** 353 E 78th St, New York, NY 10075
**Phone:** (347) 673-1733
**Website:** https://www.cositascafenyc.com/
**Price Level:** $

**Highlights:**
- Authentic Latin American café with Colombian influence
- Famous for fresh baked goods including empanadas, almojábanas, and buñuelos
- Excellent flan and smoothies
- Family-owned Latinx business
- Affordable pricing

**Customer Feedback:**
Reviewers with Latin American heritage particularly appreciate the authenticity of the food and drinks. The pastries receive consistent praise, with many calling them "incredible" and "perfect." The coffee is also highly rated, and customers enjoy the cozy, authentic atmosphere.

## 3. La Bomboniera
**Rating:** 4.7/5 (560 reviews)
**Address:** 1378 Lexington Ave, New York, NY 10128
**Phone:** (646) 370-6302
**Website:** http://labombonieranyc.com/

**Highlights:**
- Environmentally conscious café with zero single-use plastics
- Beautiful ambiance with both coffee shop and bar options
- Excellent hot chocolate with cream (cioccolata calda con panna)
- Known for delicious biscotti and cakes
- Popular date spot, especially in winter months

**Customer Feedback:**
Customers love the sustainable approach and cozy atmosphere. The hot chocolate and Italian desserts receive particular praise. While most reviews highlight excellent service, there are occasional mentions of inconsistent service quality during busy periods. The café is noted as being quite popular, especially during summer months.

---

All three cafés are within approximately 0.5-0.7 miles from The Metropolitan Museum of Art, making them convenient options for a coffee break before or after visiting the museum.
