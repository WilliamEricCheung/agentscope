# Top 3 Cafes Near The Metropolitan Museum of Art

## 1. 787 Coffee
**Rating:** 4.9/5 (3,477 reviews)
**Address:** 228 E 80th St, New York, NY 10075
**Phone:** (646) 850-0234
**Website:** http://www.787coffee.com/
**Distance from Met:** Approximately 0.5 miles (8-minute walk)

**Highlights:**
- Exceptionally high rating with consistently positive reviews
- Known for unique rum-infused coffee beans
- Cozy, neighborhood feel with friendly staff who remember regulars
- Features a swing by the window for a unique seating experience
- Fast WiFi and pet-friendly
- Freshly baked pastries available all day

**Customer Feedback:**
Customers praise the exceptional service, unique coffee flavors (especially the rum-infused options), and welcoming atmosphere. Many reviewers mention the staff by name, particularly Alex, Fox, and Isa, highlighting their knowledge and friendliness.

## 2. Cositas Café
**Rating:** 4.8/5 (184 reviews)
**Address:** 353 E 78th St, New York, NY 10075
**Phone:** (347) 673-1733
**Website:** https://www.cositascafenyc.com/
**Price Level:** $ (Budget-friendly)
**Distance from Met:** Approximately 0.7 miles (12-minute walk)

**Highlights:**
- Authentic Latin American café with Colombian influence
- Famous for fresh baked goods including flan, empanadas, and almojábanas
- Budget-friendly pricing
- Family-owned Latinx business
- Offers unique Latin American beverages like Colombian hot chocolate

**Customer Feedback:**
Reviewers with Latin American heritage particularly appreciate the authenticity of the food and drinks. The baked goods receive consistent praise, especially the flan and empanadas. Most customers find the coffee excellent and the service warm and welcoming.

## 3. La Bomboniera
**Rating:** 4.7/5 (560 reviews)
**Address:** 1378 Lexington Ave, New York, NY 10128
**Phone:** (646) 370-6302
**Website:** http://labombonieranyc.com/
**Distance from Met:** Approximately 0.6 miles (10-minute walk)

**Highlights:**
- Environmentally conscious café with zero single-use plastics
- Beautiful ambiance with both coffee shop and bar options
- Known for excellent hot chocolate with cream (cioccolata calda con panna)
- Offers Italian pastries and cakes
- Popular date spot with cozy atmosphere

**Customer Feedback:**
Customers love the sustainable approach, delicious hot chocolate, and Italian pastries. The ambiance is frequently described as beautiful and cozy. While most reviews praise the service, there are occasional mentions of inconsistent service quality during busy periods.

## Summary
All three cafés offer excellent coffee and unique experiences within walking distance of the Metropolitan Museum of Art. 787 Coffee stands out for its exceptionally high rating and unique rum-infused coffee options. Cositas Café offers authentic Latin American flavors at budget-friendly prices. La Bomboniera provides an environmentally conscious option with Italian influences and a beautiful ambiance.
