## Summary
For the best overall experience, 787 Coffee stands out with its exceptional 4.9 rating and unique offerings like rum-infused coffee beans. If you're looking for a French café experience, Café Blériot XI offers authentic pastries in a charming setting. Cabuc Coffee provides a unique Turkish-inspired coffee experience with excellent service. Note that Tarallucci e Vino is currently closed but worth visiting when open for its museum location and courtyard seating. Rosa's Cafe & Bakery offers a good mix of coffee, pastries, and even pizza options.
