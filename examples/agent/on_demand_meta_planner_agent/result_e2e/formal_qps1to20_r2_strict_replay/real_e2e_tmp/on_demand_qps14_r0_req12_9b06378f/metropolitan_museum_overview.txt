# The Metropolitan Museum of Art: Overview

## Location
The Metropolitan Museum of Art (often referred to as "The Met") is located on the eastern edge of Central Park along Manhattan's Museum Mile in New York City, at 1000 Fifth Avenue.

## History
- Founded in 1870 by a group of American citizens including businessmen, financiers, and leading artists
- Opened to the public on February 20, 1872, in the Dodworth Building at 681 Fifth Avenue
- Moved to its current location in Central Park in 1880
- Has grown to become one of the world's largest and most comprehensive art museums

## Notable Collections
The Met houses over 2 million works of art spanning 5,000 years of world culture, including:

- **European Paintings**: Masterpieces by Rembrandt, Vermeer, Monet, Van Gogh, and other renowned artists
- **American Art**: Extensive collection of American decorative arts and paintings
- **Ancient Egyptian Art**: Including the famous Temple of Dendur
- **Greek and Roman Antiquities**: Sculptures, vases, and other artifacts from classical antiquity
- **Asian Art**: Comprehensive collection spanning various Asian cultures and time periods
- **Islamic Art**: One of the most comprehensive collections in the Western world
- **Modern and Contemporary Art**: Works from the 20th and 21st centuries
- **Costume Institute**: Home to over 35,000 costumes and accessories, famous for the annual Met Gala

The museum continues to be a major cultural institution, attracting millions of visitors annually and serving as a center for art education and scholarship.
