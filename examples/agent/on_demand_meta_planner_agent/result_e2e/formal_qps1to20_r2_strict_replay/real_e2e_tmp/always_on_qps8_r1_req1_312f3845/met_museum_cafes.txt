# Top 3 Cafes Near The Metropolitan Museum of Art

## 1. Cabuc Coffee
**Rating:** 4.6/5 (153 reviews)
**Address:** 1211 Lexington Ave, New York, NY 10028
**Phone:** (646) 891-0011
**Currently Open:** Yes

**Highlights:**
- Known for excellent Turkish coffee and unique drinks like Baklava Latte
- Friendly staff and bright, pleasant atmosphere
- Offers Wi-Fi, making it suitable for working
- Features Turkish music creating a unique ambiance
- Popular items include Baklava Latte and Salty Honey Croissant

**Customer Reviews:**
Customers consistently praise the exceptional coffee quality and friendly service. Many mention the unique Turkish influence on the menu and atmosphere. The cafe is described as a great spot for both coffee enjoyment and getting work done.

## 2. Antonucci Cafe
**Rating:** 4.5/5 (428 reviews)
**Address:** 170 E 81st St, New York, NY 10028
**Phone:** (212) 570-5100
**Website:** https://antonuccicafe81.com/
**Price Level:** $$$ (Higher-end)
**Currently Open:** No

**Highlights:**
- Authentic Italian restaurant with cozy, intimate atmosphere
- Features artwork on walls creating a warm environment
- Known for Burrata Salad with marinated tomatoes and perfectly cooked fish
- Offers gluten-free pasta options
- Family-run establishment with attentive service

**Customer Reviews:**
Reviewers appreciate the authentic Italian cuisine and intimate setting. The Burrata Salad receives special mention as "amazing." Some note that dessert selection is limited during daytime hours. A few customers mentioned that some dishes can be on the bland side, but overall satisfaction is high.

## 3. Dear Coffee
**Rating:** 4.6/5 (330 reviews)
**Address:** 1246 Lexington Ave, New York, NY 10028
**Website:** https://www.dearcoffeenyc.com/
**Currently Open:** No

**Highlights:**
- Minimalist-style cafe in the Upper East Side
- Uses high-quality Onyx Coffee Lab beans for espresso and pour-over
- Known for unique specialty drinks like Apricot Matcha
- Artistic latte designs (customers have received dragon latte art!)
- Cozy atmosphere with limited seating

**Customer Reviews:**
Customers love the exceptional coffee quality and creative drink options. The Apricot Matcha receives particular praise for its balanced flavor profile. Reviewers note the cafe is small with limited seating and no restroom, making it better suited for quick visits or takeout rather than extended stays.

---

All three cafes are within a 5-10 minute walk from The Metropolitan Museum of Art, making them convenient options for a coffee break before or after your museum visit.
