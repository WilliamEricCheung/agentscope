# National Parks in Colorado

According to the National Park Service, there are 17 national park sites in Colorado. These include national parks, national monuments, national historic sites, and national historic trails that pass through the state.

## National Parks
1. **Black Canyon Of The Gunnison National Park** - Features some of the steepest cliffs, oldest rock, and craggiest spires in North America.
2. **Great Sand Dunes National Park & Preserve** - Home to the tallest dunes in North America, along with diverse landscapes including grasslands, wetlands, forests, alpine lakes, and tundra.
3. **Mesa Verde National Park** - Protects the cultural heritage of 27 Pueblos and Tribes, featuring ancient cliff dwellings built by Ancestral Pueblo people over 700 years ago.
4. **Rocky Mountain National Park** - Encompasses 415 square miles of mountain environments, from montane meadows to alpine lakes and towering peaks.

## National Monuments
5. **Colorado National Monument** - Preserves dramatic red rock canyons and towering monoliths near Grand Junction.
6. **Curecanti National Recreation Area** - Features three reservoirs and surrounding lands in western Colorado.
7. **Dinosaur National Monument** - Spans Colorado and Utah, preserving dinosaur fossils embedded in rocks along with mountains, desert, and river canyons.
8. **Florissant Fossil Beds National Monument** - Contains one of the richest and most diverse fossil deposits in the world, including petrified redwood stumps and detailed insect fossils.
9. **Hovenweep National Monument** - Spans Colorado and Utah, preserving six prehistoric sites built between 1200-1300 CE with impressive stone structures.
10. **Yucca House National Monument** - Preserves a large unexcavated pueblo in Montezuma Valley between Mesa Verde and Ute Mountain.

## National Historic Sites
11. **Amache National Historic Site** - Formerly the Granada Relocation Center, one of ten incarceration sites for Japanese Americans during World War II.
12. **Bent's Old Fort National Historic Site** - A reconstructed adobe trading post on the Santa Fe Trail where traders, trappers, travelers, and Native American tribes met for trade.
13. **Sand Creek Massacre National Historic Site** - Commemorates the tragic 1864 attack on Cheyenne and Arapaho people encamped along Big Sandy Creek.

## National Historic Trails (that pass through Colorado)
14. **California National Historic Trail** - Follows the route of over 250,000 emigrants who traveled to California during the 1840s-1850s.
15. **Old Spanish National Historic Trail** - Traces the routes of mule pack trains between Santa Fe, New Mexico, and Los Angeles, California.
16. **Pony Express National Historic Trail** - Follows the relay system used to carry mail from Missouri to California in just 10 days.
17. **Santa Fe National Historic Trail** - Traces the commercial highway between western Missouri and Santa Fe, New Mexico.

This list includes all units of the National Park System located in or passing through Colorado as identified by the National Park Service.
