# Top Cafes Near The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Based on proximity (within 500 meters) and customer ratings, here are the best cafe options near the Metropolitan Museum of Art located at 1000 5th Ave, New York, NY 10028.

## 1. 787 Coffee ⭐ 4.9/5 (3,472 reviews)
**Address:** 228 E 80th St, New York, NY 10075
**Phone:** (646) 850-0234
**Website:** http://www.787coffee.com/

**Highlights:**
- Highest rated cafe in the area with an exceptional 4.9/5 rating
- Known for unique rum-infused coffee beans
- Cozy, neighborhood feel with friendly staff who remember regular customers
- Fast WiFi and comfortable seating including a swing by the window
- Pet-friendly establishment
- Freshly baked pastries available all day

**Customer Feedback:** Visitors consistently praise the exceptional coffee quality, unique flavored options (especially rum and whiskey-infused coffees), and the warm, welcoming atmosphere. The staff, particularly baristas like Alex, Fox, and Isa, receive special mention for their knowledge and hospitality.

## 2. Dear Coffee ⭐ 4.6/5 (330 reviews)
**Address:** 1246 Lexington Ave, New York, NY 10028
**Website:** https://www.dearcoffeenyc.com/

**Highlights:**
- Uses high-quality Onyx Coffee Lab beans for espresso and pour-over
- Creative specialty drinks like apricot matcha
- Minimalist aesthetic with cozy atmosphere
- Convenient location on Lexington Avenue

**Customer Feedback:** Customers appreciate the excellent coffee quality and unique drink options. The apricot matcha receives particular praise for its balanced flavor profile. Note that the space is small with limited seating and no restrooms, making it better for quick visits or takeout.

## 3. Antonucci Cafe ⭐ 4.5/5 (428 reviews)
**Address:** 170 E 81st St, New York, NY 10028
**Phone:** (212) 570-5100
**Website:** https://antonuccicafe81.com/
**Price Level:** $$$

**Highlights:**
- Italian cafe and restaurant with charming, intimate atmosphere
- Features artwork on the walls creating a warm ambiance
- Offers gluten-free pasta options
- Serves both coffee and full meals including Burrata salad and fresh fish

**Customer Feedback:** Patrons enjoy the authentic Italian experience and quality food. Some note that not all desserts are available during daytime hours, and the portions can be generous. The chicken meatballs and handmade fettuccini receive special mention.

## 4. Tarallucci e Vino at Cooper Hewitt ⭐ 4.5/5 (30 reviews)
**Address:** 9 E 90th St, New York, NY 10128 (Inside Cooper Hewitt Design Museum)
**Phone:** (212) 849-8483
**Website:** https://www.taralluccievino.net/cooper-hewitt-location
**Price Level:** $$

**Highlights:**
- Located inside the beautiful Cooper Hewitt Design Museum
- Outdoor seating available in the museum courtyard during good weather
- Serves both coffee and wine
- Known for house-made pastries and cookies

**Customer Feedback:** Visitors consistently praise barista Fabian for his exceptional service and cheerful demeanor. The cafe offers a unique cultural experience combined with quality coffee and pastries. Limited seating can be an issue as some patrons stay for extended periods.
