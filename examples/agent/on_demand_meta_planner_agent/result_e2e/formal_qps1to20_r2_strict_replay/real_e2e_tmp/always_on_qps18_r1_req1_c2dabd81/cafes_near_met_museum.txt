- **Phone**: (917) 409-5555

- **Price Level**: $$ (Moderate)

### 2. Siena Cafe • Italian • Brunch • Dinner • Lunch ⭐ 4.4/5 (1,059 reviews)
- **Address**: 1580 3rd Ave, New York, NY 10128
- **Phone**: (212) 410-5588
- **Website**: https://sienacafe.com/
- **Price Level**: $$ (Moderate)
- **Currently Open**: Yes
- **Special Features**:

### 3. Antonucci Cafe ⭐ 4.5/5 (428 reviews)
- **Address**: 170 E 81st St, New York, NY 10028
- **Phone**: (212) 570-5100
- **Website**: https://antonuccicafe81.com/
- **Price Level**: $$$ (Expensive)
- **Currently Open**: Yes
- **Special Features**:
  - Authentic Italian family-style restaurant
  - Cozy, intimate setting with artwork on walls
  - Excellent Burrata salad and seafood dishes
  - Gluten-free pasta options available
  - Some desserts only available in the evening
