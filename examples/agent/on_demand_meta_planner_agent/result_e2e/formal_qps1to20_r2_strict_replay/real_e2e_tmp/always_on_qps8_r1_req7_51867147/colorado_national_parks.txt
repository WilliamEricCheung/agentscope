# National Parks and Monuments in Colorado

Based on the National Parks Service data, here are all 17 national parks, monuments, historic sites, and trails located in Colorado:

## National Parks
1. **Black Canyon Of The Gunnison National Park** - Features some of the steepest cliffs, oldest rock, and craggiest spires in North America.
2. **Great Sand Dunes National Park & Preserve** - Home to the tallest dunes in North America, along with grasslands, wetlands, forests, alpine lakes, and tundra.
3. **Mesa Verde National Park** - Protects the rich cultural heritage of 27 Pueblos and Tribes, featuring Ancestral Pueblo cliff dwellings.
4. **Rocky Mountain National Park** - Encompasses 415 square miles of mountain environments, from meadows to alpine lakes and towering peaks.

## National Monuments
5. **Colorado National Monument** - Preserves one of the grand landscapes of the American West with towering monoliths within a vast plateau and canyon panorama.
6. **Curecanti National Recreation Area** - Three reservoirs and surrounding lands offering both natural and human-made features.
7. **Dinosaur National Monument** - Contains embedded dinosaur fossils from 150 million years ago, plus mountains, desert, and rivers (shared with Utah).
8. **Florissant Fossil Beds National Monument** - One of the richest and most diverse fossil deposits in the world, featuring petrified redwood stumps.
9. **Hovenweep National Monument** - Preserves six prehistoric sites built between 1200-1300 CE with multistory towers (shared with Utah).
10. **Yucca House National Monument** - A large unexcavated pueblo nestled between Mesa Verde and Ute Mountain.

## National Historic Sites
11. **Amache National Historic Site** - Former incarceration site for Japanese Americans during World War II.
12. **Bent's Old Fort National Historic Site** - Adobe trading post on the Santa Fe Trail where traders, trappers, travelers, and Native tribes met.
13. **Sand Creek Massacre National Historic Site** - Commemorates the tragic 1864 massacre of Cheyenne and Arapaho people.

## National Historic Trails (portions in Colorado)
14. **California National Historic Trail** - Follows the route of over 250,000 emigrants who traveled to California during the 1840s-1850s.
15. **Old Spanish National Historic Trail** - Follows routes of mule pack trains between Santa Fe, New Mexico, and Los Angeles, California.
16. **Pony Express National Historic Trail** - Marks the relay system that carried mail from Missouri to California in just 10 days.
17. **Santa Fe National Historic Trail** - Traces the commercial highway between western Missouri and Santa Fe, New Mexico.

This list includes all units of the National Park System that have land within Colorado's borders.
