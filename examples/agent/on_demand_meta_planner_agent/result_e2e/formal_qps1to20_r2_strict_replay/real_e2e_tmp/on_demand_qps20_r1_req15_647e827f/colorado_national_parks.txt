# National Parks and Monuments in Colorado

Based on the National Parks Service data, here are all 17 national parks, monuments, and historic sites located in Colorado:

1. **Amache National Historic Site** (amch)
   - Designation: National Historic Site
   - Description: Former Japanese American incarceration site during World War II

2. **Bent's Old Fort National Historic Site** (beol)
   - Designation: National Historic Site
   - Description: Adobe trading post on the Santa Fe Trail from the 1840s

3. **Black Canyon Of The Gunnison National Park** (blca)
   - Designation: National Park
   - Description: Features some of the steepest cliffs and oldest rock in North America

4. **California National Historic Trail** (cali)
   - Designation: National Historic Trail
   - Description: Over 5,000-mile trail that passes through 10 states including Colorado

5. **Colorado National Monument** (colm)
   - Designation: National Monument
   - Description: Preserves red rock canyons and monoliths near Grand Junction

6. **Curecanti National Recreation Area** (cure)
   - Designation: National Recreation Area
   - Description: Three reservoirs and surrounding lands in western Colorado

7. **Dinosaur National Monument** (dino)
   - Designation: National Monument
   - Description: Contains dinosaur fossils embedded in rocks; spans Colorado and Utah

8. **Florissant Fossil Beds National Monument** (flfo)
   - Designation: National Monument
   - Description: Rich fossil deposits including petrified redwood stumps and insect fossils

9. **Great Sand Dunes National Park & Preserve** (grsa)
   - Designation: National Park & Preserve
   - Description: Home to the tallest dunes in North America

10. **Hovenweep National Monument** (hove)
    - Designation: National Monument
    - Description: Prehistoric sites with multistory towers; spans Colorado and Utah

11. **Mesa Verde National Park** (meve)
    - Designation: National Park
    - Description: Protects Ancestral Pueblo cliff dwellings and cultural heritage

12. **Old Spanish National Historic Trail** (olsp)
    - Designation: National Historic Trail
    - Description: Route of mule pack trains between Santa Fe and Los Angeles

13. **Pony Express National Historic Trail** (poex)
    - Designation: National Historic Trail
    - Description: Historic mail delivery route that passed through eight states including Colorado

14. **Rocky Mountain National Park** (romo)
    - Designation: National Park
    - Description: 415 square miles of mountain environments with over 300 miles of hiking trails

15. **Sand Creek Massacre National Historic Site** (sand)
    - Designation: National Historic Site
    - Description: Commemorates the 1864 massacre of Cheyenne and Arapaho people

16. **Santa Fe National Historic Trail** (safe)
    - Designation: National Historic Trail
    - Description: Historic trade route between Missouri and Santa Fe that passes through Colorado

17. **Yucca House National Monument** (yuho)
    - Designation: National Monument
    - Description: Unexcavated pueblo site between Mesa Verde and Ute Mountain

Note: Some sites like the California, Old Spanish, Pony Express, and Santa Fe National Historic Trails span multiple states but have portions within Colorado.
