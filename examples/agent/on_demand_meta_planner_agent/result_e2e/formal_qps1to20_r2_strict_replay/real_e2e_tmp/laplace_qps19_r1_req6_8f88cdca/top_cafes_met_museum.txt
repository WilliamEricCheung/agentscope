# Top-Rated Cafes Near The Metropolitan Museum of Art

Based on Google Maps data, here are the highest-rated cafes within walking distance (800 meters) of the Metropolitan Museum of Art in New York.

## 1. 787 Coffee ⭐ 4.9/5 (3,472 reviews)
**Address:** 228 E 80th St, New York, NY 10075
**Phone:** (646) 850-0234
**Website:** http://www.787coffee.com/
**Distance from Met:** Approximately 0.6 miles (10-minute walk)

**Highlights:**
- Highest rated cafe in the area with exceptional reviews
- Known for unique rum-infused coffee beans
- Cozy atmosphere with friendly staff who remember regular customers
- Fast WiFi and comfortable seating
- Features a swing by the window for a unique experience
- Freshly baked pastries throughout the day

**Notable Review:** "Alex made us feel at home like 787 was our neighborhood hangout spot. I stayed for hours, the WiFi was fast, and the lattes and tea were excellent."
