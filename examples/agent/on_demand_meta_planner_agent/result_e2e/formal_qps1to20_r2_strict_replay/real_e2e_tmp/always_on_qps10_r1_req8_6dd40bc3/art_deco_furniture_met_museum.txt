ADDITIONAL INFORMATION:
The Metropolitan Museum's collection includes a substantial number of Art Deco furniture pieces spanning the full range of the movement (1920s-1940s). The collection features works by both well-known designers and anonymous craftsmen, representing various materials and styles characteristic of the Art Deco period, including:
- Geometric forms and streamlined designs
- Use of exotic woods, metals, and new synthetic materials
- Combination of traditional craftsmanship with modern industrial techniques
- International representation from France, Sweden, America, and other countries

The collection is primarily housed in the Modern and Contemporary Art department, with some pieces also found in European Sculpture and Decorative Arts.
