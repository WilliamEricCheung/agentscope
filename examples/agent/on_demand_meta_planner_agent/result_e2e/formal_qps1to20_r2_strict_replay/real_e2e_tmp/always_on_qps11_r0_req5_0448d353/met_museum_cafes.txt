# Top-Rated Cafes Near The Metropolitan Museum of Art

This report details the highest-rated cafes within walking distance (approximately 1 km or less) of the Metropolitan Museum of Art in New York City.

**Distance:** 0.9 km (13-minute walk)
**Address:** 228 E 80th St, New York, NY 10075
**Phone:** (646) 850-0234
**Website:** http://www.787coffee.com/
**Currently Open:** Yes

**Highlights:**
- Exceptionally high rating with thousands of positive reviews
- Known for unique rum-infused coffee beans
- Pet-friendly establishment
- Fast WiFi and comfortable seating
- Features a swing by the window for enjoying street views
- Staff remembers regular customers' names and preferences

**Customer Review Excerpt:**
"Alex made us feel right at home, like 787 was our neighborhood hangout spot. I stayed for hours, the WiFi was fast, and the lattes and tea were excellent."

## Summary
All these cafes are highly rated (4.7+ stars) and within a 19-minute walk from the Metropolitan Museum of Art. 787 Coffee stands out with its exceptional 4.9-star rating and unique rum-infused coffee offerings, while being the closest option at just 13 minutes walking distance. For those interested in Latin American cuisine, Cositas Café offers authentic pastries and beverages. Green Lane Coffee is perfect for serious coffee enthusiasts seeking third-wave coffee experiences, and Ella's provides a charming atmosphere with the added bonus of wine service in the evenings.
