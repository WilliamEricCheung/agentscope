# Top 3 Cafes Near The Metropolitan Museum of Art

## 1. 787 Coffee
**Rating:** 4.9/5 (3,482 reviews)
**Address:** 228 E 80th St, New York, NY 10075
**Phone:** (646) 850-0234
**Website:** http://www.787coffee.com/
**Current Status:** Closed

**Highlights:**
- Exceptionally high rating with thousands of positive reviews
- Known for unique rum-infused coffee beans
- Pet-friendly establishment
- Cozy atmosphere with a swing by the window
- Fast WiFi and comfortable seating
- Staff remembers regular customers' names and preferences

**Popular Items:**
- Rum-infused cappuccino
- Matcha latte
- Freshly baked pastries throughout the day

## 2. La Bomboniera
**Rating:** 4.7/5 (560 reviews)
**Address:** 1378 Lexington Ave, New York, NY 10128
**Phone:** (646) 370-6302
**Website:** http://labombonieranyc.com/
**Current Status:** Closed

**Highlights:**
- Environmentally conscious cafe with zero single-use plastics
- Beautiful ambiance perfect for dates
- Serves both coffee and alcoholic beverages
- Offers reusable cups for a more sustainable experience
- Can get busy, especially during summer months

**Popular Items:**
- Hot chocolate with whipped cream (cioccolata calda con panna)
- Cannoli
- Lemon peel cake
- Cappuccino

## 3. Ella's Cafe Espresso & Wine Bar
**Rating:** 4.7/5 (544 reviews)
**Price Level:** $ (Affordable)
**Address:** 1465 2nd Ave, New York, NY 10075
**Phone:** (917) 409-0307
**Website:** https://www.ellascafenyc.com/
**Current Status:** Closed

**Highlights:**
- Charming pink cups and outdoor seating
- Features a small free library for book exchanges
- Comfortable indoor seating
- Serves both espresso drinks and wine
- Great for special occasions (hosted engagement parties)
- Family-friendly atmosphere

**Popular Items:**
- Avocado toast
- Shakshuka (North African eggs)
- Hummus and challah
- Perfect espresso

All three cafes are within walking distance of the Metropolitan Museum of Art and offer unique experiences beyond just coffee. While none were currently open at the time of search, they all have excellent ratings and distinctive features that make them worth visiting.
