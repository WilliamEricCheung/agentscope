# Top-Rated Cafes Near The Metropolitan Museum of Art

## 1. 787 Coffee ⭐ 4.9/5 (3,482 reviews)
**Address:** 228 E 80th St, New York, NY 10075  
**Phone:** (646) 850-0234  
**Website:** http://www.787coffee.com/  
**Currently Open:** No  

**Description:** A cozy neighborhood coffee shop known for its unique rum-infused coffee beans and friendly service. Customers rave about the excellent lattes, fast WiFi, and welcoming atmosphere that makes you feel like part of the community. The cafe features a swing by the window for enjoying street views while sipping your coffee. Regular customers note that staff remember their names and usual orders.

## 2. Cositas Café ⭐ 4.8/5 (184 reviews)
**Address:** 353 E 78th St, New York, NY 10075  
**Phone:** (347) 673-1733  
**Website:** https://www.cositascafenyc.com/  
**Price Level:** $  
**Currently Open:** No  

**Description:** A charming Latin American café offering authentic Colombian pastries, empanadas, and specialty drinks. Known for its incredible flan, delicious smoothies, and fresh-baked goods. The café provides an authentic taste of Latin America with excellent coffee and warm service in a cozy setting.

## 3. La Bomboniera ⭐ 4.7/5 (560 reviews)
**Address:** 1378 Lexington Ave, New York, NY 10128  
**Phone:** (646) 370-6302  
**Website:** http://labombonieranyc.com/  
**Currently Open:** No  

**Description:** An eco-conscious café that serves excellent coffee, hot chocolate with cream, and Italian pastries in a beautiful setting. Notable for its commitment to sustainability—using no single-use plastics or disposable products. The café offers both coffee drinks and alcoholic beverages, making it perfect for both daytime coffee dates and evening gatherings.

## 4. Madame Bonté Café ⭐ 4.5/5 (380 reviews)
**Address:** 318 E 84th St, New York, NY 10028  
**Phone:** (917) 409-5555  
**Website:** http://www.madamebonte.com/  
**Price Level:** $$  
**Currently Open:** Yes  

**Description:** A chic café with a unique policy: no electronic screens allowed in the downstairs seating area, encouraging genuine conversation. Features a hidden lower level and terrace seating. Known for excellent honey lavender lattes, croque monsieur sandwiches, and almond croissants. The café has designated laptop areas upstairs while preserving screen-free zones downstairs for social interaction.
