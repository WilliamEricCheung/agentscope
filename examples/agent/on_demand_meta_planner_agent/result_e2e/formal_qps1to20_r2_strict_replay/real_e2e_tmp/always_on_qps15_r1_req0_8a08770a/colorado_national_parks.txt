# National Parks and Monuments in Colorado

Based on the National Parks Service data, here are all 17 national parks, monuments, and historic sites located in Colorado:

## National Parks
1. **Black Canyon Of The Gunnison National Park** - Features some of the steepest cliffs, oldest rock, and craggiest spires in North America.
2. **Great Sand Dunes National Park & Preserve** - Home to the tallest dunes in North America, along with diverse landscapes including grasslands, wetlands, forests, alpine lakes, and tundra.
3. **Mesa Verde National Park** - Protects the rich cultural heritage of 27 Pueblos and Tribes, featuring Ancestral Pueblo cliff dwellings built over 700 years ago.
4. **Rocky Mountain National Park** - Encompasses 415 square miles of spectacular mountain environments, from meadows to alpine lakes and towering peaks.

## National Monuments
5. **Colorado National Monument** - Preserves one of the grand landscapes of the American West with towering monoliths within a vast plateau and canyon panorama.
6. **Curecanti National Recreation Area** - Three reservoirs and surrounding lands offering year-round recreation on both land and water.
7. **Dinosaur National Monument** - Contains embedded dinosaur fossils from 150 million years ago, along with mountains, desert, rivers, and petroglyphs (shared with Utah).
8. **Florissant Fossil Beds National Monument** - One of the richest and most diverse fossil deposits in the world, featuring petrified redwood stumps and detailed insect fossils.
9. **Hovenweep National Monument** - Preserves six prehistoric sites built between 1200-1300 CE, including multistory towers perched on canyon rims (shared with Utah).
10. **Yucca House National Monument** - A large unexcavated pueblo in Montezuma Valley, nestled between Mesa Verde and Ute Mountain.

## National Historic Sites
11. **Amache National Historic Site** - Formerly the Granada Relocation Center, one of ten incarceration sites for Japanese Americans during World War II.
12. **Bent's Old Fort National Historic Site** - An adobe trading post on the Santa Fe Trail where traders, trappers, travelers, and Native tribes met for trade.
13. **Sand Creek Massacre National Historic Site** - Commemorates the tragic 1864 massacre of Cheyenne and Arapaho people by U.S. forces.

## National Historic Trails (portions in Colorado)
14. **California National Historic Trail** - Over 5,000 miles long covering portions of 10 states, including Colorado.
15. **Old Spanish National Historic Trail** - Follows routes of mule pack trains across six states between Santa Fe, New Mexico, and Los Angeles, California.
16. **Pony Express National Historic Trail** - The historic mail delivery route that operated through eight states.
17. **Santa Fe National Historic Trail** - The historic trade route between western Missouri and Santa Fe, New Mexico.

This list includes all national park service units that are either entirely within Colorado or have significant portions within the state.
