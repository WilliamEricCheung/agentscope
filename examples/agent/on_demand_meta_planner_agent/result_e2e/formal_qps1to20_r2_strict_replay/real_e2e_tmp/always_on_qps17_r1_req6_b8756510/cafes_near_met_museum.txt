# Cafes Near The Metropolitan Museum of Art

## 1. 787 Coffee ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ (4.9/5)
- **Address**: 228 E 80th St, New York, NY 10075
- **Phone**: (646) 850-0234
- **Website**: http://www.787coffee.com/
- **Currently Open**: Yes
- **Price Level**: Not specified (appears to be moderate based on reviews)
- **Operating Hours**: Not explicitly provided, but appears to open early morning
- **Highlights**: 
  - Exceptionally high rating (4.9/5 from 3,477 reviews)
  - Known for unique rum-infused coffee beans
  - Friendly staff who remember regular customers
  - Pet-friendly (dogs welcome)
  - Fast WiFi available
  - Features a swing seat by the window
  - Freshly baked pastries all day

- **Address**: 226 E 83rd St, New York, NY 10028
- **Phone**: (646) 244-9539
- **Price Level**: $ (Budget-friendly)
- **Currently Open**: Yes
- **Operating Hours**: Not explicitly provided
- **Highlights**:
  - French atmosphere with excellent pastries
  - Small space with only four tables
  - Special offer: Buy one get one free on pastries after 5 PM
