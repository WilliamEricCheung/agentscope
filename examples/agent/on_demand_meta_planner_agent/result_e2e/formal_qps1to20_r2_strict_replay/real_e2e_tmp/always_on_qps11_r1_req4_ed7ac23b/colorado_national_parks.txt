# National Parks and Sites in Colorado

Based on the National Parks Service data, here are the national parks and sites located in Colorado:

1. **Amache National Historic Site**
   - Designation: National Historic Site
   - Description: One of ten incarceration sites established during World War II to unjustly incarcerate Japanese Americans. Over 10,000 people were incarcerated at Amache from 1942 to 1945.
   - Activities: Guided Tours, Self-Guided Tours - Auto, Junior Ranger Program

2. **Bent's Old Fort National Historic Site**
   - Designation: National Historic Site
   - Description: An adobe trading post on the Santa Fe Trail in the 1840s, where traders, trappers, travelers, and Native American tribes came together for trade.
   - Activities: Arts and Culture, Craft Demonstrations, Cultural Demonstrations, Biking, Guided Tours, Hiking, Living History, Junior Ranger Program, Wildlife Watching, Birdwatching, Park Film, Museum Exhibits

3. **Black Canyon Of The Gunnison National Park**
   - Designation: National Park
   - Description: Features some of the steepest cliffs, oldest rock, and craggiest spires in North America. The Gunnison River sculpted this canyon over two million years.
   - Activities: Astronomy, Stargazing, Camping, Climbing, Rock Climbing, Fishing, Hiking, Horseback Riding, Paddling, Kayaking, Junior Ranger Program, Skiing, Snowshoeing, Wildlife Watching

4. **California National Historic Trail** (partially in Colorado)
   - Designation: National Historic Trail
   - Description: Follows the route of over 250,000 emigrants who traveled to California during the 1840s and 1850s. The trail is over 5,000 miles long and covers portions of 10 states including Colorado.
   - Activities: Auto and ATV, Scenic Driving, Guided Tours, Self-Guided Tours - Auto, Hiking, Horseback Riding, Junior Ranger Program, Museum Exhibits

5. **Colorado National Monument**
   - Designation: National Monument
   - Description: Preserves one of the grand landscapes of the American West with towering monoliths within a vast plateau and canyon panorama.
   - Activities: Scenic Driving, Biking, Camping, Rock Climbing, Picnicking, Hiking, Junior Ranger Program, Wildlife Watching, Park Film

6. **Curecanti National Recreation Area**
   - Designation: National Recreation Area
   - Description: Three reservoirs and surrounding lands in western Colorado featuring both natural and human-made features.
   - Activities: Boating, Camping, Fishing, Hiking, Horseback Riding, Hunting, Ice Skating, Paddling, Junior Ranger Program, Skiing, Snowmobiling, Swimming, Wildlife Watching

7. **Dinosaur National Monument** (shared with Utah)
   - Designation: National Monument
   - Description: Contains dinosaur fossils embedded in rocks from 150 million years ago, along with mountains, desert, rivers, petroglyphs, and homesteader history.
   - Activities: Astronomy, Biking, Camping, Fishing, Guided Tours, Hiking, Paddling, Whitewater Rafting, Junior Ranger Program, Cross-Country Skiing, Wildlife Watching

8. **Florissant Fossil Beds National Monument**
   - Designation: National Monument
   - Description: Contains one of the richest and most diverse fossil deposits in the world, including petrified redwood stumps up to 14 feet wide and thousands of detailed fossils of insects and plants.
   - Activities: Astronomy, Picnicking, Guided Tours, Hiking, Horseback Riding, Junior Ranger Program, Snowshoeing, Park Film, Museum Exhibits

9. **Great Sand Dunes National Park & Preserve**
   - Designation: National Park & Preserve
   - Description: Home to the tallest dunes in North America within a diverse landscape of grasslands, wetlands, forests, alpine lakes, and tundra. Also designated as an International Dark Sky Park.
   - Activities: Arts and Culture, Auto Off-Roading, Astronomy, Biking, Camping, Mountain Climbing, Fishing, Dining, Picnicking, Guided Tours, Hiking, Horseback Riding, Hunting, Junior Ranger Program, Tubing, Skiing, Swimming, Wildlife Watching

10. **Hovenweep National Monument** (shared with Utah)
    - Designation: National Monument
    - Description: Preserves six prehistoric sites built between 1200 and 1300 CE which may have served as ceremonial centers, featuring multistory towers perched on canyon rims.
    - Activities: Astronomy, Stargazing, Camping, Hiking, Junior Ranger Program, Park Film
