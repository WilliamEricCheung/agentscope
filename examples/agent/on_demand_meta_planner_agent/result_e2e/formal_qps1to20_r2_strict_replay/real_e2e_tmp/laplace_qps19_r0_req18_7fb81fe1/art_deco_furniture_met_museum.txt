KEY INSIGHTS:
- The Met has an extensive collection of furniture that includes both authentic Art Deco pieces (1920s-1930s) and earlier decorative furniture that may be associated with Art Deco aesthetics
- Émile-Jacques Ruhlmann is well-represented in the collection with 39 pieces, making him a key figure for studying authentic Art Deco design
- Authentic Art Deco pieces are primarily housed in the Modern and Contemporary Art department
- The collection showcases the characteristic Art Deco elements: geometric forms, luxurious materials (ebony, ivory, exotic woods), and sophisticated craftsmanship

For researchers or visitors interested in authentic Art Deco furniture, the pieces by Émile-Jacques Ruhlmann represent the pinnacle of the movement's design philosophy.
