# The Metropolitan Museum of Art: Key Information Summary

## History
- Founded in 1870 by a group of Americans including philanthropists, artists, and businessmen
- Officially incorporated on April 13, 1870, with the goal of creating a national institution to inspire and educate the public
- First opened on February 20, 1872, at 681 Fifth Avenue
- Moved to its current location in 1880
- Key founders included John Taylor Johnston (first president), George Palmer Putnam (founding superintendent), Eastman Johnson, Frederic Edwin Church, and Henry Gurdon Marquand
- Luigi Palma di Cesnola served as the first director from 1879 to 1904

## Location
- Main building: 1000 Fifth Avenue, along Museum Mile on the eastern edge of Central Park on Manhattan's Upper East Side
- Second location: The Cloisters at Fort Tryon Park in Upper Manhattan, dedicated to medieval European art
- Third site: The Neue Galerie New York, set to become the museum's newest location in 2028
- The main building spans approximately 2 million square feet (190,000 m²), making it one of the world's largest art museums

## Notable Features
- Fourth-largest museum in the world by floor area and the largest art museum in the Americas
- Houses approximately 1.5 million works of art across 17 curatorial departments
- Most-visited museum in the United States with over 5.7 million visitors in fiscal year 2025
- Collection spans from ancient Near East and Egypt through classical antiquity to contemporary works
- Notable collections include:
  - Egyptian Art: Featuring the Temple of Dendur and over 26,000 pieces
  - European Paintings: Works by Old Masters, Impressionists, and Post-Impressionists
  - American Wing: Art from the 18th through early 20th century
  - Arms and Armor: One of the museum's most popular collections
  - Costume Institute: Known for the annual Met Gala
  - The Cloisters: Dedicated to medieval European art and architecture
  - Asian Art: One of the most comprehensive collections in the US with over 35,000 pieces
  - Islamic Art: One of the world's largest collections of Islamic art
  - Arts of Africa, Oceania, and the Americas: Housed in the Michael C. Rockefeller Wing

## Architecture
- The original portion was built in 1880
- The first part was designed by Calvert Vaux and Jacob Wrey Mould
- The Fifth Avenue facade, Great Hall, and Grand Stairway were designed in Beaux-Arts style by Richard Morris Hunt
- The building has been expanded over time with contributions from McKim, Mead & White and Roche-Dinkeloo
- Designated a National Historic Landmark in 1986
