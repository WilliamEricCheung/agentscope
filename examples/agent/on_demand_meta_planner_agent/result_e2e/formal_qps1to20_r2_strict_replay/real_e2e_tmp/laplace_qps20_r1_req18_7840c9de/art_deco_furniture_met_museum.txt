COLLECTION OVERVIEW:
The Metropolitan Museum has an extensive collection of furniture that includes both authentic Art Deco pieces from the 1920s-1930s period and earlier furniture that may have been misclassified in broader searches. The authentic Art Deco pieces are primarily housed in the Modern and Contemporary Art department, while many earlier pieces appear in European Sculpture and Decorative Arts.

Key characteristics of genuine Art Deco furniture in the collection include:
- Use of modern materials (iron, chrome, new synthetic materials)
- Geometric forms and streamlined designs
- Works by recognized Art Deco designers like Edgar Brandt
- Dating from approximately 1920-1940

For researchers interested in authentic Art Deco furniture, focusing on objects from the Modern and Contemporary Art department and pieces by known Art Deco designers will yield the most relevant results.
