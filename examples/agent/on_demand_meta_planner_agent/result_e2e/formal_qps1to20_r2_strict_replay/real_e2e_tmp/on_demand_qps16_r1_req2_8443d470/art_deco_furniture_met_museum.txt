ADDITIONAL NOTABLE FINDINGS:

The Met's collection includes Art Deco furniture from various countries and designers, representing the international nature of the Art Deco movement. The collection spans:
- French Art Deco (considered the origin and height of the style)
- Swedish/Nordic Art Deco
- American interpretations of Art Deco
- Various materials typical of the period: exotic woods, metals, lacquer, glass, and innovative synthetic materials

The broad search initially returned many pieces that were misclassified or from earlier periods (like 18th-century pieces by David Roentgen), highlighting the importance of date filtering when searching for authentic Art Deco works.

Art Deco furniture is characterized by:
- Geometric forms and clean lines
- Luxurious materials and fine craftsmanship
- Influences from Cubism, Futurism, and ancient Egyptian/Mesoamerican art
- Use of modern industrial materials alongside traditional ones
- Streamlined, elegant designs reflecting the Machine Age
