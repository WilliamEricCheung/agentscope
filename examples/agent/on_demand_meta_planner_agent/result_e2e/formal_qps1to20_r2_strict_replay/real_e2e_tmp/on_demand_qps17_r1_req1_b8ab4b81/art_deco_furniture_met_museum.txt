Art Deco Furniture in the Metropolitan Museum of Art Collection

The Metropolitan Museum of Art has an extensive collection of Art Deco furniture, with approximately 141 objects specifically from the Art Deco period (1920s-1930s).

Émile-Jacques Ruhlmann was one of the most important and influential designers of the Art Deco movement. His furniture is characterized by elegant proportions, luxurious materials (such as Macassar ebony, ivory, and exotic woods), and exquisite craftsmanship.

The Met's collection includes 39 pieces attributed to Ruhlmann, making it one of the most significant holdings of his work in the United States.
