# Top Cafes Near The Metropolitan Museum of Art

Based on geographic proximity and customer ratings, here are the top 5 cafes near the Metropolitan Museum of Art (located at 1000 5th Ave, New York, NY 10028).

## 1. Cafe d'Alsace ⭐ 4.7/5 (2,915 reviews)
**Address:** 1703 2nd Ave, New York, NY 10128
**Phone:** (212) 722-5133
**Website:** https://cafedalsace.com/
**Price Level:** $$
**Distance from Met:** Approximately 0.7 miles east

Cafe d'Alsace is a French restaurant with exceptional ratings and thousands of positive reviews. Customers praise its elegant yet cozy atmosphere, impeccable service, and excellent value for money. Their lunch special ($30) includes soup or salad, a daily main course, and dessert. Popular dishes include Provençal-style mussels with fries, steak with eggs, and perfectly cooked salmon. The restaurant features a beautiful back room for dining.

## 2. Paola's Cafe ⭐ 4.6/5 (10 reviews)
**Address:** 1361 Lexington Ave, New York, NY 10128
**Phone:** (212) 982-6650
**Website:** https://www.paolascafe.com/
**Distance from Met:** Approximately 0.8 miles northeast

Paola's Cafe is a charming Italian gem that has been serving the Upper East Side since 1985. Despite fewer reviews, it maintains an impressive 4.6-star rating. Patrons rave about their fresh pasta, meats, and mozzarella, noting that dishes aren't greasy like some other Italian restaurants. The burrata cheese comes highly recommended, and they offer an affordable selection of Italian wines. The restaurant has a loyal local following and provides exceptional service.

## 3. Noi Due Cafe ⭐ 4.6/5 (2,024 reviews)
**Address:** 491 Columbus Ave, New York, NY 10024
**Phone:** (212) 712-2222
**Website:** http://www.noiduecafe.com/
**Price Level:** $$$
**Distance from Met:** Approximately 0.9 miles west

Noi Due Cafe offers outstanding Italian cuisine with a perfect balance of flavors. Their square pizza is described as crispy and perfectly cooked, while pasta dishes feature rich, flavorful sauces. The tiramisu is considered a must-try dessert. The restaurant is certified kosher (Chalav Yisrael) and offers gluten-free options. Service is consistently praised as attentive and friendly, creating a warm, welcoming atmosphere.

## 4. Madame Bonté Café ⭐ 4.5/5 (380 reviews)
**Address:** 318 E 84th St, New York, NY 10028
**Phone:** (917) 409-5555
**Website:** http://www.madamebonte.com/
**Price Level:** $$
**Distance from Met:** Approximately 0.8 miles southeast

Madame Bonté Café offers a unique experience with its screen-free policy in the downstairs seating area, encouraging genuine conversation. The café features multiple seating areas including indoor space and outdoor terraces. Their honey lavender latte ranks among the best in the city, and food items like the Croque Monsieur sandwich receive high praise. The almond croissant with candied almonds is particularly recommended. Prices are slightly higher than average, but the ambiance makes it worth the visit.

## 5. Siena Cafe ⭐ 4.5/5 (1,056 reviews)
**Address:** 1580 3rd Ave, New York, NY 10128
**Phone:** (212) 410-5588
**Website:** https://sienacafe.com/
**Price Level:** $$
**Distance from Met:** Approximately 0.7 miles east

Siena Cafe offers Italian cuisine in a relaxed atmosphere. Their mussels appetizer receives special mention, along with vodka penne pasta described as having perfect flavor balance—spicy, rich, and savory all at once. The pasta is consistently cooked to perfection. They also serve brunch and lunch with reasonable prices. The "Pornstar" cocktail comes highly recommended by regular patrons. The restaurant has a popular bar area frequented by locals.
