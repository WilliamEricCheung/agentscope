National Parks in Colorado

Based on the National Parks Service data, here are all 17 national park sites located in Colorado:

1. Amache National Historic Site
   - Designation: National Historic Site
   - Description: Amache, also known as the Granada Relocation Center, was one of ten incarceration sites established during World War II to unjustly incarcerate Japanese Americans.

2. Bent's Old Fort National Historic Site
   - Designation: National Historic Site
   - Description: An adobe trading post on the Santa Fe Trail where traders, trappers, travelers, and Native American tribes came together for trade in the 1840s.

3. Black Canyon Of The Gunnison National Park
   - Designation: National Park
   - Description: Features some of the steepest cliffs, oldest rock, and craggiest spires in North America, carved by the Gunnison River over two million years.

4. California National Historic Trail
   - Designation: National Historic Trail
   - Description: Follows the route of over 250,000 emigrants who traveled to California during the 1840s and 1850s. The trail passes through portions of 10 states including Colorado.

5. Colorado National Monument
   - Designation: National Monument
   - Description: Preserves towering monoliths within a vast plateau and canyon panorama, with sheer-walled, red rock canyons along Rim Rock Drive.

6. Curecanti National Recreation Area
   - Designation: National Recreation Area
   - Description: Comprises three reservoirs and surrounding lands in western Colorado, offering both natural and human-made features.

7. Dinosaur National Monument
   - Designation: National Monument
   - Description: Contains dinosaur fossils embedded in rocks, along with mountains, desert, and rivers. Spans both Colorado and Utah.

8. Florissant Fossil Beds National Monument
   - Designation: National Monument
   - Description: Contains one of the richest and most diverse fossil deposits in the world, including petrified redwood stumps and detailed fossils of insects and plants.

9. Great Sand Dunes National Park & Preserve
   - Designation: National Park & Preserve
   - Description: Home to the tallest dunes in North America, set against a diverse landscape of grasslands, wetlands, forests, alpine lakes, and tundra.

10. Hovenweep National Monument
    - Designation: National Monument
    - Description: Preserves six prehistoric sites built between 1200 and 1300 CE which may have served as ceremonial centers. Spans both Colorado and Utah.

11. Mesa Verde National Park
    - Designation: National Park
    - Description: Protects the cultural heritage of the Ancestral Pueblo people who built communities on mesas and in cliffs for over 700 years.

12. Old Spanish National Historic Trail
    - Designation: National Historic Trail
    - Description: Follows the routes of mule pack trains across the Southwest between Santa Fe, New Mexico, and Los Angeles, California. Passes through six states including Colorado.

13. Pony Express National Historic Trail
    - Designation: National Historic Trail
    - Description: Marks the route where young men once rode horses to carry mail from Missouri to California in just 10 days. Passes through eight states including Colorado.

14. Rocky Mountain National Park
    - Designation: National Park
    - Description: Encompasses 415 square miles of spectacular mountain environments, from montane meadows to alpine lakes and towering peaks, with over 300 miles of hiking trails.

15. Sand Creek Massacre National Historic Site
    - Designation: National Historic Site
    - Description: Commemorates the tragic 1864 massacre of Cheyenne and Arapaho people by U.S. Army forces.

16. Santa Fe National Historic Trail
    - Designation: National Historic Trail
    - Description: Follows the route used for cargo transport between western Missouri and Santa Fe, New Mexico. Passes through five states including Colorado.

17. Yucca House National Monument
    - Designation: National Monument
    - Description: Preserves a large unexcavated pueblo in Montezuma Valley, nestled between Mesa Verde and Ute Mountain.
