# The Metropolitan Museum of Art: Overview

## History
The Metropolitan Museum of Art (often referred to as "The Met") was founded in 1870 by a group of American citizens including businessmen, financiers, and leading artists. The museum opened to the public on February 20, 1872, initially housed in a building on Fifth Avenue. In 1880, it moved to its current location in Central Park along Manhattan's Museum Mile. The iconic Beaux-Arts facade was completed in 1902, designed by architect Richard Morris Hunt.

## Significance
The Met is one of the world's largest and most comprehensive art museums, and it holds a position as one of the most important cultural institutions globally. It serves as:
- A major center for art education and scholarship
- A repository of over 2 million works spanning 5,000 years of world culture
- One of New York City's most visited tourist attractions
- A model for encyclopedic museums worldwide

## Main Collections
The Met's collection is organized into 17 curatorial departments:

### Ancient Art
- **Egyptian Art**: One of the finest collections outside Cairo, including the Temple of Dendur
- **Greek and Roman Art**: Extensive collection of sculptures, vases, and artifacts
- **Ancient Near Eastern Art**: Artifacts from Mesopotamia, Persia, and surrounding regions

### European Art
- **European Paintings**: Masterpieces by Rembrandt, Vermeer, Velázquez, Goya, Monet, Van Gogh, and many others
- **European Sculpture and Decorative Arts**: Furniture, tapestries, and decorative objects from the Renaissance through the 19th century
- **Arms and Armor**: Historic weapons and armor from around the world

### American Art
- **American Wing**: Comprehensive collection of American paintings, sculpture, and decorative arts from the colonial period through the early 20th century

### Asian Art
- **Asian Art**: Extensive collections from China, Japan, Korea, India, Southeast Asia, and the Himalayas, including paintings, sculptures, ceramics, and textiles

### Islamic Art
- **Islamic Art**: Artworks spanning 13 centuries and three continents, including calligraphy, ceramics, metalwork, and textiles

### Modern and Contemporary Art
- **Modern and Contemporary Art**: Works from the early 20th century to the present, including pieces by Picasso, Pollock, Rothko, and contemporary artists

### Other Notable Collections
- **African, Oceanic, and Americas Art**: Traditional and contemporary works from these regions
- **Costume Institute**: Historic and contemporary fashion (famous for the annual Met Gala)
- **Musical Instruments**: Historic instruments from around the world
- **Photographs**: One of the earliest and most comprehensive photography collections
- **Drawings and Prints**: Over 1 million works on paper

## Additional Information
- **Location**: 1000 Fifth Avenue, New York, NY 10028
- **Size**: Approximately 2 million square feet of floor space
- **Annual Visitors**: Over 7 million (pre-pandemic)
- **Notable Features**: The Met Cloisters in Fort Tryon Park, dedicated to medieval European art and architecture
