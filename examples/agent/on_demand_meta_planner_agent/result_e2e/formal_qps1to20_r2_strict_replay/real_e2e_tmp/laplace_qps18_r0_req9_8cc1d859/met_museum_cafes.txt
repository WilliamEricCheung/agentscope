# Top 3 Cafes Near The Metropolitan Museum of Art

## 1. 787 Coffee
**Rating:** 4.9/5 (3,472 reviews)
**Address:** 228 E 80th St, New York, NY 10075
**Phone:** (646) 850-0234
**Website:** http://www.787coffee.com/
**Distance from Met:** Approximately 0.5 miles (8-minute walk)

**Highlights:**
- Exceptionally high rating with thousands of positive reviews
- Known for unique rum-infused coffee beans
- Pet-friendly establishment
- Fast WiFi and comfortable seating
- Swing seat by the window for a unique experience
- Staff remembers regular customers' names and preferences

**Customer Feedback:**
Customers consistently praise the friendly staff (particularly mentioning baristas like Alex, Fox, and Isa), the unique rum and whiskey-flavored coffees, and the cozy neighborhood atmosphere. Many reviewers mention it as their favorite coffee shop in the area.

## 2. La Bomboniera
**Rating:** 4.7/5 (560 reviews)
**Address:** 1378 Lexington Ave, New York, NY 10128
**Phone:** (646) 370-6302
**Website:** http://labombonieranyc.com/
**Distance from Met:** Approximately 0.6 miles (12-minute walk)

**Highlights:**
- Eco-friendly establishment with no single-use plastics
- Serves coffee, hot chocolate, pastries, and has a bar
- Beautiful, cozy atmosphere
- Italian-inspired menu with items like cannoli
- Sustainable business practices

**Customer Feedback:**
Reviewers love the hot chocolate with cream, Italian pastries, and the overall ambiance. The eco-friendly approach is highly appreciated by environmentally conscious customers. One minor criticism mentioned occasional service issues during busy periods.

## 3. Café Blériot XI
**Rating:** 4.6/5 (310 reviews)
**Address:** 226 E 83rd St, New York, NY 10028
**Phone:** (646) 244-9539
**Price Level:** $ (Affordable)
**Distance from Met:** Approximately 0.3 miles (6-minute walk)

**Highlights:**
- French-inspired café with authentic pastries
- Cozy space with only four small tables
- After 5 PM: Buy one pastry, get one free!
- No laptop policy (not designed for remote work)
- Freshly baked pastries including croissants, financiers, and canelés

**Customer Feedback:**
Customers rave about the delicious pastries, particularly the ham and cheese croissants. The French atmosphere and friendly staff receive consistent praise. Some note that music can be loud for such a small space, and the limited seating means it's best for quick visits rather than extended stays.

---

All three cafes are within a 15-minute walk from The Metropolitan Museum of Art, making them perfect spots to relax before or after exploring the museum's extensive collections.
