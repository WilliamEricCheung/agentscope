# Cafes Near The Metropolitan Museum of Art

Based on Google Maps search results, here are some highly-rated cafes within 500 meters of the Metropolitan Museum of Art (1000 5th Ave, New York, NY 10028).

## Top Recommendations:

### 1. 787 Coffee ⭐ 4.9/5 (3,472 reviews)
- **Address**: 228 E 80th St, New York, NY 10075
- **Phone**: (646) 850-0234
- **Website**: http://www.787coffee.com/
- **Highlights**: 
  - Exceptionally high rating with nearly perfect scores
  - Known for unique rum-infused coffee beans
  - Friendly staff who remember regular customers
  - Pet-friendly with a cozy atmosphere
  - Fast WiFi and comfortable seating including a swing by the window

### 2. Antonucci Cafe ⭐ 4.5/5 (428 reviews)
- **Address**: 170 E 81st St, New York, NY 10028
- **Phone**: (212) 570-5100
- **Website**: https://antonuccicafe81.com/
- **Price Level**: $$$
- **Highlights**:
  - Charming Italian cafe with warm, intimate atmosphere
  - Excellent Burrata salad with marinated tomatoes
  - Perfectly cooked fettuccini with generous mushroom portions
  - Cozy space with artwork on the walls
  - Note: Some desserts are only available in the evening

### 3. Tarallucci e Vino at Cooper Hewitt ⭐ 4.5/5 (30 reviews)
- **Address**: 9 E 90th St, New York, NY 10128 (Inside Cooper Hewitt Design Museum)
- **Phone**: (212) 849-8483
- **Website**: https://www.taralluccievino.net/cooper-hewitt-location
- **Price Level**: $$
- **Highlights**:
  - Located inside the beautiful Cooper Hewitt Design Museum
  - Outdoor seating available in the museum courtyard (weather permitting)
  - Excellent coffee and house-made pastries
  - Notable staff member Fabian receives consistent praise for exceptional service
  - Good wine selection
