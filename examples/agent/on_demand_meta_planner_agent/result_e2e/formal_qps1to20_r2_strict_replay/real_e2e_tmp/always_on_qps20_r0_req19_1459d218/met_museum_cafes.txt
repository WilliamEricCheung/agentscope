# Top 3 Cafes Near The Metropolitan Museum of Art

## 1. 787 Coffee
- **Rating**: 4.9/5 (3,482 reviews)
- **Address**: 228 E 80th St, New York, NY 10075
- **Phone**: (646) 850-0234
- **Website**: http://www.787coffee.com/
- **Distance from Met**: Approximately 400m southeast
- **Highlights**: 
  - Exceptionally high rating with thousands of positive reviews
  - Known for unique rum-infused coffee beans
  - Pet-friendly establishment
  - Cozy atmosphere with a swing by the window
  - Fast WiFi available
  - Staff remembers regular customers by name

## 2. Dear Coffee
- **Rating**: 4.6/5 (331 reviews)
- **Address**: 1246 Lexington Ave, New York, NY 10028
- **Website**: https://www.dearcoffeenyc.com/
- **Distance from Met**: Approximately 450m east
- **Highlights**:
  - Uses Onyx Coffee Lab beans for espresso and pour-over
  - Known for specialty drinks like Apricot Matcha
  - Minimalist style cafe in the Upper East Side
  - Limited seating (mostly counter seating)
  - No restrooms available
  - Not ideal for laptop work sessions

## 3. Café Blériot XI
- **Rating**: 4.6/5 (310 reviews)
- **Address**: 226 E 83rd St, New York, NY 10028
- **Phone**: (646) 244-9539
- **Price Level**: $ (Budget-friendly)
- **Distance from Met**: Approximately 500m east
- **Highlights**:
  - French-inspired cafe atmosphere
  - Excellent pastries including ham & cheese croissants
  - Small space with only four small tables
  - No laptop policy (not a work cafe)
  - Buy one get one free on pastries after 5 PM
  - Specializes in espresso drinks

Note: Operating hours information was not available through the search tools. It's recommended to check the official websites or call ahead before visiting.
