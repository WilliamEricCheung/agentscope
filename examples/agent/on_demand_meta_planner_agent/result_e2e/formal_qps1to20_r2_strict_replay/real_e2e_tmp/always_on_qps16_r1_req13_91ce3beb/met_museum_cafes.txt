# Top 3 Cafes Near The Metropolitan Museum of Art

## 1. La Bomboniera ⭐ 4.7/5 (560 reviews)
**Address:** 1378 Lexington Ave, New York, NY 10128  
**Phone:** (646) 370-6302  
**Website:** http://labombonieranyc.com/  
**Currently Open:** No

La Bomboniera is the highest-rated cafe near the Met with an impressive 4.7-star rating. This charming establishment is known for its commitment to sustainability, using no single-use plastics or disposable products. Customers rave about their hot chocolate with cream, Italian biscotti, and cappuccino. The cafe offers a cozy atmosphere perfect for dates or meeting friends, though it can get crowded during peak times. They also have a bar area and serve delicious pastries including lemon peel cake and chocolate cake.

## 2. Cabuc Coffee ⭐ 4.6/5 (153 reviews)
**Address:** 1211 Lexington Ave, New York, NY 10028  
**Phone:** (646) 891-0011  
**Currently Open:** Yes

Cabuc Coffee is currently open and offers a bright, welcoming space perfect for both coffee enjoyment and working. With a 4.6-star rating, this cafe specializes in Turkish-inspired coffee drinks including Turkish coffee and baklava lattes. Customers praise the friendly staff, excellent coffee quality, and unique menu items like salty honey croissants. The cafe provides WiFi and ceramic cups for in-house dining, creating a comfortable environment for both quick visits and longer stays.

## 3. Uptown Cafe and Pastries ⭐ 4.5/5 (85 reviews)
**Address:** 100 E 96th St, New York, NY 10128  
**Phone:** (646) 410-0132  
**Currently Open:** Yes

Uptown Cafe and Pastries offers a modern yet cozy atmosphere with plenty of seating options including window seats. Currently open, this cafe features seasonal specialty drinks like cookie butter latte, lavender latte, and various cold brew foam beverages. Customers appreciate the use of ceramic mugs instead of paper cups, encouraging patrons to sit and enjoy their drinks. The cafe serves fresh pastries including chocolate brownies and Danish pastries, and provides a quiet, comfortable space ideal for working or relaxing.

All three cafes are within walking distance of the Metropolitan Museum of Art, making them perfect spots to grab a coffee before or after your museum visit.
