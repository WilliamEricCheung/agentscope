# Top-Rated Cafes Near the Metropolitan Museum of Art

Based on research using Google Maps data, here are the best cafes within walking distance (500 meters) of the Metropolitan Museum of Art, ranked by rating:

## 1. 787 Coffee (4.9/5 stars)
- **Address**: 228 E 80th St, New York, NY 10075
- **Phone**: (646) 850-0234
- **Website**: http://www.787coffee.com/
- **Highlights**: 
  - Exceptionally high rating with over 3,400 reviews
  - Known for unique rum and whiskey-infused coffee beans
  - Friendly staff who remember regular customers
  - Cozy atmosphere with a swing seat by the window
  - Fast WiFi and pet-friendly

## 2. Dear Coffee (4.6/5 stars)
- **Address**: 1246 Lexington Ave, New York, NY 10028
- **Website**: https://www.dearcoffeenyc.com/
- **Highlights**:
  - Uses Onyx Coffee Lab beans (a premium coffee brand)
  - Specializes in unique drinks like apricot matcha
  - Minimalist aesthetic
  - Note: Small space with limited seating and no restrooms

## 3. Café Blériot XI (4.6/5 stars)
- **Address**: 226 E 83rd St, New York, NY 10028
- **Phone**: (646) 244-9539
- **Highlights**:
  - French-inspired café with excellent pastries
  - Buy-one-get-one-free pastries after 5 PM
  - Cozy atmosphere with only four small tables
  - Note: No laptop use allowed

## 4. Antonucci Cafe (4.5/5 stars)
- **Address**: 170 E 81st St, New York, NY 10028
- **Phone**: (212) 570-5100
- **Website**: https://antonuccicafe81.com/
- **Highlights**:
  - Italian restaurant/café with authentic cuisine
  - Excellent Burrata salad and handmade pasta
  - Intimate setting with artwork on the walls
  - Higher price point (price level 3)

## 5. Tarallucci e Vino at Cooper Hewitt (4.5/5 stars)
- **Address**: 9 E 90th St, New York, NY 10128 (inside Cooper Hewitt Design Museum)
- **Phone**: (212) 849-8483
- **Website**: https://www.taralluccievino.net/cooper-hewitt-location
- **Highlights**:
  - Located in the beautiful Cooper Hewitt Design Museum
  - Outdoor seating in the courtyard when weather permits
  - Excellent coffee and house-made pastries
  - Wine selection available
  - Note: Limited vegetarian options

All of these cafes are within a 5-7 minute walk from the Metropolitan Museum of Art, making them perfect spots to relax before or after your museum visit. Based on ratings and reviews, 787 Coffee stands out as the highest-rated option with exceptional service and unique coffee offerings.
