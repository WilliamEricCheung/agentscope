# Top 3 Cafes Near The Metropolitan Museum of Art

## 1. 787 Coffee
**Rating:** 4.9/5 (3,482 reviews)
**Address:** 228 E 80th St, New York, NY 10075
**Phone:** (646) 850-0234
**Website:** http://www.787coffee.com/
**Distance from Met:** Approximately 0.5 miles southeast

**Highlights:**
- Exceptionally high rating with thousands of positive reviews
- Known for unique rum-infused coffee beans
- Pet-friendly establishment
- Fast WiFi and comfortable seating
- Swing seat by the window for people-watching
- Staff remembers regular customers' names and orders

**Customer Reviews:**
- "The rum-infused cappuccino was fantastic!" - Annonymouse Foodie
- "Coffee is delicious, baristas are adorable" - Uesfoodsandfinds
- "They have fresh baked pastries all day" - Jaye Aster Broder

## 2. Café Blériot XI
**Rating:** 4.6/5 (310 reviews)
**Address:** 226 E 83rd St, New York, NY 10028
**Phone:** (646) 244-9539
**Price Level:** $
**Distance from Met:** Approximately 0.4 miles east

**Highlights:**
- Charming French atmosphere
- Freshly baked pastries described as "to die for"
- Small, intimate space with only four small tables
- Buy one get one free on pastries after 5 PM
- Not suitable for laptop work (no laptop policy)

**Customer Reviews:**
- "Cozy, cute, and delicious little spot" - Julia Kennedy
- "Strong French vibes immediately upon entering" - Mario B
- "After 5pm pastries are BOGO!" - Lily Clark

## 3. Dear Coffee
**Rating:** 4.6/5 (331 reviews)
**Address:** 1246 Lexington Ave, New York, NY 10028
**Website:** https://www.dearcoffeenyc.com/
**Distance from Met:** Approximately 0.3 miles east

**Highlights:**
- Uses Onyx Coffee Lab beans (a premium coffee brand)
- Unique specialty drinks like Apricot Matcha
- Minimalist aesthetic typical of the Upper East Side
- Great for quick coffee to-go
- Note: Very limited seating and no restrooms available

**Customer Reviews:**
- "The apricot matcha was so good! Super refreshing" - Monica Moon Kim
- "One of the most unique and special coffee shops in the UES" - Kate N
- "Great quality drinks after a long walk in the park" - Jea Kim

## Summary
All three cafes offer excellent coffee experiences within walking distance of the Metropolitan Museum of Art. 787 Coffee stands out with its exceptional rating and unique rum-infused coffee options, making it ideal for those seeking something different. Café Blériot XI offers a charming French café experience with outstanding pastries, while Dear Coffee provides high-quality specialty drinks in a minimalist setting. Depending on your preferences for atmosphere, seating availability, and specialty offerings, any of these three would make an excellent choice for a coffee break during or after your visit to the Met.
