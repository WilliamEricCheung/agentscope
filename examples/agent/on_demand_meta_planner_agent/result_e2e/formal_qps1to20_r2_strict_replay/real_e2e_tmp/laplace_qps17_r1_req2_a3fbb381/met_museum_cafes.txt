# Cafes Near The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

## Top-Rated Cafes

### 1. 787 Coffee ⭐ 4.9/5 (3,472 reviews)
- **Address**: 228 E 80th St, New York, NY 10075
- **Phone**: (646) 850-0234
- **Website**: http://www.787coffee.com/
- **Currently Open**: No
- **Specialties**: Rum-infused coffee beans, unique swing seating by the window
- **Highlights**: Exceptional customer service, fast WiFi, dog-friendly, known for their rum and whiskey flavored coffees

### 2. Antonucci Cafe ⭐ 4.5/5 (428 reviews)
- **Address**: 170 E 81st St, New York, NY 10028
- **Phone**: (212) 570-5100
- **Website**: https://antonuccicafe81.com/
- **Price Level**: $$$ (3/4)
- **Currently Open**: No
- **Specialties**: Italian cuisine, Burrata salad with marinated tomatoes, fresh pasta
- **Highlights**: Cozy atmosphere with artwork on walls, family-run establishment, offers gluten-free pasta options

### 3. Tarallucci e Vino at Cooper Hewitt ⭐ 4.5/5 (30 reviews)
- **Address**: 9 E 90th St, New York, NY 10128 (Inside Cooper Hewitt Design Museum)
- **Phone**: (212) 849-8483
- **Website**: https://www.taralluccievino.net/cooper-hewitt-location
- **Price Level**: $$ (2/4)
- **Currently Open**: No
- **Specialties**: House-made pastries, wine selection
- **Highlights**: Located in the beautiful Cooper Hewitt Museum courtyard, excellent service (particularly from Fabian), outdoor seating available in good weather

### 4. Dear Coffee ⭐ 4.6/5 (330 reviews)
- **Address**: 1246 Lexington Ave, New York, NY 10028
- **Website**: https://www.dearcoffeenyc.com/
- **Currently Open**: No
- **Specialties**: Apricot matcha, drinks made with Onyx Coffee Lab beans
- **Highlights**: Minimalist Upper East Side cafe, artistic latte art (including dragon designs!), not suitable for laptop work due to small size

### 5. Café Blériot XI ⭐ 4.6/5 (310 reviews)
- **Address**: 226 E 83rd St, New York, NY 10028
- **Phone**: (646) 244-9539
- **Price Level**: $ (1/4)
- **Currently Open**: No
- **Specialties**: French pastries, ham and cheese croissants
- **Highlights**: Authentic French atmosphere, afternoon pastry BOGO (buy one get one) after 5pm, cozy corner seating
