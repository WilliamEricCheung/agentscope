# Top Cafes Near The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

The Metropolitan Museum of Art is located at 1000 5th Ave, New York, NY 10028 (coordinates: 40.7794366, -73.963244).

## 1. 787 Coffee ⭐ 4.9/5 (3,482 reviews)
**Address:** 228 E 80th St, New York, NY 10075
**Phone:** (646) 850-0234
**Website:** http://www.787coffee.com/
**Distance from Met:** Approximately 0.5 miles southeast

**Highlights:**
- Exceptionally high rating (4.9/5) with thousands of positive reviews
- Known for unique rum-infused coffee beans
- Cozy, neighborhood feel with friendly staff who remember regular customers
- Fast WiFi and comfortable seating
- Pet-friendly establishment
- Features a swing by the window for a unique experience

**Customer Feedback:**
Customers consistently praise the exceptional service, particularly mentioning staff members like Alex and Fox who create a welcoming atmosphere. The rum-infused coffee is a standout specialty, and many reviewers mention it as their favorite coffee shop in the area.

## 2. Antonucci Cafe ⭐ 4.5/5 (428 reviews)
**Address:** 170 E 81st St, New York, NY 10028
**Phone:** (212) 570-5100
**Website:** https://antonuccicafe81.com/
**Price Level:** $$$ (Higher-end)
**Distance from Met:** Approximately 0.4 miles southeast

**Highlights:**
- Authentic Italian café and restaurant
- Intimate setting with artwork on the walls
- Excellent Burrata salad with marinated tomatoes
- Perfectly cooked fresh pasta (including gluten-free options)
- Charming outdoor seating with adequate heating

**Customer Feedback:**
Reviewers appreciate the authentic Italian cuisine and cozy atmosphere. Some note that portions can be generous, while others mention that not all desserts are available during daytime hours. A few customers found the food somewhat bland, but most praise the quality and authenticity.

## 3. Tarallucci e Vino at Cooper Hewitt ⭐ 4.5/5 (30 reviews)
**Address:** 9 E 90th St, New York, NY 10128
**Phone:** (212) 849-8483
**Website:** https://www.taralluccievino.net/cooper-hewitt-location
**Price Level:** $$ (Moderate)
**Distance from Met:** Approximately 0.6 miles north

**Highlights:**
- Located inside the Cooper Hewitt Design Museum
- Beautiful courtyard seating when weather permits
- Excellent coffee and house-made pastries
- Outstanding service, particularly from staff member Fabian
- Wine selection available

**Customer Feedback:**
Customers consistently mention the exceptional service, especially from Fabian who appears to be a beloved staff member. The café offers a peaceful retreat with high-quality coffee and pastries. Limited seating can be an issue during busy times, and some note the lack of vegetarian options.
