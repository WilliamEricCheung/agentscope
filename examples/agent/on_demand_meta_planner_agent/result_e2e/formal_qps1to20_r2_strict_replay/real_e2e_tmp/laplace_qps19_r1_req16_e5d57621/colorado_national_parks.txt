# National Parks and Related Sites in Colorado

Based on the National Parks Service data, here are all 17 national parks and related sites located within the state of Colorado:

## National Parks (4)
1. **Black Canyon Of The Gunnison National Park** - Big enough to be overwhelming, yet still intimate enough to feel the pulse of time. Features some of the steepest cliffs, oldest rock, and craggiest spires in North America.

2. **Great Sand Dunes National Park & Preserve** - Home to the tallest dunes in North America, set against a diverse landscape of grasslands, wetlands, forests, alpine lakes, and tundra.

3. **Mesa Verde National Park** - Protects over 700 years of Ancestral Pueblo history with thriving communities built on mesas and in cliffs. A World Heritage Site and International Dark Sky Park.

4. **Rocky Mountain National Park** - Encompasses 415 square miles of spectacular mountain environments, from montane meadows to alpine lakes and towering peaks, with over 300 miles of hiking trails.

## National Monuments (4)
5. **Colorado National Monument** - Preserves grand landscapes of the American West with towering monoliths within vast plateaus and canyon panoramas.

6. **Dinosaur National Monument** - Features 150-million-year-old dinosaur fossils still embedded in rocks, along with mountains, desert, rivers, petroglyphs, and diverse wildlife.

7. **Florissant Fossil Beds National Monument** - Contains one of the world's richest and most diverse fossil deposits, including petrified redwood stumps up to 14 feet wide and thousands of detailed insect and plant fossils.

8. **Yucca House National Monument** - Preserves a large unexcavated pueblo in Montezuma Valley, offering visitors a sense of discovery while maintaining the site's integrity.

## National Historic Sites (3)
9. **Amache National Historic Site** - Commemorates the Granada Relocation Center, one of ten incarceration sites where over 10,000 Japanese Americans were unjustly incarcerated during World War II.

10. **Bent's Old Fort National Historic Site** - A reconstructed adobe trading post on the Santa Fe Trail where traders, trappers, travelers, and Native American tribes met for peaceful trade in the 1840s.

11. **Sand Creek Massacre National Historic Site** - Honors the memory of the approximately 750 Arapaho and Cheyenne people who were encamped when the tragic massacre occurred on November 29, 1864.

## National Recreation Area (1)
12. **Curecanti National Recreation Area** - Comprises three reservoirs and surrounding lands in western Colorado, offering year-round recreation on both land and water.

## National Historic Trails (5)
These trails pass through Colorado as part of their longer routes:

13. **California National Historic Trail** - Follows the route of over 250,000 emigrants who traveled to California during the 1840s-1850s gold rush era.

14. **Old Spanish National Historic Trail** - Traces the routes of mule pack trains between Santa Fe, New Mexico, and Los Angeles, California.

15. **Pony Express National Historic Trail** - Marks the historic relay system that carried mail from Missouri to California in just 10 days.

16. **Santa Fe National Historic Trail** - Follows the commercial highway between western Missouri and Santa Fe, New Mexico, used for freight transport.

17. **Hovenweep National Monument** - While primarily in Utah, this site also extends into Colorado and preserves six prehistoric sites built between 1200-1300 CE with multistory towers perched on canyon rims.

Note: Hovenweep National Monument is listed separately as it spans both Colorado and Utah, but is managed as a single unit by the National Park Service.
