# National Parks and Monuments in Colorado

Based on the National Parks Service data, here are all 17 national parks, monuments, historic sites, and trails located in Colorado:

## National Parks
1. **Black Canyon Of The Gunnison National Park** - Features some of the steepest cliffs, oldest rock, and craggiest spires in North America.
2. **Great Sand Dunes National Park & Preserve** - Home to the tallest dunes in North America, along with diverse landscapes including grasslands, wetlands, forests, alpine lakes, and tundra.
3. **Mesa Verde National Park** - Protects the rich cultural heritage of 27 Pueblos and Tribes, featuring Ancestral Pueblo cliff dwellings built over 700 years ago.
4. **Rocky Mountain National Park** - Encompasses 415 square miles of spectacular mountain environments, from meadows to alpine lakes and towering peaks.

## National Monuments
5. **Colorado National Monument** - Preserves grand landscapes with towering monoliths within vast plateaus and canyon panoramas.
6. **Curecanti National Recreation Area** - Three reservoirs and surrounding lands offering both natural and human-made features.
7. **Dinosaur National Monument** (shared with Utah) - Features embedded dinosaur fossils from 150 million years ago, along with mountains, desert, and rivers.
8. **Florissant Fossil Beds National Monument** - Contains one of the richest and most diverse fossil deposits in the world, including petrified redwood stumps.
9. **Hovenweep National Monument** (shared with Utah) - Preserves six prehistoric sites built between 1200-1300 CE, featuring multistory towers perched on canyon rims.
10. **Yucca House National Monument** - An unexcavated pueblo in Montezuma Valley, largely untouched since being protected in 1919.

## National Historic Sites
11. **Amache National Historic Site** - Formerly the Granada Relocation Center, one of ten incarceration sites for Japanese Americans during WWII.
12. **Bent's Old Fort National Historic Site** - A reconstructed adobe trading post on the Santa Fe Trail from the 1840s.
13. **Sand Creek Massacre National Historic Site** - Commemorates the tragic 1864 massacre of Cheyenne and Arapaho people.

## National Historic Trails (passing through Colorado)
14. **California National Historic Trail** - Follows the route of over 250,000 emigrants who traveled to California during the 1840s-1850s.
15. **Old Spanish National Historic Trail** - Follows routes of mule pack trains between Santa Fe, New Mexico, and Los Angeles, California.
16. **Pony Express National Historic Trail** - Marks the route where young men carried mail from Missouri to California in just 10 days.
17. **Santa Fe National Historic Trail** - Traces the commercial highway between western Missouri and Santa Fe, New Mexico.

This list includes all units of the National Park System that are either entirely within Colorado or pass through the state.
