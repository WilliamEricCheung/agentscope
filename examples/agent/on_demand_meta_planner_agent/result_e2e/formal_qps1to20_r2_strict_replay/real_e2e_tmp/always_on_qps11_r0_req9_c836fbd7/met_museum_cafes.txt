# Top 3 Cafes Near The Metropolitan Museum of Art

## 1. 787 Coffee
- **Rating**: 4.9/5 (3,472 reviews)
- **Address**: 228 E 80th St, New York, NY 10075
- **Phone**: (646) 850-0234
- **Website**: http://www.787coffee.com/
- **Currently Open**: Yes
- **Specialties**: Rum-infused coffee beans, unique drinks like rum and whiskey flavored coffees
- **Atmosphere**: Cozy neighborhood spot with friendly staff who remember regulars' names and orders
- **Notable Features**: Swing by the window for people-watching, fast WiFi, fresh baked pastries
- **Price Range**: Moderate to premium

## 2. Cabuc Coffee
- **Rating**: 4.6/5 (153 reviews)
- **Address**: 1211 Lexington Ave, New York, NY 10028
- **Phone**: (646) 891-0011
- **Currently Open**: Yes
- **Specialties**: Turkish coffee, Baklava Latte, Salted Honey Croissant
- **Atmosphere**: Bright, pleasant atmosphere with Turkish music
- **Notable Features**: Good for working (offers WiFi), spacious interior with bright lighting
- **Price Range**: Moderate

## 3. Caffe Aronne | Mobile espresso bar & coffee catering
- **Rating**: 4.5/5 (1,181 reviews)
- **Address**: 976A Lexington Ave, New York, NY 10021
- **Phone**: (917) 639-3590
- **Website**: https://www.caffearonne.com/mobile-espresso-coffe-bar-barista-catering
- **Currently Open**: Yes
- **Specialties**: Pistachio Cardamom Latte, Smoked Salmon Open-Faced Sandwich, American soft cookies
- **Atmosphere**: Cozy spot with limited seating (only two tables)
- **Notable Features**: Offers a variety of beverages from tea to wine, friendly owner and staff
- **Price Range**: Premium (small latte costs around $9.75 including tip)

All three cafes are currently open and within walking distance of the Metropolitan Museum of Art.
