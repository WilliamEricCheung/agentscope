RESEARCH REPORT: THE GREAT WAVE OFF KANAGAWA

ARTWORK INFORMATION:
- Full Title: Under the Wave off Kanagawa (Kanagawa oki nami ura), or The Great Wave, from the series Thirty-six Views of Mount Fuji (Fugaku sanjūrokkei)
- Artist: Katsushika Hokusai (Japanese, Tokyo/Edo 1760–1849 Tokyo/Edo)
- Medium: Woodblock print; ink and color on paper
- Dimensions: Approximately 10 x 15 inches (25.4 x 38.1 cm)
