# Cafes Near The Metropolitan Museum of Art

The Metropolitan Museum of Art is located at 1000 5th Ave, New York, NY 10028.

## Top Recommended Cafes

### 1. 787 Coffee ⭐ 4.9/5 (3,472 reviews)
- **Address**: 228 E 80th St, New York, NY 10075
- **Phone**: (646) 850-0234
- **Website**: http://www.787coffee.com/
- **Distance from Met**: ~0.5 miles southeast
- **Highlights**: 
  - Exceptionally high rating (4.9/5)
  - Known for unique rum-infused coffee beans
  - Friendly staff who remember regular customers
  - Pet-friendly (dogs welcome)
  - Fast WiFi
  - Swing seating by the window

### 2. Antonucci Cafe ⭐ 4.5/5 (428 reviews)
- **Address**: 170 E 81st St, New York, NY 10028
- **Phone**: (212) 570-5100
- **Website**: https://antonuccicafe81.com/
- **Price Level**: $$$
- **Distance from Met**: ~0.4 miles east
- **Highlights**:
  - Charming Italian restaurant/cafe
  - Excellent Burrata salad with marinated tomatoes
  - Perfectly cooked fettuccini with mushrooms
  - Cozy atmosphere with artwork on walls
  - Gluten-free pasta options available

### 3. Café Fledermaus ⭐ 4.2/5 (40 reviews)
- **Address**: 1048 5th Ave, New York, NY 10028
- **Phone**: (212) 288-0665
- **Website**: http://www.neuegalerie.org/cafes/fledermaus
- **Price Level**: $$$
- **Distance from Met**: ~0.2 miles north (in the Neue Galerie)
- **Highlights**:
  - Located in the Neue Galerie museum
  - European-style cafe in the basement
  - Delicious cakes with moderate sweetness
  - Excellent herring sandwich and hot mulled wine
  - Authentic European cuisine
