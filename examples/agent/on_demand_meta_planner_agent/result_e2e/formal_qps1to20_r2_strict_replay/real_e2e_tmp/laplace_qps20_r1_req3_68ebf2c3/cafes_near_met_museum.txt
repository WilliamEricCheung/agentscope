# Cafes Near The Metropolitan Museum of Art

The Metropolitan Museum of Art is located at 1000 5th Ave, New York, NY 10028 (coordinates: 40.7794366, -73.963244).

## Top Recommended Cafes

### 1. 787 Coffee ⭐ 4.9/5 (3,472 reviews)
**Address:** 228 E 80th St, New York, NY 10075
**Phone:** (646) 850-0234
**Website:** http://www.787coffee.com/
**Distance from MET:** Approximately 0.5 miles southeast

**Highlights:**
- Exceptionally high rating (4.9/5)
- Known for unique rum-infused coffee beans
- Cozy, neighborhood feel with friendly staff
- Fast WiFi and pet-friendly
- Features a swing by the window for people-watching
- Freshly baked pastries daily

**Notable Review:** "Alex made us feel right at home... The rum-infused coffee beans are so cool! Even the guy behind me said they taste amazing and he comes almost every day."

### 2. Antonucci Cafe ⭐ 4.5/5 (428 reviews)
**Address:** 170 E 81st St, New York, NY 10028
**Phone:** (212) 570-5100
**Website:** https://antonuccicafe81.com/
**Price Level:** $$$
**Distance from MET:** Approximately 0.4 miles southeast

**Highlights:**
- Charming Italian cafe/restaurant with intimate atmosphere
- Excellent Burrata salad with marinated tomatoes
- Perfectly cooked fettuccini with generous mushroom portions
- Family-owned establishment with artwork on the walls
- Note: Some desserts only available in the evening

**Notable Review:** "The Burrata salad was absolutely stunning... The sea bass was also very well done, though the portion was quite large for one person."

### 3. Dear Coffee ⭐ 4.6/5 (330 reviews)
**Address:** 1246 Lexington Ave, New York, NY 10028
**Website:** https://www.dearcoffeenyc.com/
**Distance from MET:** Approximately 0.4 miles east

**Highlights:**
- Uses Onyx Coffee Lab beans for espresso and pour-over
- Unique specialty drinks like Apricot Matcha
- Minimalist aesthetic in the Upper East Side style
- Great for quick coffee but limited seating
- Note: No restrooms available

**Notable Review:** "The apricot matcha was SO good! Super refreshing with just the right sweetness and the apricot flavor complemented the matcha perfectly without overpowering it."
