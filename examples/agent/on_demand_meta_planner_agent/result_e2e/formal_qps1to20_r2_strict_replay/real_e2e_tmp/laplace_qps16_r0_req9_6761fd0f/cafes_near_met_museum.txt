# Top Cafes Near The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Based on location data and customer reviews, here are the highest-rated cafes within 500 meters of the Metropolitan Museum of Art (1000 5th Ave, New York, NY 10028).

## 1. 787 Coffee ⭐ 4.9/5 (3,472 reviews)
**Address:** 228 E 80th St, New York, NY 10075  
**Phone:** (646) 850-0234  
**Website:** http://www.787coffee.com/

**Highlights:**
- Exceptionally high rating with nearly perfect scores
- Known for unique rum-infused coffee beans
- Pet-friendly establishment with a cozy atmosphere
- Fast WiFi and comfortable seating including a swing by the window
- Staff remembers regular customers by name
- Freshly baked pastries available all day

**Customer Feedback:** 
Customers consistently praise the friendly staff (particularly Alex and Fox), unique coffee flavors, and welcoming atmosphere. Many mention it feels like a neighborhood gathering spot despite being in Manhattan.

## 2. Dear Coffee ⭐ 4.6/5 (330 reviews)
**Address:** 1246 Lexington Ave, New York, NY 10028  
**Website:** https://www.dearcoffeenyc.com/

**Highlights:**
- Uses premium Onyx Coffee Lab beans for espresso and pour-over
- Creative specialty drinks like Apricot Matcha
- Minimalist aesthetic with quality-focused approach
- Convenient location on Lexington Avenue

**Customer Feedback:**
Reviewers appreciate the high-quality coffee and creative drink options. The cafe is noted as being small with limited seating and no restrooms, making it better for quick visits or takeout rather than extended stays.

## 3. Antonucci Cafe ⭐ 4.5/5 (428 reviews)
**Address:** 170 E 81st St, New York, NY 10028  
**Phone:** (212) 570-5100  
**Website:** https://antonuccicafe81.com/  
**Price Level:** $$$

**Highlights:**
- Italian cafe and restaurant with authentic cuisine
- Cozy, intimate atmosphere with artwork on the walls
- Excellent Burrata salad with marinated tomatoes
- Fresh pasta dishes including gluten-free options
- Full wine list available

**Customer Feedback:**
Patrons enjoy the authentic Italian food and warm atmosphere. Some note that not all desserts are available during daytime hours, and the portions can be generous. The cafe has a connection to Madonna, who reportedly dined there and gifted the restaurant her photo.

## 4. Tarallucci e Vino at Cooper Hewitt ⭐ 4.5/5 (30 reviews)
**Address:** 9 E 90th St, New York, NY 10128  
**Phone:** (212) 849-8483  
**Website:** https://www.taralluccievino.net/cooper-hewitt-location  
**Price Level:** $$

**Highlights:**
- Located inside the Cooper Hewitt Design Museum
- Outdoor seating available in the museum courtyard (weather permitting)
- Excellent coffee and wine selection
- House-made pastries and cookies
- Notable staff member Fabian receives consistent praise

**Customer Feedback:**
Visitors appreciate the charming location within the museum and the friendly service. Some limitations include few vegetarian options and limited seating that can be occupied by non-paying laptop users.

## Distance from The Met:
All these cafes are within a 5-10 minute walk from the Metropolitan Museum of Art, making them convenient options for a coffee break before or after your museum visit.
