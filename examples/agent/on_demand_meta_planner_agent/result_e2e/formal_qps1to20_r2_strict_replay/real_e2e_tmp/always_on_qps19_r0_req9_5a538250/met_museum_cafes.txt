# Top Cafes Near The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Based on proximity and ratings, here are the best cafe options within 500 meters of the Metropolitan Museum of Art (1000 5th Ave, New York, NY 10028).

## 1. 787 Coffee ⭐ 4.9/5 (3,482 reviews)
**Address:** 228 E 80th St, New York, NY 10075  
**Phone:** (646) 850-0234  
**Website:** http://www.787coffee.com/  
**Distance from Met:** Approximately 0.5 miles southeast

**Highlights:**
- Exceptionally high rating with nearly perfect scores
- Known for unique rum-infused coffee beans
- Pet-friendly establishment
- Fast WiFi and comfortable seating
- Swing seat by the window for people-watching
- Staff remembers regular customers by name

**Customer Feedback:**
Customers consistently praise the friendly staff (particularly mentioning baristas like Alex, Fox, and Isa), unique coffee flavors, and cozy atmosphere. Many reviewers mention it feels like a neighborhood gathering spot with excellent coffee and pastries.

## 2. Café Blériot XI ⭐ 4.6/5 (310 reviews)
**Address:** 226 E 83rd St, New York, NY 10028  
**Phone:** (646) 244-9539  
**Price Level:** $ (Inexpensive)  
**Distance from Met:** Approximately 0.4 miles east

**Highlights:**
- Charming French-inspired cafe
- Freshly baked pastries including ham & cheese croissants
- After 5 PM: Buy one pastry, get one free!
- No laptop policy (not suitable for remote work)
- Limited seating (only four small tables)

**Customer Feedback:**
Reviewers love the French atmosphere, delicious pastries, and attentive service. Some note that music can be loud for such a small space. The cafe is praised for its authentic espresso and specialty cold brew (though these sometimes sell out).

## 3. Antonucci Cafe ⭐ 4.5/5 (428 reviews)
**Address:** 170 E 81st St, New York, NY 10028  
**Phone:** (212) 570-5100  
**Website:** https://antonuccicafe81.com/  
**Price Level:** $$$ (Expensive)  
**Distance from Met:** Approximately 0.3 miles east

**Highlights:**
- Italian restaurant with cafe offerings
- Intimate setting with artwork on walls
- Excellent Burrata salad with marinated tomatoes
- Fresh pasta dishes available
- Gluten-free pasta options
- Madonna reportedly dined here and gifted them her photo

**Customer Feedback:**
Customers appreciate the authentic Italian cuisine and cozy atmosphere. Some note that the space is small and can be noisy. Dessert availability varies by time of day (some items only available in the evening). Portions are appropriate for individual dining.
