# Top 3 Cafes Near The Metropolitan Museum of Art

## 1. 787 Coffee
**Rating:** 4.9/5 (3,482 reviews)
**Address:** 228 E 80th St, New York, NY 10075
**Phone:** (646) 850-0234
**Website:** http://www.787coffee.com/
**Currently Open:** No

**Highlights:**
- Exceptionally high rating with thousands of positive reviews
- Known for unique rum-infused coffee beans
- Friendly staff who remember regular customers
- Cozy atmosphere with unique features like a swing by the window
- Fast WiFi and comfortable seating
- Pet-friendly establishment

**Popular Items:**
- Rum-infused cappuccino
- Matcha latte
- Freshly baked pastries throughout the day

## 2. Dear Coffee
**Rating:** 4.6/5 (331 reviews)
**Address:** 1246 Lexington Ave, New York, NY 10028
**Website:** https://www.dearcoffeenyc.com/
**Currently Open:** No

**Highlights:**
- Uses Onyx Coffee Lab beans (a premium coffee brand)
- Unique specialty drinks like apricot matcha
- Minimalist aesthetic typical of Upper East Side cafes
- Small space, better for quick visits than extended stays
- No restrooms available
- Great for takeout options

**Popular Items:**
- Apricot matcha (described as refreshing with perfect sweetness)
- Peach soda
- Cappuccino with artistic latte art (including dragon designs!)

## 3. Café Blériot XI
**Rating:** 4.6/5 (310 reviews)
**Address:** 226 E 83rd St, New York, NY 10028
**Phone:** (646) 244-9539
**Price Level:** $ (Inexpensive)
**Currently Open:** No

**Highlights:**
- French-inspired café with authentic pastries
- Cozy space with only four small tables
- No laptop policy (not designed for remote work)
- Buy-one-get-one-free pastries after 5 PM
- Freshly baked, fluffy pastries described as "finger-licking good"
- Warm, welcoming staff

**Popular Items:**
- Ham and cheese croissants
- Teddy bear financiers (almond cookies)
- Almond croissants
- Specialty cold brew coffee

All three cafes are within walking distance of the Metropolitan Museum of Art and offer unique experiences with excellent coffee and food options.
