ADDITIONAL SEARCH RESULTS:
The Met's collection includes 5,593 objects related to "Art Deco furniture," though many are from earlier decorative arts periods that influenced or were later categorized under the broader Art Deco aesthetic. The more focused search for pieces specifically from the 1920s-1940s period yielded 145 authentic Art Deco works.

The collection spans various departments including:
- European Sculpture and Decorative Arts
- Modern and Contemporary Art
- American Wing (for American Art Deco pieces)

Materials commonly found in authentic Art Deco furniture include:
- Exotic woods (mahogany, walnut, ebony)
- Metals (iron, chrome, brass)
- Lacquered surfaces
- Geometric inlays
- Bold geometric patterns
- Luxurious upholstery fabrics

To explore the complete collection, visit The Met's online collection database or visit the museum in person to see these iconic pieces of 20th-century design.
