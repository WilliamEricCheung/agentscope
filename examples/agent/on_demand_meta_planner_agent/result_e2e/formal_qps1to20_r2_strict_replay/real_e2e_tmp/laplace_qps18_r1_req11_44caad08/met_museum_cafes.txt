# Top 3 Cafes Near The Metropolitan Museum of Art

## 1. 787 Coffee
**Rating:** 4.9/5 (3,472 reviews)
**Address:** 228 E 80th St, New York, NY 10075
**Phone:** (646) 850-0234
**Website:** http://www.787coffee.com/

**Highlights:**
- Exceptionally high rating with thousands of positive reviews
- Known for unique rum-infused coffee beans
- Cozy, neighborhood feel with friendly staff who remember regulars
- Features a swing by the window for a unique seating experience
- Fast WiFi and comfortable atmosphere for working or relaxing
- Pet-friendly establishment

**Customer Feedback:**
Customers consistently praise the quality of coffee, particularly the rum-infused varieties, and the welcoming atmosphere. The staff, especially baristas like Alex, Fox, and Isa, are noted for their friendliness and knowledge. Many reviewers mention it as their favorite coffee shop in the area.

## 2. Cositas Café
**Rating:** 4.8/5 (184 reviews)
**Address:** 353 E 78th St, New York, NY 10075
**Phone:** (347) 673-1733
**Website:** https://www.cositascafenyc.com/
**Price Level:** $

**Highlights:**
- Authentic Latin American café with Colombian influence
- Freshly baked goods including empanadas, almojábanas, and buñuelos
- Excellent flan and smoothies
- Family-owned Latinx business
- Affordable pricing

**Customer Feedback:**
Reviewers with Latin American heritage particularly appreciate the authenticity of the food and drinks. The pastries and baked goods receive consistent praise for being fresh and delicious. Some customers note that certain specialty drinks like Colombian hot chocolate with cheese might be an acquired taste.

## 3. La Bomboniera
**Rating:** 4.7/5 (560 reviews)
**Address:** 1378 Lexington Ave, New York, NY 10128
**Phone:** (646) 370-6302
**Website:** http://labombonieranyc.com/

**Highlights:**
- Environmentally conscious café with zero single-use plastics
- Beautiful ambiance with both coffee shop and bar options
- Excellent hot chocolate with cream (cioccolata calda con panna)
- Delicious cannoli and other Italian pastries
- Popular date spot with cozy atmosphere

**Customer Feedback:**
Customers love the sustainable approach and beautiful interior. The hot chocolate and pastries receive particular praise. Most reviewers mention excellent service, though there are occasional complaints about service quality during busy periods. The café tends to get crowded in summer but is more manageable in winter.

---

All three cafés are within walking distance of the Metropolitan Museum of Art (approximately 0.5-0.7 miles away) and offer distinct experiences ranging from specialty coffee to Latin American cuisine to Italian pastries, all with excellent ratings and unique atmospheres.
