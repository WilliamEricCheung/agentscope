# National Parks and Monuments in Colorado

Based on the National Parks Service data, here are the 17 national parks, monuments, historic sites, and trails located in Colorado:

## National Parks
1. **Black Canyon Of The Gunnison National Park**
   - Description: Features some of the steepest cliffs, oldest rock, and craggiest spires in North America.
   - Activities: Astronomy, camping, climbing, fishing, hiking, horseback riding, kayaking, skiing, snowshoeing, wildlife watching
   - Website: https://www.nps.gov/blca/index.htm

2. **Great Sand Dunes National Park & Preserve**
   - Description: Home to the tallest dunes in North America, set against diverse landscapes of grasslands, wetlands, forests, alpine lakes, and tundra.
   - Activities: Sand sledding, camping, hiking, fishing, horseback riding, hunting (in preserve), swimming, wildlife watching
   - Website: https://www.nps.gov/grsa/index.htm

3. **Mesa Verde National Park**
   - Description: Protects the cultural heritage of 27 Pueblos and Tribes with over 700 years of Ancestral Pueblo communities built on mesas and in cliffs.
   - Activities: Scenic driving, astronomy, biking, camping, guided tours, hiking, skiing, snowshoeing, wildlife watching
   - Website: https://www.nps.gov/meve/index.htm

4. **Rocky Mountain National Park**
   - Description: Encompasses 415 square miles of spectacular mountain environments from meadows to alpine lakes and towering peaks.
   - Activities: Scenic driving, astronomy, camping, climbing, fishing, hiking, skiing, snowshoeing, wildlife watching
   - Website: https://www.nps.gov/romo/index.htm

## National Monuments
5. **Colorado National Monument**
   - Description: Preserves grand landscapes with towering monoliths within a vast plateau and canyon panorama.
   - Activities: Scenic driving, biking, camping, climbing, hiking, wildlife watching
   - Website: https://www.nps.gov/colm/index.htm

6. **Curecanti National Recreation Area**
   - Description: Three reservoirs and surrounding lands featuring both natural and human-made features.
   - Activities: Boating, camping, fishing, hiking, hunting, paddling, skiing, snowmobiling, swimming, wildlife watching
   - Website: https://www.nps.gov/cure/index.htm

7. **Dinosaur National Monument** (also in Utah)
   - Description: Contains dinosaur fossils embedded in rocks, mountains, desert, rivers, petroglyphs, and homesteader history.
   - Activities: Astronomy, biking, camping, fishing, guided tours, hiking, paddling, wildlife watching
   - Website: https://www.nps.gov/dino/index.htm

8. **Florissant Fossil Beds National Monument**
   - Description: Contains one of the world's richest and most diverse fossil deposits, including petrified redwood stumps and detailed insect fossils.
   - Activities: Astronomy, picnicking, guided tours, hiking, horseback riding, snowshoeing
   - Website: https://www.nps.gov/flfo/index.htm

9. **Hovenweep National Monument** (also in Utah)
   - Description: Preserves six prehistoric sites built between 1200-1300 CE, featuring multistory towers perched on canyon rims.
   - Activities: Astronomy, camping, hiking
   - Website: https://www.nps.gov/hove/index.htm

10. **Yucca House National Monument**
    - Description: Preserves a large unexcavated pueblo in Montezuma Valley between Mesa Verde and Ute Mountain.
    - Activities: Guided tours, self-guided walking tours
    - Website: https://www.nps.gov/yuho/index.htm

## National Historic Sites
11. **Amache National Historic Site**
    - Description: Former incarceration site for Japanese Americans during World War II.
    - Activities: Guided tours, self-guided auto tours, junior ranger program
    - Website: https://www.nps.gov/amch/index.htm

12. **Bent's Old Fort National Historic Site**
    - Description: Reconstructed adobe trading post on the Santa Fe Trail where traders, trappers, travelers, and Native tribes met.
    - Activities: Arts and culture, craft demonstrations, guided tours, hiking, living history, wildlife watching
    - Website: https://www.nps.gov/beol/index.htm

13. **Sand Creek Massacre National Historic Site**
    - Description: Commemorates the tragic 1864 massacre of Cheyenne and Arapaho people.
    - Activities: Guided tours, hiking, wildlife watching
    - Website: https://www.nps.gov/sand/index.htm

## National Historic Trails (passing through Colorado)
14. **California National Historic Trail**
    - Description: Follows the route of over 250,000 emigrants who traveled to California during the 1840s-1850s.
    - Activities: Scenic driving, guided tours, hiking, horseback riding
    - Website: https://www.nps.gov/cali/index.htm

15. **Old Spanish National Historic Trail**
    - Description: Follows routes of mule pack trains between Santa Fe, New Mexico, and Los Angeles, California.
    - Activities: Arts and culture, scenic driving, hiking, horse trekking
    - Website: https://www.nps.gov/olsp/index.htm

16. **Pony Express National Historic Trail**
    - Description: Commemorates the relay mail system that carried mail from Missouri to California in just 10 days.
    - Activities: Scenic driving, guided tours, hiking, horseback riding, living history
    - Website: https://www.nps.gov/poex/index.htm

17. **Santa Fe National Historic Trail**
    - Description: Follows the route of freight wagons carrying cargo between western Missouri and Santa Fe, New Mexico.
    - Activities: Arts and culture, scenic driving, guided tours, hiking
    - Website: https://www.nps.gov/safe/index.htm
