ADDITIONAL NOTABLE DESIGNERS REPRESENTED:
The collection includes works by other significant Art Deco designers and manufacturers, though some initial search results included earlier period pieces that were misclassified or broadly categorized.

The Metropolitan Museum's Art Deco furniture collection spans the full range of the movement's characteristics:
- Geometric forms and streamlined shapes
- Use of luxurious materials (lacquer, exotic woods, metals)
- Integration of industrial materials with fine craftsmanship
- International influences from the 1920s-1940s period
