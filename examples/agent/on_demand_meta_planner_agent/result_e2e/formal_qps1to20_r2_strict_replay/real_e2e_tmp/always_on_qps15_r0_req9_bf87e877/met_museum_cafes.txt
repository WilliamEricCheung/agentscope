# Top 3 Cafes Near The Metropolitan Museum of Art

## 1. 787 Coffee (Rating: 4.9/5)
**Address:** 228 E 80th St, New York, NY 10075
**Phone:** (646) 850-0234
**Website:** http://www.787coffee.com/

**Description:**
787 Coffee is an exceptional coffee shop with an outstanding 4.9-star rating from over 3,400 reviews. Located just a short walk from the Met, this cozy neighborhood spot is known for its unique rum-infused and whiskey-flavored coffee beans. The cafe features a charming swing by the window where you can enjoy your drink while watching the street scene.

**Highlights:**
- Specialty rum and whiskey-infused coffee beans
- Freshly baked pastries throughout the day
- Friendly baristas who remember regular customers
- Fast WiFi
- Pet-friendly atmosphere
- Unique serving containers for cold drinks (similar to Capri-Sun pouches)

**Customer Reviews:**
Customers consistently praise the exceptional quality of coffee, particularly the rum-infused cappuccino and matcha latte. The staff, especially baristas like Alex, Fox, and Isa, are noted for their warmth and knowledge about the specialty coffee offerings.

## 2. Cositas Café (Rating: 4.8/5)
**Address:** 353 E 78th St, New York, NY 10075
**Phone:** (347) 673-1733
**Website:** https://www.cositascafenyc.com/
**Price Level:** $

**Description:**
Cositas Café is a charming Latin American coffee shop offering authentic Colombian and Latin American treats. With a stellar 4.8-star rating, this mini cafe specializes in traditional pastries and desserts that transport visitors straight to Latin America.

**Highlights:**
- Authentic Latin American pastries including empanadas, almojábanas, and buñuelos
- Excellent flan and smoothies
- Traditional Colombian hot chocolate (served with cheese)
- Latin-owned business with warm, friendly service
- Affordable pricing

**Customer Reviews:**
Visitors with Latin American heritage particularly appreciate the authenticity of the offerings. The baked goods receive consistent praise for being fresh and delicious. While most reviews are extremely positive, one customer noted that the Colombian hot chocolate with cheese might not be to everyone's taste.

## 3. La Bomboniera (Rating: 4.7/5)
**Address:** 1378 Lexington Ave, New York, NY 10128
**Phone:** (646) 370-6302
**Website:** http://labombonieranyc.com/

**Description:**
La Bomboniera is a beautiful, eco-conscious cafe with a 4.7-star rating that offers excellent coffee, hot chocolate, and Italian pastries. The establishment prides itself on sustainability, using no single-use plastics or disposable products.

**Highlights:**
- Environmentally sustainable practices (no single-use plastics)
- Delicious hot chocolate with cream (cioccolata calda con panna)
- Italian pastries including biscotti and lemon peel cake
- Bar available for evening drinks
- Cozy, inviting atmosphere

**Customer Reviews:**
Customers rave about the hot chocolate and Italian pastries. The cafe is noted for its pleasant ambiance, making it perfect for dates or meeting friends. Most reviewers praise the friendly and efficient service, though one mentioned inconsistent service quality during a particular visit. The eco-friendly approach is highly appreciated by environmentally conscious patrons.

---

All three cafes are within walking distance of the Metropolitan Museum of Art, making them perfect spots to relax before or after exploring the museum's extensive collections.
