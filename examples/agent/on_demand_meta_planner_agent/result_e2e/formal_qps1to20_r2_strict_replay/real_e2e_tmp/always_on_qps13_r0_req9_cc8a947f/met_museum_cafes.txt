# Top 3 Cafes Near The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

## 1. 787 Coffee
**Rating:** 4.9/5 (3,477 reviews)
**Address:** 228 E 80th St, New York, NY 10075
**Phone:** (646) 850-0234
**Website:** http://www.787coffee.com/
**Distance from Met:** Approximately 0.5 miles (8-minute walk)

**Highlights:**
- Exceptionally high rating with consistently positive reviews
- Known for unique rum-infused coffee beans
- Friendly staff who remember regular customers
- Cozy atmosphere with a swing by the window
- Fast WiFi available
- Pet-friendly establishment
- Freshly baked pastries throughout the day

**Customer Feedback:**
Customers consistently praise the quality of coffee, particularly the rum-infused varieties. The staff (especially Alex, Fox, and Isa) are noted for their exceptional friendliness and hospitality. Many reviewers mention the cozy, neighborhood feel of the cafe.

## 2. La Bomboniera
**Rating:** 4.7/5 (560 reviews)
**Address:** 1378 Lexington Ave, New York, NY 10128
**Phone:** (646) 370-6302
**Website:** http://labombonieranyc.com/
**Distance from Met:** Approximately 0.6 miles (12-minute walk)

**Highlights:**
- Environmentally conscious cafe with no single-use plastics
- Encourages reusable cups for a more intimate experience
- Excellent hot chocolate with cream (cioccolata calda con panna)
- Beautiful Italian pastries and desserts
- Bar service available in addition to coffee
- Cozy atmosphere, though can get crowded during peak times

**Customer Feedback:**
Reviewers appreciate the cafe's commitment to sustainability and zero-waste practices. The hot chocolate and pastries receive consistent praise. Some customers noted that service quality can be inconsistent, with one reviewer mentioning poor service despite excellent food.

## 3. Café Blériot XI
**Rating:** 4.6/5 (310 reviews)
**Address:** 226 E 83rd St, New York, NY 10028
**Phone:** (646) 244-9539
**Price Level:** $ (Affordable)
**Distance from Met:** Approximately 0.4 miles (8-minute walk)

**Highlights:**
- Authentic French atmosphere
- Freshly baked pastries including ham and cheese croissants
- Specialty cold brew coffee and canelés
- Charming small space with only four tables
- Buy-one-get-one-free pastries after 5 PM
- No laptop policy (not suitable for remote work)

**Customer Feedback:**
Customers love the French ambiance and high-quality pastries. The ham and cheese croissants are particularly popular. One minor complaint mentioned music being too loud for the small space. Note that some specialty items like cold brew and canelés may sell out.

## Summary
All three cafes offer excellent coffee and pastries within walking distance of the Metropolitan Museum of Art. 787 Coffee stands out for its exceptional rating and unique rum-infused coffee options. La Bomboniera offers an eco-friendly experience with Italian influences, while Café Blériot XI provides an authentic French cafe experience at an affordable price point.
