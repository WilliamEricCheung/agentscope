ADDITIONAL INFORMATION:
The Metropolitan Museum has an extensive collection of furniture that includes both authentic Art Deco pieces from the 1920s-1940s period as well as earlier decorative arts that may be catalogued under broader "Art Deco" search terms. The collection spans various departments including European Sculpture and Decorative Arts and Modern and Contemporary Art.

For the most authentic Art Deco examples, focus on pieces from the Modern and Contemporary Art department with artists active during 1920-1940, such as Edgar Brandt, Jacques-Émile Ruhlmann, Eileen Gray, and other recognized Art Deco designers.
