# Top Cafes Near The Metropolitan Museum of Art

Based on location data and reviews, here are the best cafe options within 500 meters of the Metropolitan Museum of Art (1000 5th Ave, New York, NY 10028).

## Currently Open Cafes

### 1. Cabuc Coffee ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ (4.6/5)
**Address:** 1211 Lexington Ave, New York, NY 10028  
**Phone:** (646) 891-0011  
**Distance from Met:** ~0.3 miles southeast

**Highlights:**
- Highest rated cafe in the area with excellent reviews
- Known for specialty coffee including Turkish coffee and unique Baklava Latte
- Friendly staff and cozy atmosphere with Turkish music
- Offers Wi-Fi, making it suitable for working
- Bright, pleasant ambiance

**Customer Feedback:**
"Amazing coffee shop with friendly staff. Great place to work with Wi-Fi, bright space, and delicious coffee." - Victorandmanuela

"The baklava latte and salty honey croissant are absolutely amazing!" - Tiffany Miller

### 2. Starbucks ⭐⭐⭐⭐ (4.1/5)
**Address:** 1142 Madison Ave, New York, NY 10028  
**Phone:** (212) 288-1506  
**Distance from Met:** ~0.2 miles east

**Highlights:**
- Convenient familiar option with consistent quality
- App ordering available
- Has restrooms (noted as important by visitors)
- Comfortable seating area

**Customer Feedback:**
"A good place to rest after walking around Central Park. Important: they have bathrooms!" - hhehey

Note: Some customers report occasional long wait times during peak hours.

**Phone:** (212) 327-4900

## Recommendation

For the best experience, **Cabuc Coffee** stands out as the top choice with its exceptional rating, unique menu offerings, and welcoming atmosphere. It's slightly further from the museum than the Madison Avenue options but well worth the short walk for coffee enthusiasts.

For convenience and familiarity, the **Starbucks** on Madison Avenue provides reliable service right near the museum entrance.

If you're looking for a meal rather than just coffee, **Le Pain Quotidien** offers a more substantial menu, though be prepared for potentially higher prices and smaller portions.
