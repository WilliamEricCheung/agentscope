# Top Cafes Near The Metropolitan Museum of Art

Based on a search within 500 meters of the Metropolitan Museum of Art (1000 5th Ave, New York, NY 10028), here are the highest-rated cafe options with detailed information:

## 1. 787 Coffee ⭐ 4.9/5 (3,472 reviews)
**Address:** 228 E 80th St, New York, NY 10075
**Phone:** (646) 850-0234
**Website:** http://www.787coffee.com/
**Currently Open:** No

**Highlights:**
- Exceptionally high rating of 4.9/5
- Known for unique rum-infused coffee beans
- Pet-friendly establishment
- Fast WiFi and comfortable seating
- Features a swing by the window for a unique experience
- Staff remembers regular customers' names and preferences

**Customer Feedback:**
Customers consistently praise the friendly staff (particularly Alex, Fox, and Isa), unique coffee flavors, and cozy atmosphere. Many reviewers mention it feels like a neighborhood gathering spot with excellent coffee and pastries.

## 2. Café Blériot XI ⭐ 4.6/5 (310 reviews)
**Address:** 226 E 83rd St, New York, NY 10028
**Phone:** (646) 244-9539
**Price Level:** $ (Inexpensive)
**Currently Open:** No

**Highlights:**
- French-inspired cafe with authentic atmosphere
- Freshly baked pastries including ham & cheese croissants
- Limited seating (only four small tables)
- Pastries are buy-one-get-one after 5 PM
- No laptop use allowed (not designed as a work cafe)

**Customer Feedback:**
Reviewers love the French ambiance, delicious pastries, and friendly service. Some note that the music can be too loud for the small space. The cafe is praised for its quality espresso and specialty cold brew (when available).

## 3. Tarallucci e Vino at Cooper Hewitt ⭐ 4.5/5 (30 reviews)
**Address:** 9 E 90th St, New York, NY 10128 (Inside Cooper Hewitt Design Museum)
**Phone:** (212) 849-8483
**Website:** https://www.taralluccievino.net/cooper-hewitt-location
**Price Level:** $$ (Moderate)
**Currently Open:** No

**Highlights:**
- Located inside the beautiful Cooper Hewitt Design Museum
- Outdoor seating available in the museum courtyard (weather permitting)
- Excellent coffee and wine selection
- Outstanding pastries and cookies
- Notable staff member: Fabian (consistently mentioned in positive reviews)

**Customer Feedback:**
Guests appreciate the charming location, quality beverages, and attentive service. Limited vegetarian options and seating availability are mentioned as minor drawbacks. The museum setting provides a unique cafe experience.

## 4. Antonucci Cafe ⭐ 4.5/5 (428 reviews)
**Address:** 170 E 81st St, New York, NY 10028
**Phone:** (212) 570-5100
**Website:** https://antonuccicafe81.com/
**Price Level:** $$$ (Expensive)
**Currently Open:** No

**Highlights:**
- Authentic Italian restaurant/cafe with intimate atmosphere
- Art-filled walls creating a warm, private ambiance
- Excellent Burrata salad with marinated tomatoes
- Perfectly cooked fresh pasta (including gluten-free options)
- Homemade desserts (selection varies by time of day)

**Customer Feedback:**
Diners praise the authentic Italian cuisine, particularly the Burrata salad, sea bass, and handmade pasta. Some note that portions can be large and certain desserts are only available in the evening. A few reviews mention inconsistent seasoning in some dishes.

## Summary
All four top-rated cafes are currently closed at the time of this search. 787 Coffee stands out with its exceptional 4.9/5 rating and unique rum-infused coffee offerings. For a French experience, Café Blériot XI offers authentic pastries at an affordable price point. Tarallucci e Vino provides a museum cafe experience with outdoor seating, while Antonucci Cafe delivers a more upscale Italian dining option with higher prices but quality food.
