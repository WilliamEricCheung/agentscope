# Top-Rated Cafes Near The Metropolitan Museum of Art

Based on our search within an 800-meter (10-minute walk) radius of the Metropolitan Museum of Art (1000 5th Ave, New York, NY 10028), here are the best cafe options:

## Currently Open Cafes

### 1. Cabuc Coffee ⭐ 4.6/5 (153 reviews)
**Address:** 1211 Lexington Ave, New York, NY 10028  
**Phone:** (646) 891-0011  
**Status:** Currently open

**Highlights:**
- Highly rated for their Turkish coffee and unique drinks like baklava lattes
- Friendly staff and pleasant atmosphere with Turkish music
- Offers Wi-Fi, making it suitable for working
- Bright, inviting space with ceramic cups for in-house use
- Some reviewers note limited pastry selection

### 2. Uptown Cafe and Pastries ⭐ 4.5/5 (85 reviews)
**Address:** 100 E 96th St, New York, NY 10128  
**Phone:** (646) 410-0132  
**Status:** Currently open

**Highlights:**
- Modern yet cozy space with plenty of seating
- Seasonal specialty drinks like cookie butter latte and lavender matcha
- Serves drinks in mugs rather than paper cups
- Fresh pastries including honey pecan croissants and chocolate brownies
- Clean, comfortable environment ideal for relaxing or working

### 3. Starbucks ⭐ 4.1/5 (927 reviews)

**Phone:** (212) 288-1506  
**Status:** Currently open

**Highlights:**
