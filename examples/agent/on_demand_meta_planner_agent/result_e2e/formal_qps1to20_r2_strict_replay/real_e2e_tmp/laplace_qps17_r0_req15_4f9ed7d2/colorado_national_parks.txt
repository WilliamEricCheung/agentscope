# National Parks and Sites in Colorado

Based on the National Park Service data, there are 17 national park sites located in Colorado. These include national parks, national monuments, national historic sites, and national historic trails that pass through the state.

## National Parks (3)
1. **Black Canyon Of The Gunnison National Park**
   - Description: Features some of the steepest cliffs, oldest rock, and craggiest spires in North America, carved by the Gunnison River over two million years.
   - Location: Western Colorado
   - Activities: Camping, rock climbing, fishing, hiking, wildlife watching, stargazing

2. **Great Sand Dunes National Park & Preserve**
   - Description: Home to the tallest dunes in North America, set against a diverse landscape of grasslands, wetlands, forests, alpine lakes, and tundra.
   - Location: Southern Colorado
   - Activities: Sand sledding, hiking, camping, wildlife watching, stargazing (International Dark Sky Park)

3. **Mesa Verde National Park**
   - Description: Preserves the cultural heritage of 27 Pueblos and Tribes, featuring over 700 years of Ancestral Pueblo communities built on mesas and in cliffs.
   - Location: Southwestern Colorado
   - Activities: Guided tours of cliff dwellings, hiking, museum exhibits, stargazing (International Dark Sky Park)

4. **Rocky Mountain National Park**
   - Description: Encompasses 415 square miles of spectacular mountain environments, from montane meadows to alpine lakes and towering peaks.
   - Location: Northern Colorado
   - Activities: Hiking (300+ miles of trails), wildlife viewing, camping, fishing, scenic drives

## National Monuments (4)
5. **Colorado National Monument**
   - Description: Preserves dramatic red rock canyons and towering monoliths within a vast plateau landscape.
   - Location: Western Colorado near Grand Junction
   - Activities: Scenic driving (Rim Rock Drive), hiking, rock climbing, wildlife watching

6. **Curecanti National Recreation Area**
   - Description: Three reservoirs and surrounding lands featuring both natural and human-made features.
   - Location: Western Colorado
   - Activities: Boating, fishing, camping, hiking, water sports

7. **Dinosaur National Monument** (shared with Utah)
   - Description: Contains 150-million-year-old dinosaur fossils embedded in rocks, along with mountains, desert, and river canyons.
   - Location: Northwestern Colorado
   - Activities: Fossil viewing, hiking, river rafting, camping, wildlife watching

8. **Florissant Fossil Beds National Monument**
   - Description: One of the richest and most diverse fossil deposits in the world, featuring petrified redwood stumps and detailed insect fossils.
   - Location: Central Colorado
   - Activities: Fossil viewing, hiking, museum exhibits

9. **Hovenweep National Monument** (shared with Utah)
   - Description: Preserves six prehistoric sites built between 1200-1300 CE, including multistory towers perched on canyon rims.
   - Location: Southwestern Colorado
   - Activities: Hiking, stargazing (International Dark Sky Park)

10. **Yucca House National Monument**
    - Description: A large unexcavated pueblo in Montezuma Valley, largely untouched since its protection in 1919.
    - Location: Southwestern Colorado
    - Activities: Self-guided tours

## National Historic Sites (3)
11. **Amache National Historic Site**
    - Description: Former incarceration site for Japanese Americans during World War II, where over 10,000 people were unjustly incarcerated from 1942-1945.
    - Location: Southeastern Colorado
    - Activities: Self-guided tours, historical interpretation

12. **Bent's Old Fort National Historic Site**
    - Description: A reconstructed adobe trading post on the Santa Fe Trail from the 1840s, where traders, trappers, travelers, and Native American tribes met for trade.
    - Location: Southeastern Colorado
    - Activities: Living history demonstrations, guided tours, museum exhibits

13. **Sand Creek Massacre National Historic Site**
    - Description: Commemorates the tragic 1864 massacre of Cheyenne and Arapaho people by U.S. forces.
    - Location: Southeastern Colorado
    - Activities: Guided tours, hiking, historical interpretation

## National Historic Trails (4)
These trails pass through Colorado as part of their longer routes:

14. **California National Historic Trail**
    - Description: Follows the route of over 250,000 emigrants who traveled to California during the 1840s-1850s.
    - Passes through: Eastern Colorado

15. **Old Spanish National Historic Trail**
    - Description: Follows the routes of mule pack trains between Santa Fe, New Mexico, and Los Angeles, California.
    - Passes through: Southern Colorado

16. **Pony Express National Historic Trail**
    - Description: Marks the relay system used to carry mail from Missouri to California in just 10 days.
    - Passes through: Northeastern Colorado

17. **Santa Fe National Historic Trail**
    - Description: Follows the trade route between western Missouri and Santa Fe, New Mexico.
    - Passes through: Southeastern Colorado
