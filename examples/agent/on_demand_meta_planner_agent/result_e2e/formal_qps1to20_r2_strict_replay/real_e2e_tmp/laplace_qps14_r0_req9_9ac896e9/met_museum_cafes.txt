# Top 3 Cafes Near The Metropolitan Museum of Art

## 1. Dear Coffee
- **Address**: 1246 Lexington Ave, New York, NY 10028
- **Rating**: 4.6/5 (330 reviews)
- **Website**: https://www.dearcoffeenyc.com/
- **Current Status**: Closed
- **Highlights**: 
  - Uses Onyx Coffee Lab beans for espresso and pour-over coffee
  - Known for unique drinks like apricot matcha
  - Minimalist style cafe in the Upper East Side
  - Limited seating (mostly counter seating)
  - No restroom available
  - Great for quick takeaway coffee

## 2. Cabuc Coffee
- **Address**: 1211 Lexington Ave, New York, NY 10028
- **Rating**: 4.6/5 (153 reviews)
- **Phone**: (646) 891-0011
- **Current Status**: Closed
- **Highlights**:
  - Turkish-inspired coffee shop with unique offerings like baklava latte
  - Friendly staff and bright atmosphere
  - Offers Wi-Fi, suitable for working
  - Features Turkish music creating a distinctive ambiance
  - Spacious interior with good lighting
  - Unique menu items including savory honey croissant

## 3. Antonucci Cafe
- **Address**: 170 E 81st St, New York, NY 10028
- **Rating**: 4.5/5 (428 reviews)
- **Phone**: (212) 570-5100
- **Website**: https://antonuccicafe81.com/
- **Price Level**: $$$ (Moderately expensive)
- **Current Status**: Closed
- **Highlights**:
  - Charming Italian restaurant/cafe with intimate atmosphere
  - Walls adorned with artwork
  - Excellent Burrata salad with marinated tomatoes
  - Perfectly cooked fettuccini with generous mushroom portions
  - Gluten-free pasta options available
  - Some desserts only available during dinner service
  - Madonna reportedly dined here and gifted them her photo

All three establishments are within a 5-10 minute walk from The Metropolitan Museum of Art, making them convenient options for visitors looking to enjoy a coffee or light meal before or after their museum visit.
