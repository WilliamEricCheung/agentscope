# National Parks and Monuments in Colorado

Based on the National Parks Service data, here are all 17 national parks, monuments, historic sites, and trails located in Colorado:

## National Parks
1. **Black Canyon Of The Gunnison National Park** - Features some of the steepest cliffs, oldest rock, and craggiest spires in North America.
2. **Great Sand Dunes National Park & Preserve** - Home to the tallest dunes in North America, along with grasslands, wetlands, forests, alpine lakes, and tundra.
3. **Mesa Verde National Park** - Protects the cultural heritage of 27 Pueblos and Tribes with over 700 years of Ancestral Pueblo history.
4. **Rocky Mountain National Park** - Encompasses 415 square miles of mountain environments with over 300 miles of hiking trails.

## National Monuments
5. **Colorado National Monument** - Preserves towering monoliths within a vast plateau and canyon panorama.
6. **Curecanti National Recreation Area** - Three reservoirs and surrounding lands with natural and human-made features.
7. **Dinosaur National Monument** - Contains embedded dinosaur fossils from 150 million years ago (shared with Utah).
8. **Florissant Fossil Beds National Monument** - One of the richest and most diverse fossil deposits in the world.
9. **Hovenweep National Monument** - Preserves six prehistoric sites built between 1200-1300 CE (shared with Utah).
10. **Yucca House National Monument** - A large unexcavated pueblo in Montezuma Valley.

## National Historic Sites
11. **Amache National Historic Site** - Former Japanese American incarceration site during World War II.
12. **Bent's Old Fort National Historic Site** - Adobe trading post on the Santa Fe Trail from the 1840s.
13. **Sand Creek Massacre National Historic Site** - Commemorates the tragic 1864 massacre of Cheyenne and Arapaho people.

## National Historic Trails (portions in Colorado)
14. **California National Historic Trail** - Follows the route of over 250,000 emigrants who traveled to California during the 1840s-1850s.
15. **Old Spanish National Historic Trail** - Route of mule pack trains between Santa Fe, New Mexico, and Los Angeles, California.
16. **Pony Express National Historic Trail** - The relay mail system that carried mail from Missouri to California in just 10 days.
17. **Santa Fe National Historic Trail** - Freight wagon route between western Missouri and Santa Fe, New Mexico.

This list includes all units of the National Park Service that are either entirely within Colorado or have significant portions within the state.
