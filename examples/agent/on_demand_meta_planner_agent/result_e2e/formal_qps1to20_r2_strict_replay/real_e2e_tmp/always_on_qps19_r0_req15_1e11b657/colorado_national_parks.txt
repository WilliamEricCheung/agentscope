# National Parks and Monuments in Colorado

Based on the National Parks Service data, here are all 17 national parks, monuments, historic sites, and trails located in Colorado:

1. **Amache National Historic Site**
   - Designation: National Historic Site
   - Description: Former incarceration site for Japanese Americans during World War II
   - Location: Near Granada, Colorado

2. **Bent's Old Fort National Historic Site**
   - Designation: National Historic Site
   - Description: Adobe trading post on the Santa Fe Trail from the 1840s
   - Location: Along the Arkansas River

3. **Black Canyon Of The Gunnison National Park**
   - Designation: National Park
   - Description: Features some of the steepest cliffs, oldest rock, and craggiest spires in North America
   - Location: Western Colorado

4. **California National Historic Trail**
   - Designation: National Historic Trail
   - Description: Over 5,000 miles long trail that passes through portions of 10 states including Colorado
   - Note: Only a portion of this trail runs through Colorado

5. **Colorado National Monument**
   - Designation: National Monument
   - Description: Preserves towering monoliths within a vast plateau and canyon panorama
   - Location: Near Grand Junction

6. **Curecanti National Recreation Area**
   - Designation: National Recreation Area
   - Description: Three reservoirs and surrounding lands in western Colorado
   - Features: Blue Mesa, Morrow Point, and Crystal Reservoirs

7. **Dinosaur National Monument**
   - Designation: National Monument
   - Description: Contains dinosaur fossils embedded in rocks, mountains, desert, and rivers
   - Note: Spans both Colorado and Utah

8. **Florissant Fossil Beds National Monument**
   - Designation: National Monument
   - Description: One of the richest and most diverse fossil deposits in the world
   - Features: Petrified redwood stumps and detailed fossils of insects and plants

9. **Great Sand Dunes National Park & Preserve**
   - Designation: National Park & Preserve
   - Description: Home to the tallest dunes in North America
   - Features: Diverse landscape of grasslands, wetlands, forests, alpine lakes, and tundra

10. **Hovenweep National Monument**
    - Designation: National Monument
    - Description: Preserves six prehistoric sites built between 1200 and 1300 CE
    - Note: Spans both Colorado and Utah

11. **Mesa Verde National Park**
    - Designation: National Park
    - Description: Protects Ancestral Pueblo communities built on mesas and in cliffs
    - Features: Over 700 years of cultural heritage, World Heritage Site

12. **Old Spanish National Historic Trail**
    - Designation: National Historic Trail
    - Description: Route of mule pack trains across the Southwest between Santa Fe and Los Angeles
    - Note: Only a portion of this trail runs through Colorado

13. **Pony Express National Historic Trail**
    - Designation: National Historic Trail
    - Description: Relay system for mail delivery from Missouri to California
    - Note: Only a portion of this trail runs through Colorado

14. **Rocky Mountain National Park**
    - Designation: National Park
    - Description: 415 square miles of mountain environments with over 300 miles of hiking trails
    - Features: Mountain peaks, alpine lakes, and abundant wildlife

15. **Sand Creek Massacre National Historic Site**
    - Designation: National Historic Site
    - Description: Commemorates the tragic 1864 massacre of Cheyenne and Arapaho people
    - Location: Southeastern Colorado

16. **Santa Fe National Historic Trail**
    - Designation: National Historic Trail
    - Description: Trade route between western Missouri and Santa Fe, New Mexico
    - Note: Only a portion of this trail runs through Colorado

17. **Yucca House National Monument**
    - Designation: National Monument
    - Description: Large unexcavated pueblo in Montezuma Valley
    - Note: Located between Mesa Verde and Ute Mountain

Note: Some of these sites are shared with other states, particularly the National Historic Trails which span multiple states.
