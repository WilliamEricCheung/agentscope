# Top 3 Cafes Near The Metropolitan Museum of Art

## 1. Cabuc Coffee
- **Distance from Met Museum**: 0.5 km (8-minute walk)
- **Rating**: 4.6/5 (153 reviews)
- **Address**: 1211 Lexington Ave, New York, NY 10028
- **Phone**: (646) 891-0011
- **Currently Open**: Yes
- **Highlights**: 
  - Known for excellent Turkish coffee and unique drinks like Baklava Latte
  - Friendly staff and bright, pleasant atmosphere
  - Offers Wi-Fi, making it suitable for working
  - Serves pastries including savory honey croissants

## 2. Cositas Café
- **Distance from Met Museum**: 1.4 km (19-minute walk)
- **Rating**: 4.8/5 (184 reviews)
- **Address**: 353 E 78th St, New York, NY 10075
- **Phone**: (347) 673-1733
- **Website**: https://www.cositascafenyc.com/
- **Currently Open**: No
- **Price Level**: $
- **Highlights**:
  - Latin American café with authentic pastries and desserts
  - Famous for empanadas, almojábanas, and buñuelos
  - Excellent flan and smoothies
  - Family-owned Latinx business

## 3. Uptown Cafe and Pastries
- **Distance from Met Museum**: 1.5 km (21-minute walk)
- **Rating**: 4.5/5 (85 reviews)
- **Address**: 100 E 96th St, New York, NY 10128
- **Phone**: (646) 410-0132
- **Currently Open**: Yes
- **Highlights**:
  - Modern yet cozy space with comfortable seating
  - Seasonal cold foam drinks including Chocolate Strawberry Latte and Maple Sea Salt Latte
  - Serves drinks in ceramic mugs for in-house consumption
  - Offers hidden menu items like specialty hot chocolate
  - Clean environment with friendly staff

All three cafes offer unique experiences within walking distance of the Metropolitan Museum of Art, with Cabuc Coffee being the closest option.
