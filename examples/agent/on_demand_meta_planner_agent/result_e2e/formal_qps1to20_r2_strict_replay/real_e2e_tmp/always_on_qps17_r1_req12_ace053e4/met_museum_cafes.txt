# Cafes Near The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

## Top Recommendations

### 1. 787 Coffee ⭐ 4.9/5 (3,477 reviews)
- **Address**: 228 E 80th St, New York, NY 10075
- **Phone**: (646) 850-0234
- **Website**: http://www.787coffee.com/
- **Currently Open**: Yes
- **Distance from Met**: ~0.4 miles (7-minute walk)
- **Highlights**: 
  - Exceptionally high rating (4.9/5)
  - Known for unique rum-infused coffee beans
  - Friendly staff who remember regular customers
  - Cozy atmosphere with a swing by the window
  - Fast WiFi available
  - Pet-friendly

### 2. Café Blériot XI ⭐ 4.6/5 (310 reviews)
- **Address**: 226 E 83rd St, New York, NY 10028
- **Phone**: (646) 244-9539
- **Price Level**: $ (Inexpensive)
- **Currently Open**: Yes
- **Distance from Met**: ~0.5 miles (9-minute walk)
- **Highlights**:
  - French-inspired café with authentic pastries
  - Freshly baked pastries daily
  - Buy-one-get-one-free pastries after 5 PM
  - Note: No laptop use allowed (not a remote work space)
  - Limited seating (only four small tables)

### 3. Cabuc Coffee ⭐ 4.6/5 (153 reviews)
- **Address**: 1211 Lexington Ave, New York, NY 10028
- **Phone**: (646) 891-0011
- **Currently Open**: Yes
- **Distance from Met**: ~0.3 miles (6-minute walk)
- **Highlights**:
  - Turkish-inspired coffee shop with unique offerings
  - Excellent for working (offers WiFi)
  - Bright, pleasant atmosphere
  - Specialties include Turkish coffee and baklava lattes
  - Friendly staff

### 4. Le Pain Quotidien ⭐ 3.9/5 (881 reviews)
- **Address**: 1131 Madison Ave, New York, NY 10028
- **Phone**: (212) 327-4900
- **Website**: https://lepainquotidien.com/us/stores/114:%20madison_avenue
- **Price Level**: $$ (Moderate)
- **Currently Open**: Yes
- **Distance from Met**: ~0.2 miles (4-minute walk) - CLOSEST OPTION
- **Highlights**:
  - Most convenient location (closest to the museum)
  - Part of an international chain specializing in organic breads and pastries
  - Good for quick meals before or after museum visits
  - Mixed reviews on food quality and service speed

## Summary
For the best overall experience, **787 Coffee** stands out with its exceptional rating and unique offerings, though it requires a slightly longer walk. **Café Blériot XI** offers excellent value with its French pastries and afternoon deals. **Cabuc Coffee** provides a unique Turkish coffee experience in a work-friendly environment. If convenience is your priority, **Le Pain Quotidien** is the closest option to the museum entrance.
