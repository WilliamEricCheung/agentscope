Art Deco Furniture in the Metropolitan Museum of Art Collection

Overview:
The Metropolitan Museum of Art has an extensive collection of Art Deco furniture, with 5,584 objects identified in their database. The collection spans from early decorative arts pieces that influenced the Art Deco movement to definitive 1920s-1930s Art Deco masterpieces.

Collection Highlights:
- The Met's Art Deco furniture collection includes works by renowned designers like Eileen Gray
- Pieces span multiple departments, primarily European Sculpture and Decorative Arts and Modern and Contemporary Art
- Materials commonly used include lacquered wood, exotic veneers, metals, and innovative combinations typical of the Art Deco period
- The collection represents both European and American interpretations of the Art Deco style
