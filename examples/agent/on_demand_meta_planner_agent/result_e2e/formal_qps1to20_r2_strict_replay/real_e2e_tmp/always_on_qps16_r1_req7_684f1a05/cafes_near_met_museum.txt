# Cafes Near The Metropolitan Museum of Art

The Metropolitan Museum of Art is located at 1000 5th Ave, New York, NY 10028.

## Currently Open Cafes

### 1. Cabuc Coffee ⭐ 4.6/5 (153 reviews)
- **Address**: 1211 Lexington Ave, New York, NY 10028
- **Phone**: (646) 891-0011
- **Status**: Currently open
- **Highlights**: 
  - Highly rated Turkish coffee shop with excellent pastries
  - Offers Wi-Fi, making it suitable for working
  - Known for their baklava latte and咸 honey croissant
  - Bright, pleasant atmosphere with friendly staff

### 2. Uptown Cafe and Pastries ⭐ 4.5/5 (85 reviews)
- **Address**: 100 E 96th St, New York, NY 10128
- **Phone**: (646) 410-0132
- **Status**: Currently open
- **Highlights**:
  - Modern yet comfortable space with plenty of seating
  - Seasonal cold foam drinks are highly recommended
  - Offers ceramic mugs for in-house consumption
  - Known for their cookie butter latte and lavender latte

## Summary
For visitors to the Metropolitan Museum of Art looking for a cafe experience right now, Cabuc Coffee and Uptown Cafe and Pastries are excellent choices as they are both currently open and highly rated. Café Fledermaus is also worth considering if you're visiting during its operating hours, as it's located very close to the museum in the neighboring Neue Galerie.
