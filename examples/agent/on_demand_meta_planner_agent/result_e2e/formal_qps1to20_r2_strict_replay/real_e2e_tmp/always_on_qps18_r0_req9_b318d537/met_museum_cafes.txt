# Top 3 Cafes Near The Metropolitan Museum of Art

## 1. Cositas Café
**Rating:** 4.8/5 (184 reviews)
**Address:** 353 E 78th St, New York, NY 10075
**Phone:** (347) 673-1733
**Website:** https://www.cositascafenyc.com/
**Price Level:** $
**Currently Open:** No

**Description:**
Cositas Café is a charming Latin American café offering authentic pastries and coffee. It's known for its excellent flan, smoothies, and empanadas. The café has a cozy atmosphere and serves traditional Colombian treats like almojábanas and buñuelos. Despite its small size, it offers an impressive selection of Latin American baked goods.

**Highlights from Reviews:**
- "Amazing Colombian heritage options with GREAT baked fresh goods"
- "Perfect for satisfying Latin American pastry cravings"
- "Authentic empanadas and excellent coffee"

## 2. Cafe Commerce
**Rating:** 4.7/5 (370 reviews)
**Address:** 964 Lexington Ave, New York, NY 10021
**Phone:** (212) 390-8106
**Website:** http://cafecommercenyc.com/
**Currently Open:** Yes

**Description:**
Cafe Commerce is an upscale café and restaurant offering a sophisticated dining experience. While it serves excellent coffee, it's particularly known for its exceptional food menu featuring dishes like rabbit pappardelle, lamb, and their famous coconut cake. The intimate setting makes it perfect for special occasions.

**Highlights from Reviews:**
- "Every dish was perfect with outstanding rabbit pappardelle and lamb"
- "Famous for their incredible coconut cake"
- "Intimate atmosphere with attentive service"
- "Great for brunch or special occasions"

## 3. La Bomboniera
**Rating:** 4.7/5 (560 reviews)
**Address:** 1378 Lexington Ave, New York, NY 10128
**Phone:** (646) 370-6302
**Website:** http://labombonieranyc.com/
**Currently Open:** No

**Description:**
La Bomboniera is an eco-conscious café that stands out for its sustainability practices, using no single-use plastics or disposable products. The café offers excellent hot chocolate with cream, cappuccino, and Italian pastries in a beautiful, cozy setting. It's particularly busy during summer months.

**Highlights from Reviews:**
- "Eco-friendly café with no single-use plastics"
- "Delicious hot chocolate with cream (cioccolata calda con panna)"
- "Beautiful ambiance perfect for dates"
- "Known for lemon peel cake and chocolate cake options"

All three cafés are within walking distance of the Metropolitan Museum of Art, located on the Upper East Side of Manhattan.
