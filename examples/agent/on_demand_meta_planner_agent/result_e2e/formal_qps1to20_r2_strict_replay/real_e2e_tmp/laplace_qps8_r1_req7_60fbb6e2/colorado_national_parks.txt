# National Parks and Monuments in Colorado

Based on the National Parks API search, there are 17 national parks, monuments, and historic sites in Colorado:

1. **Amache National Historic Site**
   - Designation: National Historic Site
   - Description: Former Japanese American incarceration site during World War II

2. **Bent's Old Fort National Historic Site**
   - Designation: National Historic Site
   - Description: Adobe trading post on the Santa Fe Trail from the 1840s

3. **Black Canyon Of The Gunnison National Park**
   - Designation: National Park
   - Description: Features some of the steepest cliffs and oldest rock in North America

4. **California National Historic Trail** (partially in Colorado)
   - Designation: National Historic Trail
   - Description: Part of the 5,000-mile trail used by emigrants traveling to California

5. **Colorado National Monument**
   - Designation: National Monument
   - Description: Preserves towering monoliths within a vast plateau and canyon panorama

6. **Curecanti National Recreation Area**
   - Designation: National Recreation Area
   - Description: Three reservoirs and surrounding lands in western Colorado

7. **Dinosaur National Monument** (shared with Utah)
   - Designation: National Monument
   - Description: Contains dinosaur fossils embedded in rocks and diverse landscapes

8. **Florissant Fossil Beds National Monument**
   - Designation: National Monument
   - Description: Contains one of the richest and most diverse fossil deposits in the world

9. **Great Sand Dunes National Park & Preserve**
   - Designation: National Park & Preserve
   - Description: Home to the tallest dunes in North America

10. **Hovenweep National Monument** (shared with Utah)
    - Designation: National Monument
    - Description: Preserves six prehistoric sites built between 1200-1300 CE

11. **Mesa Verde National Park**
    - Designation: National Park
    - Description: Protects Ancestral Pueblo communities built on mesas and in cliffs

12. **Old Spanish National Historic Trail** (partially in Colorado)
    - Designation: National Historic Trail
    - Description: Route of mule pack trains between Santa Fe and Los Angeles

13. **Pony Express National Historic Trail** (partially in Colorado)
    - Designation: National Historic Trail
    - Description: Relay system for mail delivery between Missouri and California

14. **Rocky Mountain National Park**
    - Designation: National Park
    - Description: 415 square miles of mountain environments with over 300 miles of hiking trails

15. **Sand Creek Massacre National Historic Site**
    - Designation: National Historic Site
    - Description: Commemorates the 1864 massacre of Cheyenne and Arapaho people

16. **Santa Fe National Historic Trail** (partially in Colorado)
    - Designation: National Historic Trail
    - Description: Trade route between western Missouri and Santa Fe, New Mexico

17. **Yucca House National Monument**
    - Designation: National Monument
    - Description: Preserves a large unexcavated pueblo in Montezuma Valley

Note: Some sites are shared with other states or are part of larger trail systems that pass through multiple states.
