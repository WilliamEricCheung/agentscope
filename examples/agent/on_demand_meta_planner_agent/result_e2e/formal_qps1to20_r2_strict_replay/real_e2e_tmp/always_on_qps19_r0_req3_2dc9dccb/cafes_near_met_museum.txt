# Cafes Near The Metropolitan Museum of Art

The Metropolitan Museum of Art is located at 1000 5th Ave, New York, NY 10028 (Coordinates: 40.7794366, -73.963244).

## Top Recommended Cafes

### 1. Antonucci Cafe ⭐ 4.5/5 (Currently Open)
- **Address**: 170 E 81st St, New York, NY 10028
- **Phone**: (212) 510-5100
- **Website**: https://antonuccicafe81.com/
- **Price Level**: $$$
- **Distance from MET**: ~0.4 miles (7-minute walk)
- **Highlights**: 
  - Authentic Italian cafe with warm, intimate atmosphere
  - Highly rated for their Burrata salad and seafood dishes
  - Homemade pasta available (including gluten-free options)
  - Currently open for visitors

### 2. 787 Coffee ⭐ 4.9/5 (Highest Rated)
- **Address**: 228 E 80th St, New York, NY 10075
- **Phone**: (646) 850-0234
- **Website**: http://www.787coffee.com/
- **Distance from MET**: ~0.5 miles (9-minute walk)
- **Highlights**:
  - Specialty coffee shop with unique rum-infused coffee beans
  - Cozy neighborhood feel with friendly baristas who remember regulars
  - Features a swing seat by the window for people-watching
  - Excellent matcha latte and rum-infused cappuccino
  - Pet-friendly establishment

### 3. Dear Coffee ⭐ 4.6/5
- **Address**: 1246 Lexington Ave, New York, NY 10028
- **Website**: https://www.dearcoffeenyc.com/
- **Distance from MET**: ~0.4 miles (8-minute walk)
- **Highlights**:
  - Minimalist coffee shop using Onyx Coffee Lab beans
  - Known for creative seasonal drinks like Apricot Matcha
  - Unique latte art (customers have received dragon designs!)
  - Small space, better for takeout than extended stays
  - No restroom available
