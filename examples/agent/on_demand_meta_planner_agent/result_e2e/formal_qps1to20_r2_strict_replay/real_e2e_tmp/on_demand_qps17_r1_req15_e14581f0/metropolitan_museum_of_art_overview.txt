# The Metropolitan Museum of Art - Comprehensive Overview

## Location
The Metropolitan Museum of Art (often referred to as "The Met") is located at 1000 Fifth Avenue, along the eastern edge of Central Park in Manhattan, New York City, New York 10028, United States.

## History
- **Founded**: April 13, 1870
- **Opened to the public**: February 20, 1872
- **Current building construction began**: 1880
- The museum was established by a group of American citizens including businessmen, financiers, leading artists, and thinkers who wanted to bring art and art education to the American people.
- The original location was at 681 Fifth Avenue, but the museum moved to its current location in Central Park in 1880.
- Over the decades, the museum has undergone numerous expansions, with major additions including:
  - The American Wing (1924)
  - The Cloisters (1938) - dedicated to medieval European art
  - The Met Breuer (2016-2020) - modern and contemporary art (now closed)
  - The Met Fifth Avenue continues to be the main location

## Significance
- One of the world's largest and most comprehensive art museums
- Among the most visited museums globally, typically welcoming over 7 million visitors annually (pre-pandemic)
- Houses over 2 million works of art spanning 5,000 years of world culture
- Considered one of the "Big Three" art museums alongside the Louvre (Paris) and the British Museum (London)
- Serves as a cultural landmark and educational institution for New York City and the world
- Plays a crucial role in art preservation, scholarship, and public education

## Main Collections
The Met's collection is organized into 17 curatorial departments:

### 1. **American Wing**
- American decorative arts, paintings, sculptures, and period rooms from the colonial period through the early 20th century
- Notable works include pieces by John Singer Sargent, Winslow Homer, and Gilbert Stuart

### 2. **Ancient Near Eastern Art**
- Artifacts from Mesopotamia, Iran, Arabia, Anatolia, and the Caucasus
- Includes cuneiform tablets, cylinder seals, and monumental sculptures

### 3. **Arms and Armor**
- European, Japanese, and Islamic arms and armor from the Middle Ages to the 19th century
- Features tournament armor, ceremonial weapons, and functional military equipment

### 4. **Arts of Africa, Oceania, and the Americas**
- Traditional art from sub-Saharan Africa, the Pacific Islands, and the indigenous peoples of North, Central, and South America
- Includes masks, sculptures, textiles, and ceremonial objects

### 5. **Asian Art**
- One of the most comprehensive collections outside Asia
- Covers Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Indian, Southeast Asian, and Himalayan art
- Includes paintings, sculptures, ceramics, textiles, and decorative arts

### 6. **The Costume Institute**
- Houses over 35,000 costumes and accessories spanning five continents and centuries
- Famous for the annual Met Gala and thematic exhibitions

### 7. **European Paintings**
- Masterpieces from the 13th through the early 20th century
- Includes works by Rembrandt, Vermeer, Velázquez, Goya, Turner, Monet, Van Gogh, and many others

### 8. **European Sculpture and Decorative Arts**
- Renaissance to early 20th-century European sculpture, furniture, tapestries, and decorative objects
- Features period rooms and complete interiors

### 9. **Greek and Roman Art**
- Artifacts from the Neolithic period through the late Roman Empire
- Includes sculptures, vases, jewelry, and architectural elements

### 10. **Islamic Art**
- Art from the 7th to the 20th century across three continents
- Includes calligraphy, manuscripts, ceramics, metalwork, and textiles

### 11. **Medieval Art and The Cloisters**
- European medieval art from the Byzantine to the early Renaissance periods
- The Cloisters museum in Fort Tryon Park houses the medieval collection in a reconstructed monastery setting

### 12. **Modern and Contemporary Art**
- Works from 1890 to the present
- Includes paintings, sculptures, drawings, prints, photographs, and design objects

### 13. **Musical Instruments**
- Over 5,000 instruments from six continents and the Pacific Islands
- Spans from ancient times to the present

### 14. **Photographs**
- Over 75,000 photographs from the 1830s to the present
- Includes works by major photographers across all genres

### 15. **Prints and Drawings**
- Over 1 million works on paper from the 15th century to the present
- Includes master drawings, watercolors, and prints

### 16. **Robert Lehman Collection**
- Diverse collection donated by Robert Lehman including paintings, decorative arts, and manuscripts

### 17. **Egyptian Art**
- One of the finest collections of Egyptian art outside Cairo
- Includes the Temple of Dendur, mummies, jewelry, and everyday objects from ancient Egypt

## Additional Information
- **Admission**: Pay-what-you-wish for New York State residents and students from NY, NJ, and CT; fixed admission for other visitors
- **Hours**: Typically open Sunday through Thursday 10 AM–5:30 PM, Friday and Saturday 10 AM–9 PM
- **Website**: metmuseum.org
- **Annual Attendance**: Approximately 7 million visitors (pre-pandemic figures)
- **Staff**: Over 2,200 employees including curators, conservators, educators, and support staff

The Metropolitan Museum of Art continues to serve as a vital cultural institution, preserving humanity's artistic heritage while making it accessible to audiences worldwide through exhibitions, educational programs, digital initiatives, and scholarly research.
