# Top Cafes Near The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Based on proximity (within 500 meters) and customer ratings, here are the top cafe options near the Metropolitan Museum of Art located at 1000 5th Ave, New York, NY 10028.

## 1. 787 Coffee ⭐ 4.9/5 (3,472 reviews)
**Address:** 228 E 80th St, New York, NY 10075
**Phone:** (646) 850-0234
**Website:** http://www.787coffee.com/
**Distance from Met:** Approximately 0.4 miles southeast

**Highlights:**
- Exceptionally high rating with thousands of positive reviews
- Known for unique rum-infused coffee beans
- Pet-friendly establishment with a cozy, neighborhood feel
- Fast WiFi and comfortable seating including a swing by the window
- Freshly baked pastries throughout the day
- Staff remembers regular customers by name

**Customer Feedback:**
Customers consistently praise the friendly baristas (particularly Alex, Fox, and Isa), the unique rum and whiskey-flavored coffee options, and the welcoming atmosphere. Many reviewers mention it as their favorite coffee shop in the area.

## 2. Antonucci Cafe ⭐ 4.5/5 (428 reviews)
**Address:** 170 E 81st St, New York, NY 10028
**Phone:** (212) 570-5100
**Website:** https://antonuccicafe81.com/
**Price Level:** $$$ (Higher-end)
**Distance from Met:** Approximately 0.3 miles east

**Highlights:**
- Authentic Italian café and restaurant
- Intimate setting with artwork covering the walls
- Excellent Burrata salad with marinated tomatoes
- Perfectly cooked fresh pasta (including gluten-free options)
- Cozy outdoor seating with adequate heating
- Family-owned establishment

**Customer Feedback:**
Reviews highlight the authentic Italian cuisine, particularly the Burrata salad and fresh pasta dishes. Some note that dessert selection is limited during daytime hours, and a few found certain dishes underseasoned. Overall considered a charming spot for a more substantial meal rather than just coffee.

## 3. Dear Coffee ⭐ 4.6/5 (330 reviews)
**Address:** 1246 Lexington Ave, New York, NY 10028
**Website:** https://www.dearcoffeenyc.com/
**Distance from Met:** Approximately 0.3 miles east

**Highlights:**
- Minimalist coffee shop with excellent coffee quality
- Uses Onyx Coffee Lab beans for espresso and pour-over
- Creative specialty drinks like Apricot Matcha
- Artistic latte art (customers mention dragon designs)
- Convenient location near Guggenheim Museum

**Customer Feedback:**
Customers appreciate the high-quality coffee and creative drink options. The space is noted as small with limited seating and no restroom, making it better suited for quick visits or takeout rather than extended stays. The apricot matcha receives particular praise for its balanced flavor profile.
