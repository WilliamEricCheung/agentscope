National Parks in Colorado

According to the National Park Service, there are 17 national park sites located within the state of Colorado. These include national parks, national monuments, national historic sites, and national historic trails that pass through the state.

1. Amache National Historic Site
   - Designation: National Historic Site
   - Description: One of ten incarceration sites established during World War II to unjustly incarcerate Japanese Americans. Over 10,000 people were incarcerated at Amache from 1942 to 1945.

2. Bent's Old Fort National Historic Site
   - Designation: National Historic Site
   - Description: An adobe trading post on the Santa Fe Trail in the 1840s, where traders, trappers, travelers, and Native American tribes came together for trade.

3. Black Canyon Of The Gunnison National Park
   - Designation: National Park
   - Description: Features some of the steepest cliffs, oldest rock, and craggiest spires in North America, carved by the Gunnison River over two million years.

4. California National Historic Trail
   - Designation: National Historic Trail
   - Description: Passes through Colorado as part of its 5,000-mile route covering 10 states, following the path of over 250,000 emigrants who traveled to California during the 1840s and 1850s.

5. Colorado National Monument
   - Designation: National Monument
   - Description: Preserves towering monoliths within a vast plateau and canyon panorama, with sheer-walled, red rock canyons along Rim Rock Drive.

6. Curecanti National Recreation Area
   - Designation: National Recreation Area
   - Description: Comprises three reservoirs and surrounding lands in western Colorado, featuring both natural and human-made features.

7. Dinosaur National Monument
   - Designation: National Monument (shared with Utah)
   - Description: Contains fossils embedded in rocks from 150 million years ago, along with mountains, desert, rivers, petroglyphs, and diverse wildlife.

8. Florissant Fossil Beds National Monument
   - Designation: National Monument
   - Description: Contains one of the richest and most diverse fossil deposits in the world, including petrified redwood stumps up to 14 feet wide and thousands of detailed fossils of insects and plants.

9. Great Sand Dunes National Park & Preserve
   - Designation: National Park & Preserve
   - Description: Home to the tallest dunes in North America, set against a diverse landscape of grasslands, wetlands, forests, alpine lakes, and tundra.

10. Hovenweep National Monument
    - Designation: National Monument (shared with Utah)
    - Description: Preserves six prehistoric sites built between 1200 and 1300 CE, featuring multistory towers perched on canyon rims and balanced on boulders.

11. Mesa Verde National Park
    - Designation: National Park
    - Description: Protects the cultural heritage of 27 Pueblos and Tribes, showcasing communities built by Ancestral Pueblo people on mesas and in cliffs over 700 years.

12. Old Spanish National Historic Trail
    - Designation: National Historic Trail
    - Description: Passes through Colorado as part of its route between Santa Fe, New Mexico, and Los Angeles, California, following mule pack trains across the Southwest.

13. Pony Express National Historic Trail
    - Designation: National Historic Trail
    - Description: Passes through Colorado as part of its route from Missouri to California, commemorating the relay system that carried mail in just 10 days before the telegraph.

14. Rocky Mountain National Park
    - Designation: National Park
    - Description: Encompasses 415 square miles of mountain environments, from montane meadows to alpine lakes and towering peaks, with over 300 miles of hiking trails.

15. Sand Creek Massacre National Historic Site
    - Designation: National Historic Site
    - Description: Commemorates the tragic events of November 29, 1864, when around 750 Arapaho and Cheyenne people were attacked while encamped in a valley by Big Sandy Creek.

16. Santa Fe National Historic Trail
    - Designation: National Historic Trail
    - Description: Passes through Colorado as part of its route between western Missouri and Santa Fe, New Mexico, following the path of freight wagons carrying cargo.

17. Yucca House National Monument
    - Designation: National Monument
    - Description: Preserves a large unexcavated pueblo in Montezuma Valley, nestled between Mesa Verde and Ute Mountain, largely untouched since being protected in 1919.
