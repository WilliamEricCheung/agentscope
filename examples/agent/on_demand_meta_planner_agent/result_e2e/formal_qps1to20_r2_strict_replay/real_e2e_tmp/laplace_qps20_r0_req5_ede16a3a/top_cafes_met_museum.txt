# Top-Rated Cafes Near The Metropolitan Museum of Art

Based on Google Maps data, here are the highest-rated cafes within walking distance (1000 meters) of the Metropolitan Museum of Art in New York.

## 1. 787 Coffee ⭐ 4.9/5 (3,472 reviews)
**Address:** 228 E 80th St, New York, NY 10075
**Phone:** (646) 850-0234
**Website:** http://www.787coffee.com/

**Highlights:**
- Highest rated cafe in the area with nearly perfect 4.9/5 rating
- Known for unique rum-infused coffee beans
- Friendly staff, especially barista Alex who receives special mentions
- Fast WiFi and dog-friendly
- Cozy, neighborhood feel with excellent lattes and tea
- Freshly baked pastries daily

**Customer Feedback:**
Customers consistently praise the exceptional coffee quality, friendly service, and welcoming atmosphere. Many mention it feels like a true neighborhood gathering spot.

## 2. La Bomboniera ⭐ 4.7/5 (560 reviews)
**Address:** 1378 Lexington Ave, New York, NY 10128
**Phone:** (646) 370-6302
**Website:** http://labombonieranyc.com/

**Highlights:**
- Eco-friendly establishment with no single-use plastics
- Beautiful, cozy atmosphere
- Excellent hot chocolate with whipped cream
- Great Italian pastries and cappuccino
- Bar available in addition to coffee offerings

**Customer Feedback:**
Customers love the sustainable approach and delicious drinks. One minor complaint mentioned occasional service issues, but food quality is consistently praised.

## 3. Café Blériot XI ⭐ 4.6/5 (310 reviews)
**Address:** 226 E 83rd St, New York, NY 10028
**Phone:** (646) 244-9539

**Highlights:**
- Authentic French atmosphere
- Delicious pastries, especially ham and cheese croissants
- After 5 PM: buy one get one free on pastries!
- No laptop policy (encourages conversation)
- Charming, intimate setting with only four small tables

**Customer Feedback:**
Praised for its authentic French feel and excellent pastries. Some mention music can be too loud for the small space.

## 4. Madame Bonté Café ⭐ 4.5/5 (380 reviews)
**Address:** 318 E 84th St, New York, NY 10028
**Phone:** (917) 409-5555
**Website:** http://www.madamebonte.com/

**Highlights:**
- Unique policy: no electronic screens allowed downstairs
- Beautiful hidden gem with outdoor patio seating
- Excellent honey lavender latte and other specialty drinks
- Perfect for conversation and socializing
- Multiple seating areas including upstairs and downstairs

**Customer Feedback:**
Customers appreciate the screen-free environment that encourages real conversation. The coffee quality is consistently excellent.

## 5. Antonucci Cafe ⭐ 4.5/5 (428 reviews)
**Address:** 170 E 81st St, New York, NY 10028
**Phone:** (212) 570-5100
**Website:** https://antonuccicafe81.com/

**Highlights:**
- Italian restaurant/cafe with warm, intimate atmosphere
- Excellent Burrata salad and fresh pasta
- Art-filled walls creating a cozy ambiance
- Some desserts only available in the evening
- Higher price point but considered worth it for quality

**Customer Feedback:**
Mixed reviews on food seasoning, but generally positive about the authentic Italian experience and atmosphere.

## Summary Recommendations:

**Best Overall Experience:** 787 Coffee - Highest ratings, exceptional service, and unique coffee offerings
**Most Unique Atmosphere:** Madame Bonté Café - Screen-free environment perfect for conversation
**Best for Pastries:** Café Blériot XI - Authentic French pastries with afternoon deals
**Most Sustainable:** La Bomboniera - Eco-friendly practices with excellent drinks
**Best Italian Option:** Antonucci Cafe - Authentic Italian cuisine in a cozy setting

All of these cafes are within a 10-15 minute walk from the Metropolitan Museum of Art, making them perfect stops before or after your museum visit.
