# Top 3 Cafes Near The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

## 1. 787 Coffee
**Rating:** 4.9/5 (3,482 reviews)
**Address:** 228 E 80th St, New York, NY 10075
**Coordinates:** 40.774289, -73.955632
**Phone:** (646) 850-0234
**Website:** http://www.787coffee.com/
**Currently Open:** No

**Highlights:**
- Exceptionally high rating with nearly perfect reviews
- Known for unique rum-infused coffee beans
- Cozy, neighborhood feel with friendly staff who remember regulars
- Features a swing by the window for a unique seating experience
- Fast WiFi and comfortable atmosphere for working or relaxing
- Pet-friendly establishment

**Customer Reviews:**
Customers consistently praise the exceptional coffee quality, particularly the rum-infused options. The staff (especially Alex, Fox, and Isa) are noted for their warmth and hospitality. Many reviewers mention the cozy, authentic New York neighborhood cafe atmosphere.

## 2. Cositas Café
**Rating:** 4.8/5 (184 reviews)
**Address:** 353 E 78th St, New York, NY 10075
**Coordinates:** 40.772066, -73.953613
**Phone:** (347) 673-1733
**Website:** https://www.cositascafenyc.com/
**Price Level:** $ (Inexpensive)
**Currently Open:** No

**Highlights:**
- Authentic Latin American café with Colombian influence
- Freshly baked goods including empanadas, almojábanas, and buñuelos
- Excellent flan and smoothies
- Family-owned Latinx business
- Great coffee alongside traditional Latin American treats

**Customer Reviews:**
Reviewers with Latin American heritage particularly appreciate the authenticity of the offerings. The baked goods receive consistent praise, especially the flan and empanadas. One negative review mentioned disappointment with the Colombian hot chocolate, but this appears to be an outlier.

## 3. La Bomboniera
**Rating:** 4.7/5 (560 reviews)
**Address:** 1378 Lexington Ave, New York, NY 10128
**Coordinates:** 40.782672, -73.953466
**Phone:** (646) 370-6302
**Website:** http://labombonieranyc.com/
**Currently Open:** No

**Highlights:**
- Environmentally conscious café with zero single-use plastics
- Uses reusable cups and containers as part of their sustainability mission
- Beautiful ambiance with both coffee shop and bar options
- Excellent hot chocolate with cream (cioccolata calda con panna)
- Known for delicious cannoli and cakes
- Popular date spot with cozy atmosphere

**Customer Reviews:**
Customers love the commitment to sustainability and the quality of both coffee and desserts. The hot chocolate and cannoli receive special mention. One reviewer noted that service can occasionally be lacking despite the excellent food and drinks.

## Summary
All three cafés are within approximately 0.5-0.7 miles from The Metropolitan Museum of Art, making them convenient options for visitors looking for a coffee break or light meal after exploring the museum. Each offers a distinct experience: 787 Coffee for specialty coffee enthusiasts, Cositas Café for those seeking Latin American flavors, and La Bomboniera for environmentally conscious patrons who appreciate Italian-inspired treats.
