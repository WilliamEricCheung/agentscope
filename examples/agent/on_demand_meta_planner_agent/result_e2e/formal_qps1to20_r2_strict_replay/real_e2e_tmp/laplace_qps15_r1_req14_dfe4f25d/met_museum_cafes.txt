# Top Cafes Near The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Based on proximity and ratings, here are the best cafe options near the Metropolitan Museum of Art (located at 1000 5th Ave, New York, NY 10028).

## 1. 787 Coffee ⭐ 4.9/5 (3,472 reviews)
**Address:** 228 E 80th St, New York, NY 10075  
**Phone:** (646) 850-0234  
**Website:** http://www.787coffee.com/

**Highlights:**
- Exceptionally high rating (4.9/5) with thousands of positive reviews
- Known for unique rum-infused coffee beans
- Friendly staff who remember regular customers
- Cozy atmosphere with fast WiFi
- Pet-friendly establishment
- Features a swing seat by the window for people-watching

**Customer Feedback:** 
Customers consistently praise the exceptional service, unique coffee flavors, and welcoming atmosphere. Many mention the staff going above and beyond to make visitors feel comfortable, even with pets during rainy weather.

## 2. Café Blériot XI ⭐ 4.6/5 (310 reviews)
**Address:** 226 E 83rd St, New York, NY 10028  
**Phone:** (646) 244-9539  
**Price Level:** $ (Inexpensive)

**Highlights:**
- Charming French-inspired cafe with authentic atmosphere
- Freshly baked pastries including ham & cheese croissants
- After 5 PM: Buy one pastry, get one free!
- Note: No laptop use allowed (not designed as a work cafe)
- Limited seating (only four small tables)

**Customer Feedback:**
Visitors love the French ambiance and delicious pastries. Some mention the music can be loud for such a small space, but most appreciate the cozy, authentic experience.

## 3. Antonucci Cafe ⭐ 4.5/5 (428 reviews)
**Address:** 170 E 81st St, New York, NY 10028  
**Phone:** (212) 570-5100  
**Website:** https://antonuccicafe81.com/  
**Price Level:** $$$ (Expensive)

**Highlights:**
- Authentic Italian restaurant/cafe with charming, intimate atmosphere
- Art-covered walls creating a warm, private ambiance
- Excellent Burrata salad with marinated tomatoes
- Perfectly cooked fresh pasta dishes
- Gluten-free pasta options available
- Note: Not all desserts available during daytime hours

**Customer Feedback:**
Customers appreciate the authentic Italian cuisine and intimate setting. Some mention it can be noisy and prices are on the higher side, but most find the quality justifies the cost.

## Distance from The Met:
All these cafes are within a 5-10 minute walk from the Metropolitan Museum of Art, making them convenient options before or after your museum visit.

*Note: Operating hours may vary. It's recommended to check current hours before visiting.*
