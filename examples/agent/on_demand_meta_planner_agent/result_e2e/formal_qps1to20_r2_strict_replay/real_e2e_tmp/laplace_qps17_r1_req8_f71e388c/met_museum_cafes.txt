# Top-Rated Cafes Near The Metropolitan Museum of Art

This report details the highest-rated cafes within walking distance of the Metropolitan Museum of Art in New York City, including their ratings, distances, and key features.

## 1. 787 Coffee
- **Rating**: 4.9/5 (3,472 reviews)
- **Distance**: 0.9 km (13-minute walk)
- **Address**: 228 E 80th St, New York, NY 10075
- **Phone**: (646) 850-0234
- **Website**: http://www.787coffee.com/
- **Key Features**:
  - Known for unique rum-infused coffee beans
  - Cozy, neighborhood feel with friendly staff
  - Fast WiFi and comfortable seating
  - Swing seat by the window for people-watching
  - Freshly baked pastries throughout the day
  - Staff remembers regular customers' names and orders

## 2. Cositas Café
- **Rating**: 4.8/5 (184 reviews)
- **Distance**: 1.3 km (19-minute walk)
- **Address**: 353 E 78th St, New York, NY 10075
- **Phone**: (347) 673-1733
- **Website**: https://www.cositascafenyc.com/
- **Price Level**: $
- **Key Features**:
  - Latin American café specializing in Colombian treats
  - Excellent flan and empanadas
  - Fresh baked goods daily
  - Authentic arepas and almojábanas
  - Family-owned Latinx business
  - Great smoothies and coffee

## 3. La Bomboniera
- **Rating**: 4.7/5 (560 reviews)
- **Distance**: 1.2 km (17-minute walk)
- **Address**: 1378 Lexington Ave, New York, NY 10128
- **Phone**: (646) 370-6302
- **Website**: http://labombonieranyc.com/
- **Key Features**:
  - Eco-friendly establishment with no single-use plastics
  - Serves coffee, pastries, and has a bar
  - Excellent hot chocolate with cream
  - Beautiful atmosphere for dates or meeting friends
  - Known for lemon peel cake and chocolate desserts
  - Reusable cups for a more intimate experience

## 4. Café Fledermaus
- **Rating**: 4.2/5 (40 reviews)
- **Distance**: 0.4 km (5-minute walk)
- **Address**: 1048 5th Ave, New York, NY 10028
- **Phone**: (212) 288-0665
- **Website**: http://www.neuegalerie.org/cafes/fledermaus
- **Price Level**: $$$
- **Key Features**:
  - Located in the Neue Galerie museum (not the Met)
  - European-style café in the basement level
  - Known for herring sandwiches and mulled wine
  - Excellent cakes with balanced sweetness
  - Closest option to the Met at just 5 minutes walking
  - More expensive than other options

## Summary
For the highest-rated experience, 787 Coffee stands out with its exceptional 4.9 rating and unique rum-infused coffee offerings. If you're looking for something closer to the museum, Café Fledermaus is just a 5-minute walk away, though it's slightly lower rated and more expensive. For authentic Latin American treats, Cositas Café offers excellent value and highly-rated pastries. La Bomboniera provides an eco-conscious option with a great atmosphere for socializing.
