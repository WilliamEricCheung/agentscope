# Top-Rated Cafes Near The Metropolitan Museum of Art

Based on Google Maps data, here are the highest-rated cafes within walking distance (1000 meters) of the Metropolitan Museum of Art in New York City.

## 1. 787 Coffee ⭐ 4.9/5 (3,472 reviews)
**Address:** 228 E 80th St, New York, NY 10075  
**Phone:** (646) 850-0234  
**Website:** http://www.787coffee.com/

**Highlights:**
- Highest rated cafe in the area with nearly perfect 4.9/5 stars
- Known for unique rum-infused coffee beans
- Cozy neighborhood feel with friendly staff who remember regulars
- Features a swing by the window for enjoying street views
- Fast WiFi and pet-friendly
- Popular with locals who visit daily

**Customer Feedback:** Customers consistently praise the exceptional coffee quality, unique flavor options (especially rum and whiskey-infused coffees), and the warm, welcoming atmosphere. Staff members like Alex and Fox receive special mentions for their hospitality.

## 2. Cositas Café ⭐ 4.8/5 (184 reviews)
**Address:** 353 E 78th St, New York, NY 10075  
**Phone:** (347) 673-1733  
**Website:** https://www.cositascafenyc.com/

**Highlights:**
- Authentic Latin American café with Colombian influence
- Freshly baked goods including empanadas, almojábanas, and buñuelos
- Excellent flan and smoothies
- Family-owned Latinx business
- Affordable price point ($)

**Customer Feedback:** Loved by those with Latin American heritage for its authenticity. Customers rave about the fresh baked goods and Latin pastries. One negative review mentioned disappointment with Colombian hot chocolate that included cheese chunks.

## 3. La Bomboniera ⭐ 4.7/5 (560 reviews)
**Address:** 1378 Lexington Ave, New York, NY 10128  
**Phone:** (646) 370-6302  
**Website:** http://labombonieranyc.com/

**Highlights:**
- Eco-conscious café with zero single-use plastics
- Encourages reusable cups for a more intimate experience
- Beautiful ambiance with excellent hot chocolate and cappuccino
- Known for biscotti and Italian-inspired treats
- Has a bar section in addition to café offerings

**Customer Feedback:** Praised for sustainability efforts and cozy atmosphere. Most customers love the food and drinks, though one review mentioned inconsistent service quality during busy periods.

## 4. Ella's Cafe Espresso & Wine Bar ⭐ 4.7/5 (543 reviews)
**Address:** 1465 2nd Ave, New York, NY 10075  
**Phone:** (917) 409-0307  
**Website:** https://www.ellascafenyc.com/

**Highlights:**
- Charming pink cups and outdoor seating
- Features a small free library for book exchanges
- Serves both espresso and wine (day-to-night transition)
- Popular for breakfast items like avocado toast and shakshuka
- Hosts special events like engagement parties

**Customer Feedback:** Customers appreciate the cozy atmosphere, excellent coffee, and friendly service. The café is noted for making guests feel like family and having delicious food that's perfect for takeout.

## 5. JJ Brown Cup ⭐ 4.6/5 (119 reviews)
**Address:** 1707 2nd Ave 1st floor, New York, NY 10128  
**Phone:** (212) 369-3949  
**Website:** https://m.facebook.com/pages/The-Brown-Cup/315693311885913

**Highlights:**
- Favorite takeaway spot for commuters
- Known for hazelnut coffee without added sugar
- Experiments with different pastry fillings and flavors
- Great neighborhood spot with loyal following

**Customer Feedback:** Generally positive reviews for coffee quality and friendly staff, though one customer raised concerns about cleanliness standards.

## 6. Café Blériot XI ⭐ 4.6/5 (310 reviews)
**Address:** 226 E 83rd St, New York, NY 10028  
**Phone:** (646) 244-9539

**Highlights:**
- French-inspired café with authentic pastries
- Famous for ham and cheese croissants
- Intimate space with only four small tables
- Buy-one-get-one-free pastries after 5 PM
- No laptop policy (not designed for remote work)

**Customer Feedback:** Customers love the French atmosphere and high-quality pastries. One reviewer mentioned music volume being too loud for the small space, but most praise the delicious coffee and baked goods.

## Summary Recommendations

For the absolute best coffee experience: **787 Coffee** stands out with its exceptional rating and unique infused coffee options.

For authentic Latin American flavors: **Cositas Café** offers the most distinctive menu with traditional baked goods.

For eco-conscious visitors: **La Bomboniera** provides excellent drinks while maintaining sustainable practices.

For a versatile day-to-night option: **Ella's Cafe** transitions from espresso bar to wine bar and offers a charming atmosphere.

All these cafes are within a 10-15 minute walk from the Metropolitan Museum of Art, making them perfect for a pre- or post-museum refreshment.
