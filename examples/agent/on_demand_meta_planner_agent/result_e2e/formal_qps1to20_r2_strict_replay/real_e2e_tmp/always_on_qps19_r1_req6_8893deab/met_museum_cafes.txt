# Top-Rated Cafes Near The Metropolitan Museum of Art

## 1. 787 Coffee ⭐ 4.9/5 (3,482 reviews)
**Address:** 228 E 80th St, New York, NY 10075  
**Walking Distance:** 0.9 km (13 minutes)  
**Currently Open:** No  
**Phone:** (646) 850-0234  
**Website:** http://www.787coffee.com/

**Key Features:**
- Specialty rum and whiskey-infused coffee beans
- Cozy, neighborhood feel with friendly baristas who remember regulars
- Swing seating by the window for people-watching
- Freshly baked pastries throughout the day
- Fast WiFi
- Pet-friendly establishment
- Unique drinks served in innovative containers (iced drinks in Capri-Sun style pouches)

**Popular Items:** Rum-infused cappuccino, matcha latte, tres leches cake

## 2. Cositas Café ⭐ 4.8/5 (184 reviews)
**Address:** 353 E 78th St, New York, NY 10075  
**Walking Distance:** 1.4 km (19 minutes)  
**Currently Open:** No  
**Phone:** (347) 673-1733  
**Website:** https://www.cositascafenyc.com/  
**Price Level:** $

**Key Features:**
- Authentic Latin American café with Colombian influence
- Freshly baked Latin pastries including empanadas, almojábanas, and buñuelos
- Excellent flan and smoothies
- Family-owned Latinx business
- Small, cozy atmosphere

**Popular Items:** Colombian hot chocolate, empanadas, flan, Latin pastries

## 3. La Bomboniera ⭐ 4.7/5 (560 reviews)
**Address:** 1378 Lexington Ave, New York, NY 10128  
**Walking Distance:** 1.2 km (17 minutes)  
**Currently Open:** No  
**Phone:** (646) 370-6302  
**Website:** http://labombonieranyc.com/

**Key Features:**
- Eco-friendly establishment with zero single-use plastics
- Reusable cups and sustainable practices
- Beautiful ambiance with bar service in addition to coffee
- Italian-inspired café with pastries and desserts
- Can get crowded during peak hours

**Popular Items:** Hot chocolate with whipped cream (cioccolata calda con panna), lemon zest cake, cannoli, cappuccino

## 4. Inès ⭐ 4.5/5 (422 reviews)
**Address:** 419 E 74th St, New York, NY 10021  
**Walking Distance:** 1.8 km (25 minutes)  
**Currently Open:** Yes  
**Phone:** (845) 283-0535  
**Price Level:** $$

**Key Features:**
- French bistro/café atmosphere
- Doubles as a wine bar in the evenings with an extensive wine list
- Spanish tapas and late-night food menu
- Happy hour offerings
- Small but comfortable seating
- Clean facilities

**Popular Items:** Bacon egg sandwich, ham and cheese sandwich, Americano, apple pie, wine selection

## Summary
All these cafes are within walking distance of the Metropolitan Museum of Art (under 25 minutes walk). 787 Coffee stands out with the highest rating (4.9/5) and unique specialty drinks, while Cositas Café offers authentic Latin American flavors. La Bomboniera is notable for its eco-friendly practices, and Inès is currently open and offers both daytime café service and evening wine bar options.

Note: Opening hours may vary, so it's recommended to check their websites or call ahead before visiting.
