COLLECTION OVERVIEW:
The Metropolitan Museum of Art houses an extensive collection of furniture that includes both genuine Art Deco pieces from the 1920s-1940s period as well as earlier furniture that may have been misclassified in broader searches. The authentic Art Deco collection features works by renowned designers of the era and showcases the characteristic materials, geometric forms, and luxurious finishes typical of the Art Deco style.

Key characteristics of authentic Art Deco furniture in the collection include:
- Use of modern materials like chrome, iron, and exotic woods
- Geometric and streamlined forms
- Luxurious upholstery and decorative elements
- Works by famous Art Deco designers such as Edgar Brandt and Louis Süe

The collection spans multiple departments, with authentic Art Deco pieces primarily housed in the Modern and Contemporary Art department, while some earlier pieces may appear in European Sculpture and Decorative Arts.

For researchers and enthusiasts, the Met's online collection provides access to detailed information, images, and provenance for all 5,593 furniture objects tagged as "Art Deco," with 145 confirmed pieces from the actual Art Deco period (1920-1940).
