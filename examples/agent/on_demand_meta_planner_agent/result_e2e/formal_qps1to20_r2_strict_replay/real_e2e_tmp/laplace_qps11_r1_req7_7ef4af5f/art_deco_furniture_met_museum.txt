ABOUT EMILE-JACQUES RUHLMANN:
Emile-Jacques Ruhlmann was one of the most celebrated and influential furniture designers of the Art Deco movement. His work is characterized by:
- Luxurious materials (exotic woods, ivory, precious metals)
- Geometric forms with elegant proportions
- Exceptional craftsmanship and attention to detail
- Sophisticated, modern aesthetic that defined high-end Art Deco

SEARCH NOTES:
The Metropolitan Museum collection contains numerous pieces that may be catalogued with "Art Deco" terminology but are actually from earlier periods. Authentic Art Deco furniture from the 1920s-1930s period is primarily found in the Modern and Contemporary Art department, while many decorative arts pieces in the European Sculpture and Decorative Arts department are from earlier centuries despite appearing in Art Deco searches.
