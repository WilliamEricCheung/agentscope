# National Parks and Monuments in Colorado

1. **Amache National Historic Site**
   - Designation: National Historic Site
   - Description: Amache, also known as the Granada Relocation Center, was one of ten incarceration sites established during World War II to unjustly incarcerate Japanese Americans.
   - Website: https://www.nps.gov/amch/index.htm

2. **Bent's Old Fort National Historic Site**
   - Designation: National Historic Site
   - Description: An adobe trading post on the Santa Fe Trail in the 1840s, where traders, trappers, travelers, and Native American tribes came together for trade.
   - Website: https://www.nps.gov/beol/index.htm

3. **Black Canyon Of The Gunnison National Park**
   - Designation: National Park
   - Description: Features some of the steepest cliffs, oldest rock, and craggiest spires in North America, carved by the Gunnison River over two million years.
   - Website: https://www.nps.gov/blca/index.htm

4. **California National Historic Trail** (partial)
   - Designation: National Historic Trail
   - Description: Follow the route of over 250,000 emigrants who traveled to California during the 1840s and 1850s. The trail passes through portions of 10 states including Colorado.
   - Website: https://www.nps.gov/cali/index.htm

5. **Colorado National Monument**
   - Designation: National Monument
   - Description: Preserves towering monoliths within a vast plateau and canyon panorama, with sheer-walled red rock canyons along Rim Rock Drive.
   - Website: https://www.nps.gov/colm/index.htm

6. **Curecanti National Recreation Area**
   - Designation: National Recreation Area
   - Description: Three reservoirs and surrounding lands featuring both natural and human-made features, from sagebrush shrublands to forests.
   - Website: https://www.nps.gov/cure/index.htm

7. **Dinosaur National Monument** (shared with Utah)
   - Designation: National Monument
   - Description: Contains dinosaur fossils embedded in rocks, mountains, desert, rivers, petroglyphs, and historical homesteader sites.
   - Website: https://www.nps.gov/dino/index.htm

8. **Florissant Fossil Beds National Monument**
   - Designation: National Monument
   - Description: Contains one of the richest and most diverse fossil deposits in the world, including petrified redwood stumps and detailed insect fossils.
   - Website: https://www.nps.gov/flfo/index.htm

9. **Great Sand Dunes National Park & Preserve**
   - Designation: National Park & Preserve
   - Description: Home to the tallest dunes in North America, set against a diverse landscape of grasslands, wetlands, forests, alpine lakes, and tundra.
   - Website: https://www.nps.gov/grsa/index.htm

10. **Hovenweep National Monument** (shared with Utah)
    - Designation: National Monument
    - Description: Preserves six prehistoric sites built between 1200 and 1300 CE, featuring multistory towers perched on canyon rims and balanced on boulders.
    - Website: https://www.nps.gov/hove/index.htm

11. **Mesa Verde National Park**
    - Designation: National Park
    - Description: Protects the cultural heritage of 27 Pueblos and Tribes, showcasing communities built by Ancestral Pueblo people on mesas and in cliffs over 700 years.
    - Website: https://www.nps.gov/meve/index.htm

12. **Old Spanish National Historic Trail** (partial)
    - Designation: National Historic Trail
    - Description: Follows routes of mule pack trains across the Southwest between Santa Fe, New Mexico, and Los Angeles, California, passing through six states including Colorado.
    - Website: https://www.nps.gov/olsp/index.htm

13. **Pony Express National Historic Trail** (partial)
    - Designation: National Historic Trail
    - Description: Commemorates the relay system that carried mail from Missouri to California in just 10 days before the telegraph, passing through eight states including Colorado.
    - Website: https://www.nps.gov/poex/index.htm

14. **Rocky Mountain National Park**
    - Designation: National Park
    - Description: Encompasses 415 square miles of mountain environments, from montane meadows to alpine lakes and towering peaks, with over 300 miles of hiking trails.
    - Website: https://www.nps.gov/romo/index.htm

15. **Sand Creek Massacre National Historic Site**
    - Designation: National Historic Site
    - Description: Commemorates the tragic 1864 massacre of Cheyenne and Arapaho people, preserving the memory and legacy of this painful event in American history.
    - Website: https://www.nps.gov/sand/index.htm

16. **Santa Fe National Historic Trail** (partial)
    - Designation: National Historic Trail
    - Description: Follows the route of freight wagons carrying cargo between western Missouri and Santa Fe, New Mexico, passing through five states including Colorado.
    - Website: https://www.nps.gov/safe/index.htm

17. **Yucca House National Monument**
    - Designation: National Monument
    - Description: Preserves a large unexcavated pueblo in Montezuma Valley, largely untouched since being protected as a national monument in 1919.
    - Website: https://www.nps.gov/yuho/index.htm
