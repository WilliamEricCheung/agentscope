# Cafes Near The Metropolitan Museum of Art

Here are 5 notable cafes near the Metropolitan Museum of Art in New York City, with details on location, distance, ratings, and key features:

## 1. Café Fledermaus
- **Distance**: 0.3 km (5-minute walk)
- **Address**: 1048 5th Ave, New York, NY 10028
- **Rating**: 4.2/5 (40 reviews)
- **Price Level**: $$$ (High-end)
- **Phone**: (212) 288-0665
- **Website**: http://www.neuegalerie.org/cafes/fledermaus
- **Highlights**: Located in the Neue Galerie, offers Austrian-inspired cuisine including herring sandwiches, wiener schnitzel, and pastries. Known for authentic European atmosphere in the basement level.

## 2. Starbucks
- **Distance**: 0.4 km (6-minute walk)
- **Address**: 1142 Madison Ave, New York, NY 10028
- **Rating**: 4.1/5 (927 reviews)
- **Price Level**: $$ (Moderate)
- **Phone**: (212) 288-1506
- **Website**: https://www.starbucks.com/store-locator/store/1015661
- **Highlights**: Convenient chain option that's currently open. Popular spot for visitors to Central Park to rest and use facilities. Offers standard Starbucks menu and mobile ordering.

## 3. Church of Heavenly Rest Café (Bluestone Lane)
- **Distance**: 0.7 km (10-minute walk)
- **Address**: 1085 5th Ave, New York, NY 10128
- **Rating**: 4.4/5 (2,256 reviews)
- **Price Level**: $$ (Moderate)
- **Phone**: (718) 374-6858
- **Website**: https://bluestonelane.com/cafes/upper-east-side-1085-fifth-avenue-new-york/
- **Highlights**: Australian-inspired café known for excellent chocolate croissants, tomato soup, and brunch options. Calm, friendly atmosphere right across from Central Park.

## 4. Rosa's Cafe & Bakery
- **Distance**: 0.9 km (13-minute walk)
- **Address**: 1100 Lexington Ave, New York, NY 10017
- **Rating**: 4.3/5 (32 reviews)
- **Phone**: (646) 429-8194
- **Highlights**: Family-owned café offering fresh pizza, pastries (especially mango cheesecake), and coffee. Known for friendly service and homemade baked goods.

## 5. Antonucci Cafe
- **Distance**: 0.7 km (10-minute walk)
- **Address**: 170 E 81st St, New York, NY 10028
- **Rating**: 4.5/5 (428 reviews)
- **Price Level**: $$$ (High-end)
- **Phone**: (212) 570-5100
- **Website**: https://antonuccicafe81.com/
- **Highlights**: Charming Italian restaurant with intimate atmosphere, featuring dishes like burrata salad, sea bass, and handmade pasta. Offers gluten-free pasta options and has artwork covering the walls.

All these cafes are within a 15-minute walk from the Metropolitan Museum of Art, making them convenient options before or after your museum visit.
