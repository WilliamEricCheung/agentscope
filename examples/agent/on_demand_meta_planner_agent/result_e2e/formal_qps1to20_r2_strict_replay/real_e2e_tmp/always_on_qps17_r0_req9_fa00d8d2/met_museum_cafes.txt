# Top 3 Cafes Near The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

## 1. 787 Coffee
**Rating:** 4.9/5 (3,477 reviews)
**Address:** 228 E 80th St, New York, NY 10075
**Phone:** (646) 850-0234
**Website:** http://www.787coffee.com/
**Currently Open:** Yes

**Highlights:**
- Known for unique rum-infused coffee beans
- Pet-friendly establishment
- Fast WiFi and comfortable seating
- Friendly staff who remember regular customers
- Features a swing seat by the window for people-watching
- Freshly baked pastries available all day

**Customer Reviews:**
Customers consistently praise the exceptional coffee quality, particularly the rum-infused varieties. The staff, especially baristas like Alex, Fox, and Isa, are noted for their warmth and hospitality. Many reviewers mention feeling at home in this cozy neighborhood spot.

## 2. INCREPEABLE - Crêpe Cafe
**Rating:** 5.0/5 (215 reviews)
**Address:** 1654 3rd Ave, New York, NY 10128
**Phone:** (970) 852-5808
**Website:** https://increpeable.mom/links
**Currently Open:** No

**Highlights:**
- Specializes in both sweet and savory crêpes
- Offers vegan and gluten-free options
- Homemade jams that customers rave about
- Dairy-free whipped cream available
- Family-owned with personalized service
- Freshly made crêpes to order with customization options

**Customer Reviews:**
Patrons describe the crêpes as among the best they've ever had. The owner, Irina, receives special mention for her friendly demeanor and excellent cooking. Customers appreciate the attention to dietary restrictions and the homemade touches like fresh-baked cookies offered as samples.

## 3. Ella's Cafe Espresso & Wine Bar
**Rating:** 4.7/5 (544 reviews)
**Address:** 1465 2nd Ave, New York, NY 10075
**Phone:** (917) 409-0307
**Website:** https://www.ellascafenyc.com/
**Price Level:** $
**Currently Open:** No

**Highlights:**
- Serves both espresso drinks and wine
- Cozy atmosphere with pink cups and outdoor seating
- Free little library for book exchanges
- Diverse menu including avocado toast and Shakshuka
- Comfortable indoor seating
- Popular for special events like engagement parties

**Customer Reviews:**
Visitors love the charming ambiance and excellent food quality. The café is noted for its attentive service and delicious coffee. Many reviewers mention returning multiple times and recommend trying different menu items, particularly the hummus and pita bread.

---

All three establishments are within walking distance of The Metropolitan Museum of Art, making them perfect spots for a coffee break before or after exploring the museum's collections.
