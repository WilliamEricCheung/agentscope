# Top 3 Cafes Near The Metropolitan Museum of Art

## 1. 787 Coffee
- **Distance from Museum**: 0.9 km (13-minute walk)
- **Rating**: 4.9/5 (3,482 reviews)
- **Address**: 228 E 80th St, New York, NY 10075
- **Phone**: (646) 850-0234
- **Website**: http://www.787coffee.com/
- **Description**: A cozy neighborhood coffee shop known for its rum-infused coffee beans and friendly service. Customers praise the skilled baristas who remember regulars' names and preferences. The café features unique touches like swings by the window and serves excellent lattes, matcha, and specialty drinks. They also offer fresh-baked pastries throughout the day.

## 2. La Bomboniera
- **Distance from Museum**: 1.2 km (17-minute walk)
- **Rating**: 4.7/5 (560 reviews)
- **Address**: 1378 Lexington Ave, New York, NY 10128
- **Phone**: (646) 370-6302
- **Website**: http://labombonieranyc.com/
- **Description**: An eco-conscious café that avoids single-use plastics and disposable products. Known for its delicious hot chocolate with cream, cannoli, and cappuccino. The atmosphere is described as beautiful and intimate, though it can get crowded during peak times. They also serve food and have a bar area, making it suitable for both coffee breaks and light meals.

## 3. Cositas Café
- **Distance from Museum**: 1.4 km (19-minute walk)
- **Rating**: 4.8/5 (184 reviews)
- **Address**: 353 E 78th St, New York, NY 10075
- **Phone**: (347) 673-1733
- **Website**: https://www.cositascafenyc.com/
- **Description**: A charming Latin American café offering authentic Colombian pastries and treats. Famous for its flan, empanadas, almojábanas, and buñuelos. Customers with Colombian heritage particularly appreciate the authenticity of the offerings. The café serves excellent coffee alongside traditional Latin American beverages, including Colombian hot chocolate. It's a small but delightful spot perfect for those seeking Latin flavors.

All three cafés are within a 20-minute walk from The Metropolitan Museum of Art and offer unique experiences ranging from specialty coffee to Latin American treats and eco-friendly dining.
