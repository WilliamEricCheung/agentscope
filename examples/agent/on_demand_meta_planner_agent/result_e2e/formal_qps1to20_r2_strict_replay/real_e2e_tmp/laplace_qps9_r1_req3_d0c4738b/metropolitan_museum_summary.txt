# Metropolitan Museum of Art - Summary

## Location
- Located on the eastern edge of Central Park along Manhattan's Museum Mile in New York City
- Address: 1000 Fifth Avenue, New York, NY

## History
- Founded in 1870 by a group of American citizens including businessmen, financiers, and leading artists
- Opened to the public on February 20, 1872
- Moved to its current location on Fifth Avenue in 1880
- Has undergone numerous expansions over the decades

## Significance
- One of the largest art museums in the world by floor area
- Houses over 2 million works of art spanning 5,000 years of world culture
- Collections include European paintings, American art, ancient Egyptian artifacts, Asian art, Islamic art, and much more
- The Met Cloisters (located in Fort Tryon Park) houses medieval European art and architecture
- Receives millions of visitors annually and is considered one of New York City's most important cultural institutions
