# Cafes Near The Metropolitan Museum of Art

The Metropolitan Museum of Art is located at 1000 5th Ave, New York, NY 10028 (coordinates: 40.7794366, -73.963244).

## Top Recommended Cafes

### 1. 787 Coffee ⭐ 4.9/5 (3,472 reviews)
- **Address**: 228 E 80th St, New York, NY 10128
- **Phone**: (646) 850-0234
- **Website**: http://www.787coffee.com/
- **Currently Open**: No
- **Highlights**: 
  - Highest rated cafe in the area
  - Known for unique rum-infused coffee beans
  - Friendly staff who remember regular customers
  - Cozy atmosphere with a swing by the window
  - Fast WiFi and pet-friendly

### 2. Tarallucci e Vino at Cooper Hewitt ⭐ 4.5/5 (30 reviews)
- **Address**: 9 E 90th St, New York, NY 10128
- **Phone**: (212) 849-8483
- **Website**: https://www.taralluccievino.net/cooper-hewitt-location
- **Price Level**: $$
- **Currently Open**: No
- **Highlights**:
  - Located inside the Cooper Hewitt Design Museum
  - Outdoor seating available in good weather
  - Excellent coffee and wine selection
  - Freshly baked pastries
  - Friendly service (particularly from staff member Fabian)

### 3. Café Fledermaus ⭐ 4.2/5 (40 reviews)
- **Address**: 1048 5th Ave, New York, NY 10028
- **Phone**: (212) 288-0665
- **Website**: http://www.neuegalerie.org/cafes/fledermaus
- **Price Level**: $$$
- **Highlights**:
  - Located in the Neue Galerie museum
  - European-style café in the basement
  - Known for delicious cakes and herring sandwiches
  - Glühwein (hot mulled wine) available
  - Authentic Austrian cuisine
