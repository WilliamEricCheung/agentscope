# Cafes Near The Metropolitan Museum of Art

The Metropolitan Museum of Art is located at 1000 5th Ave, New York, NY 10028 (coordinates: 40.7794366, -73.963244).

## Top Recommended Cafes

### 1. 787 Coffee ⭐ 4.9/5 (3,472 reviews)
**Address:** 228 E 80th St, New York, NY 10075
**Phone:** (646) 850-0234
**Website:** http://www.787coffee.com/
**Distance from MET:** ~0.5 miles southeast

This highly-rated coffee shop is known for its unique rum-infused coffee beans and friendly service. Customers praise the excellent coffee, fast WiFi, and cozy atmosphere. The staff is noted for being welcoming even to customers with large dogs. They offer specialty drinks like rum-infused cappuccinos and whiskey-flavored coffee options.

### 2. Café Blériot XI ⭐ 4.6/5 (310 reviews)
**Address:** 226 E 83rd St, New York, NY 10028
**Phone:** (646) 244-9539
**Price Level:** $
**Distance from MET:** ~0.6 miles east

A charming French-style café with delicious pastries and coffee. Known for their ham and cheese croissants and a variety of espresso drinks. Note that this is not a laptop-friendly café (no work/study allowed), but they offer a buy-one-get-one deal on pastries after 5 PM.

### 3. Antonucci Cafe ⭐ 4.5/5 (428 reviews)
**Address:** 170 E 81st St, New York, NY 10028
**Phone:** (212) 570-5100
**Website:** https://antonuccicafe81.com/
**Price Level:** $$$
**Distance from MET:** ~0.4 miles east

More of an Italian restaurant than just a café, Antonucci offers excellent food including Burrata salad, sea bass, and handmade pasta. The atmosphere is cozy and intimate with artwork on the walls. Note that not all desserts are available during daytime hours.
