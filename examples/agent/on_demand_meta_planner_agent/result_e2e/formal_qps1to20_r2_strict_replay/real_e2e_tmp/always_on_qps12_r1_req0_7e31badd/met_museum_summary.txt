# The Metropolitan Museum of Art: Key Information

## History
- Founded in 1870 by a group of Americans including philanthropists, artists, and businessmen
- First opened on February 20, 1872, at 681 Fifth Avenue
- Moved to its current location in 1880
- Established with the goal of creating a national institution to inspire and educate the public
- The museum's first accessioned object was a Roman sarcophagus, which is still on display today
- Luigi Palma di Cesnola served as the first director from 1879 to 1904

## Location
- Main building: 1000 Fifth Avenue, along Museum Mile on the eastern edge of Central Park in Manhattan's Upper East Side
- The main building is approximately 2 million square feet (190,000 m²), making it one of the world's largest art museums by area
- Second location: The Cloisters at Fort Tryon Park in Upper Manhattan, dedicated to medieval European art, architecture, and artifacts
- Third site: The Neue Galerie New York, set to become part of the Met in 2028, focusing on early 20th century Austrian and German art

## Notable Collections
- Contains approximately 1.5 million works divided into 17 curatorial departments
- Ranges from ancient Near East and ancient Egypt through classical antiquity to contemporary art

### Key Collection Highlights:

**Egyptian Art**
- Over 26,000 pieces spanning from the Paleolithic era through the Ptolemaic era
- Temple of Dendur - a large sandstone temple given to the US by Egypt in 1965
- William the Faience Hippopotamus - the museum's informal mascot

**European Paintings**
- Over 2,600 works from the 13th through early 20th century
- Strong holdings of Old Masters, Impressionist, and Post-Impressionist works
- Notable artists include Rembrandt, Van Gogh, Monet, Cézanne, and Picasso

**American Wing**
- Comprehensive collection of American art from the 18th through early 20th century
- Includes Art of Native America exhibition opened in 2018

**Asian Art**
- Over 35,000 pieces spanning 4,000 years of Asian art
- Comprehensive collections of Chinese, Indian, Cambodian, and Japanese art
- Features the Astor Court, a complete Ming Dynasty-style garden court

**Arts of Africa, Oceania, and the Americas**
- Over 11,000 pieces housed in the Michael C. Rockefeller Wing
- Includes 40,000-year-old indigenous Australian rock paintings and 15-foot-tall Asmat memorial poles

**Islamic Art**
- One of the world's largest collections of Islamic art with approximately 12,000 objects
- Includes secular items, religious works, and miniature paintings from Iran and Mughal India

**Other Notable Collections**
- Arms and Armor: 14,000 objects including pieces belonging to European royalty
- Costume Institute: Over 35,000 costumes and accessories
- Musical Instruments: About 5,000 instruments from around the world, including Stradivari violins
- Robert Lehman Collection: One of the most extraordinary private art collections ever assembled in the US
- Modern and Contemporary Art: Includes iconic works like Picasso's portrait of Gertrude Stein and Jackson Pollock's Autumn Rhythm

## Significance
- Fourth-largest museum in the world by floor area
- Largest art museum in the Americas
- Most-visited museum in the United States with over 5.7 million visitors in fiscal year 2025
- Fifth-most visited art museum in the world
