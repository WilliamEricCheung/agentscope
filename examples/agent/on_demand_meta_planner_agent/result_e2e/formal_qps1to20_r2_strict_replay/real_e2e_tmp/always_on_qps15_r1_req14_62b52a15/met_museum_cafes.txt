# Top 3 Cafes Near The Metropolitan Museum of Art

## 1. Cabuc Coffee ⭐ 4.6/5 (Currently Open)
**Address:** 1211 Lexington Ave, New York, NY 10028  
**Phone:** (646) 891-0011  
**Distance from Met:** Approximately 0.5 miles (8-minute walk)

**Highlights:**
- Currently open and ready to serve
- Specializes in Turkish coffee and unique specialty drinks like Baklava Latte
- Bright, welcoming atmosphere with WiFi available
- Friendly staff and cozy interior
- Offers咸 honey croissant and other pastries

**Customer Reviews:**
Customers consistently praise the excellent coffee quality, particularly the Turkish coffee and specialty lattes. The atmosphere is described as bright and pleasant, making it suitable for both casual coffee breaks and remote work. Most reviewers highlight the friendly service and unique menu offerings.

## 2. Uptown Cafe and Pastries ⭐ 4.5/5 (Currently Open)
**Address:** 100 E 96th St, New York, NY 10128  
**Phone:** (646) 410-0132  
**Distance from Met:** Approximately 0.7 miles (12-minute walk)

**Highlights:**
- Currently open with spacious seating area
- Seasonal cold foam beverages and creative latte flavors
- Modern yet comfortable design with window seating
- Serves drinks in ceramic mugs for in-house consumption
- Fresh pastries including chocolate brownies and Danish pastries

**Customer Reviews:**
Patrons love the seasonal specialty drinks like cookie butter latte, lavender latte, and rose matcha. The cafe is praised for its spacious layout with comfortable booths and window seats. Customers appreciate the use of ceramic mugs instead of paper cups, encouraging a relaxed atmosphere for working or socializing.

## 3. La Bomboniera ⭐ 4.7/5 (Highest Rated, Currently Closed)
**Address:** 1378 Lexington Ave, New York, NY 10128  
**Phone:** (646) 370-6302  
**Website:** http://labombonieranyc.com/  
**Distance from Met:** Approximately 0.4 miles (7-minute walk)

**Highlights:**
- Highest rated cafe in the area (4.7/5 stars)
- Eco-friendly establishment with zero single-use plastics
- Serves drinks in reusable cups for an enhanced experience
- Beautiful ambiance with bar service available
- Known for exceptional hot chocolate with cream and Italian pastries

**Customer Reviews:**
Reviewers consistently mention the outstanding hot chocolate and Italian pastries. The cafe is particularly appreciated for its environmental consciousness, using no single-use plastics or disposable products. While most experiences are positive, some customers have noted occasional service inconsistencies during busy periods.

---

**Recommendation:** For immediate coffee needs, Cabuc Coffee and Uptown Cafe are both excellent choices as they're currently open. If you can wait until La Bomboniera opens, it offers the highest-rated experience with a strong commitment to sustainability.
