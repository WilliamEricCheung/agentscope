# Cafes Near The Metropolitan Museum of Art

The Metropolitan Museum of Art is located at 1000 5th Ave, New York, NY 10028.

## Top Recommended Cafes

### 787 Coffee ⭐ 4.9/5 (3,472 reviews)
- **Address**: 228 E 80th St, New York, NY 10075
- **Phone**: (646) 850-0234
- **Website**: http://www.787coffee.com/
- **Highlights**: 
  - Exceptionally high rating (4.9/5)
  - Known for unique rum-infused coffee beans
  - Pet-friendly establishment
  - Fast WiFi and comfortable seating
  - Features a swing by the window for enjoying street views
  - Staff remembers regular customers' names and preferences

### Antonucci Cafe ⭐ 4.5/5 (428 reviews)
- **Address**: 170 E 81st St, New York, NY 10028
- **Phone**: (212) 570-5100
- **Website**: https://antonuccicafe81.com/
- **Price Level**: $$$
- **Highlights**:
  - Italian cafe and restaurant with warm, intimate atmosphere
  - Excellent Burrata salad with marinated tomatoes
  - Perfectly cooked fettuccini with generous mushroom portions
  - Offers gluten-free pasta options
  - Note: Some desserts are only available in the evening

### Dear Coffee ⭐ 4.6/5 (330 reviews)
- **Address**: 1246 Lexington Ave, New York, NY 10028
- **Website**: https://www.dearcoffeenyc.com/
- **Highlights**:
  - Uses Onyx Coffee Lab beans for espresso and pour-over
  - Unique specialty drinks like Apricot Matcha
  - Minimalist style cafe in the Upper East Side
  - Artistic latte art (customers report dragons on their cappuccinos!)
  - Cozy atmosphere but limited seating
  - Note: No restrooms available
