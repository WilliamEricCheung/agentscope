# The Metropolitan Museum of Art - Overview

## History
The Metropolitan Museum of Art (often referred to as "The Met") was founded on April 13, 1870, by a group of American citizens including businessmen, financiers, leading artists, and thinkers. The museum was established with the mission to bring art and art education to the American people. It opened to the public on February 20, 1872, in a temporary location at 681 Fifth Avenue before moving to its current location on Fifth Avenue along the eastern edge of Central Park in 1880.

Over the decades, The Met has grown from a modest collection to become one of the world's largest and most comprehensive art museums, with over two million works spanning 5,000 years of world culture.

## Location
- **Address**: 1000 Fifth Avenue, New York, NY 10028
- **Location**: Situated along the eastern edge of Central Park in Manhattan's Upper East Side
- **Campus**: The main building spans approximately 2 million square feet, making it one of the largest art museums in the world by area

## Notable Features

### Collections
The Met houses encyclopedic collections that include:
- **European Paintings**: Masterpieces by Rembrandt, Vermeer, Van Gogh, Monet, and many others
- **American Wing**: Extensive collection of American art and decorative arts from the colonial period through the early 20th century
- **Egyptian Art**: One of the finest collections outside of Egypt, including the famous Temple of Dendur
- **Arms and Armor**: Impressive collection of medieval and Renaissance arms and armor
- **Asian Art**: Comprehensive collections spanning Chinese, Japanese, Korean, and South/Southeast Asian art
- **Islamic Art**: Significant holdings of Islamic art from the 7th to the 19th centuries
- **Modern and Contemporary Art**: Works from the early 20th century to the present

### Architecture
- The main building features a distinctive Beaux-Arts facade designed by Richard Morris Hunt
- The Great Hall serves as the main entrance and is one of the most iconic spaces in the museum
- Multiple wings and additions have been added over time, including the modern Robert Lehman Wing and the American Wing

### The Met Cloisters
- A branch of The Met dedicated to medieval European art and architecture
- Located in Fort Tryon Park in northern Manhattan
- Features actual medieval architectural elements reassembled into a harmonious whole

### Visitor Experience
- Annual attendance exceeds 7 million visitors, making it one of the most visited museums in the world
- Offers extensive educational programs, concerts, lectures, and special exhibitions
- The museum's rooftop garden provides stunning views of Central Park and hosts seasonal installations

## Significance
The Metropolitan Museum of Art stands as one of the world's premier cultural institutions, serving as both a repository of human artistic achievement and a vital center for education, scholarship, and public engagement with the arts.
