# The Metropolitan Museum of Art - Overview

## History
The Metropolitan Museum of Art (often referred to as "The Met") was founded in 1870 in New York City. It was established by a group of American citizens including businessmen, financiers, leading artists, and thinkers who wanted to bring art and art education to the American people. The museum first opened to the public on February 20, 1872, at 681 Fifth Avenue. In 1880, it moved to its current location on the eastern edge of Central Park along Manhattan's Museum Mile.

## Significance
The Met is one of the largest and most comprehensive art museums in the world. It is:
- The largest art museum in the Americas
- Among the most visited museums globally
- A cultural landmark of New York City
- An educational institution serving millions of visitors annually
- Home to over 2 million works spanning 5,000 years of world culture

## Main Collections
The Met's collection is organized into 17 curatorial departments:

1. **American Wing** - American decorative arts, paintings, and sculpture from the colonial period through the early 20th century
2. **Ancient Near Eastern Art** - Artifacts from Mesopotamia, Iran, and surrounding regions
3. **Arms and Armor** - European, Japanese, and Islamic arms and armor
4. **Arts of Africa, Oceania, and the Americas** - Traditional art from these regions
5. **Asian Art** - Comprehensive collection including Chinese, Japanese, Korean, South Asian, and Southeast Asian works
6. **Egyptian Art** - One of the finest collections outside Cairo, including the Temple of Dendur
7. **European Paintings** - Masterpieces from the 13th through early 20th centuries, including works by Rembrandt, Vermeer, and Van Gogh
8. **European Sculpture and Decorative Arts** - Renaissance to early 20th-century European decorative arts
9. **Greek and Roman Art** - Classical antiquities
10. **Islamic Art** - Works from Spain to India spanning 7th to 19th centuries
11. **Modern and Contemporary Art** - Works from 1890 to present
12. **Musical Instruments** - Historical instruments from around the world
13. **Photographs** - Images from the invention of photography to present
14. **Prints and Drawings** - Works on paper from the 15th century onward
15. **Robert Lehman Collection** - Private collection integrated into the museum
16. **The Costume Institute** - Fashion and costume design (hosts the annual Met Gala)
17. **Medieval Art and The Cloisters** - Medieval European art, with The Cloisters being a separate branch dedicated to medieval art

## Additional Information
- Location: 1000 Fifth Avenue, New York, NY 10028
- Annual visitors: Approximately 7 million (pre-pandemic)
- The Met has two locations: The main building on Fifth Avenue and The Met Cloisters in Fort Tryon Park
- The museum offers extensive educational programs, conservation efforts, and scholarly research
